## EduAll Foundation Launches 2026 Grant Initiative: CHF 500,000 Available for International Students Facing Financial Barriers

*Swiss Non-Profit Commits to Equal Educational Opportunity with New Call for International Student Applicants*

**Geneva, Switzerland – 15 June 2026** – EduAll Foundation, a Geneva-based non-profit foundation that supports international students, announced today the launch of its 2026 Grant Call, offering CHF 500,000 in financial support to international students pursuing higher education at accredited Swiss institutions.

The competitive grant program, now entering its second decade of operation, will support an estimated 140-180 students across bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs. Since its founding in 2015, EduAll Foundation has supported more than 1,700 international students, 98% of whom have graduated or are on track to graduate.

### Key Facts About the 2026 Grant Call

**Funding Available:**
- Total Budget: CHF 500,000
- Maximum Individual Awards:
  - Bachelor's: CHF 8,000/year (up to 4 years)
  - Master's: CHF 6,500/year (up to 2 years)
  - Doctoral: CHF 5,000/year (up to 3 years, exceptional cases)

**Application Timeline:**
- Portal Opens: June 20, 2026
- Deadline: September 1, 2026, 23:59 CET
- Decisions: by June 30, 2027
- Disbursement and start of funding: September 1, 2027

**Eligibility:**
- Non-Swiss citizens legally residing in Switzerland
- Enrolled or accepted to accredited Swiss higher education institutions
- Demonstrated genuine financial need
- Commitment to academic studies
- Full-time or part-time study

### About EduAll Foundation

"Our mission is simple but powerful: ensure that financial constraints do not prevent talented, motivated students from pursuing higher education," said Dr. Lisa Benetti, Executive Director of EduAll Foundation.

"Since 2015, we've supported more than 1,700 students from more than 70 countries. This year, with CHF 500,000 to distribute, we'll continue our commitment to equal opportunity and social mobility through education."

### Unique Approach to Grant Selection

Unlike many scholarship programs focused on academic excellence or competitive achievement, EduAll Foundation prioritizes:

- **Financial Need:** Genuine difficulty in funding education and living costs
- **Educational Commitment:** Clear goals and motivation for studies
- **Resilience:** Ability to overcome obstacles and challenges
- **Diversity:** Support for underrepresented geographic regions
- **Equal Opportunity:** Commitment to leveling the educational playing field

"We're not looking for the 'best' students in terms of grades," explains Dr. Heinrich Müller, President of EduAll Foundation. "We're looking for students with genuine financial need who have the determination to succeed. A 3.0 GPA student struggling financially is just as deserving as a 4.0 student with family wealth."

### Eligibility & Support

The grant program supports:
- Bachelor's degrees (typically 3-4 years)
- Master's degrees (typically 1-2 years)
- Doctoral studies (typically 3-4 years)

Funds cover:
- University tuition and fees
- Living expenses (rent, food, utilities, transportation)
- Essential educational materials
- Health insurance and necessary expenses

Notably, grants are **not loans** and do not require repayment.

### Application Process

All applications are submitted through EduAll's online application portal.

Required materials include:
- Completed application form
- Financial documentation (bank statements, income verification)
- Academic records and transcripts
- Written statements: financial need (300–400 words), personal statement (max. 500 words) and declared circumstances (max. 300 words)
- Two references (academic and personal)

"We've designed the application process to be accessible to students worldwide," notes Dr. Lisa Benetti. "Applications are accepted in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, and Portuguese. We don't require expensive certification or notarization for most documents."

### Geographic & Demographic Focus

EduAll Foundation intentionally prioritizes students from:
- Low-income countries (GNI per capita < USD 3,000)
- Underrepresented geographic regions
- First-generation university families
- Disadvantaged socioeconomic backgrounds

In 2025, EduAll recipients came from 45 countries, with particular emphasis on:
- Sub-Saharan Africa (35%)
- South Asia (20%)
- Southeast Asia (15%)
- Middle East/North Africa (15%)
- Eastern Europe (10%)
