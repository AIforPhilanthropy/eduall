# EduAll Foundation - Organization Profile

## Official Information

**Organization Name:** EduAll Foundation  
**Legal Status:** Swiss Non-Profit Foundation (Fondation de droit privé)  
**Seat:** Geneva, Switzerland  
**Founding Year:** 2015

---

## Mission Statement

EduAll Foundation is committed to fostering educational opportunity and social mobility by providing financial support to talented and motivated international students pursuing higher education in Switzerland. We believe that financial constraints should not be a barrier to quality education. Our mission is to ensure that deserving students from all socioeconomic backgrounds have equal access to bachelor, master, and doctoral studies at Swiss institutions.

---

## Core Values

### 1. **Equal Opportunity**
We are dedicated to promoting equal opportunity in higher education. Our focus is not on academic excellence or competition, but rather on identifying students with genuine financial need and providing them with the means to pursue their studies.

### 2. **Financial Solidarity**
We believe in the principle of financial solidarity—that those with resources have a responsibility to support those without. We invest in the future by supporting students who may otherwise be unable to afford their education.

### 3. **Social Inclusion**
We actively work to reduce barriers to education based on financial circumstances, residency status, or socioeconomic background. Our grants are designed to be inclusive and accessible.

### 4. **Dignity and Respect**
We treat all applicants with respect and dignity. Our processes are transparent, fair, and non-discriminatory.

### 5. **Sustainable Impact**
We invest in individuals who have the potential to make positive contributions to society. By supporting their education today, we invest in a more equitable future.

---

## Organizational History

EduAll Foundation was established in 2015 by a consortium of Swiss business leaders, academic institutions, and philanthropists who recognized the growing challenge faced by international students seeking to study in Switzerland. The founders observed that many talented and motivated students were unable to pursue higher education due to financial constraints—not because of lack of ability, but because of circumstance.

Over the past decade, EduAll has become an established private funder for international students in Switzerland. We have supported more than 1,700 students from more than 70 countries across Swiss universities, helping them complete degrees ranging from bachelor's to doctoral programs.

## Eligibility Requirements (General)

To be considered for EduAll grants, students must meet the following criteria:

1. **Residency Status:** Non-Swiss citizen, legally residing in Switzerland
2. **Academic Status:** Enrolled in an accredited Swiss higher education institution
3. **Financial Need:** Demonstrate genuine financial need for education and living costs
4. **Study Level:** Pursuing bachelor's, master's, or doctoral studies, full-time or part-time
5. **Academic Progress:** Satisfactory academic standing (normally a GPA of at least 2.5/4.0 or equivalent; exceptions are considered when the applicant explains the circumstances)
6. **Legal Compliance:** No outstanding legal or immigration issues

## Types of Support Offered

### Direct Financial Grants
Funds provided directly to students for:
- University tuition fees
- Registration and administrative fees
- Living expenses (rent, food, utilities)
- Books and educational materials
- Healthcare and insurance costs

### Indirect Support Services
- Mentorship and academic guidance
- Career counseling
- Networking events with other grant recipients and alumni
- Emergency financial assistance (in exceptional cases)
