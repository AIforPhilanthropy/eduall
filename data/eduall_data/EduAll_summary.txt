EDUALL FOUNDATION - 500 APPLICATIONS SYNTHETIC DATASET
GENERATION COMPLETE
================================================================================

TOTAL RECORDS: 500 student applications

TASK: Evaluate each applicant against the EduAll Grant Call 2026 criteria and 
decide which students get granted and why. Decisions are due by 30 June 2027;
funding starts 1 September 2027.

MAIN FILE: EduAll_500_Applications_Dataset.csv

================================================================================
DATASET CONTENTS
================================================================================

PRIMARY FILE:
  └─ EduAll_500_Applications_Dataset.csv (89 KB)
     • 500 records + 1 header row (501 total lines)
     • 44 columns with quantitative and discrete variables
     • Fully anonymized synthetic data
     • CSV format for easy loading into Python, R, Excel, SQL

INDIVIDUAL APPLICATION DOCUMENTS:
  └─ individual_applications/ folder (500 files)
     • One markdown file per student (STU000001_application.md through STU000500_application.md)
     • Each file is the completed application form, consistent with the CSV row:
       - Applicant information and study programme
       - Financial information (income, itemised monthly expenses, savings, need)
       - Financial Statement (written by the applicant, 300-400 words)
       - Personal Statement (written by the applicant, max. 500 words)
       - CV (education, work, leadership/volunteering, languages)
       - Circumstances checklist (8 risk and 5 resilience/strength factors)
       - Statement of Declared Circumstances (max. 300 words)
     • Statements vary in writing style and in how openly applicants
       discuss weaknesses (some only present their strengths)

================================================================================
KEY DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

• gni_tier: income group of the country of origin
    1 = GNI per capita below USD 3,000 (the Grant Call's priority
        "low-income countries"): Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Ghana, India,
        Kenya, Nigeria, Pakistan, Palestine, Senegal, Syria
    2 = middle-income: Egypt, Indonesia, Iraq, Morocco, Peru,
        Philippines, Vietnam
    3 = higher-income European countries: Bulgaria, Romania, Ukraine
• All amounts are in CHF per month, except savings_chf (total amount),
  external_scholarship_amount_chf and tuition_fees_per_year_chf
  (CHF per year).
• housing_cost_chf = monthly rent including utilities; it is part of
  monthly_expenses_chf.
• tuition_fees_per_year_chf = tuition and semester fees charged by the
  institution per academic year (included in monthly_expenses_chf as a
  monthly share).
• dependent_children = number of children the applicant supports
  (0 unless risk_parent_student = 1). EduAll grants do not cover
  dependants' costs; a separate Student Parent Support Program exists.
• financial_need_chf = the applicant's CURRENT MONTHLY shortfall at the
  time of application, in CHF per month:
      monthly_expenses_chf - family_income_chf - personal_income_chf
  (minimum 0). It is not a yearly amount and not the total needed until
  graduation. It does NOT deduct other scholarships
  (external_scholarship_amount_chf, per year) or savings (savings_chf,
  a stock); these are listed separately. How much support an applicant
  needs overall depends on how long they still need to study (within
  the grant period) and on these other resources.
• study_mode = current study mode (Full-time or Part-time).
  planned_study_mode_2027 = study mode the applicant plans for the grant
  year 2027/28. Some part-time students plan to change to full-time study
  in September 2027 in order to finish sooner. Part-time students progress
  more slowly, so their degrees take longer to complete.
• expected_personal_income_2027_chf = the applicant's expected monthly
  personal income in 2027/28 (CHF per month), as declared in the form.
  For most applicants it equals personal_income_chf; it is lower for
  applicants who plan to reduce their working hours or to change to
  full-time study. The financial_need_chf column is based on current
  income, not on this expected income.
• academic_standing "At Risk" = GPA below 2.5/4.0, below the Grant
  Call's normal minimum; such applicants may be considered if they
  explain their circumstances.
• semesters_in_program = semesters completed by the application date
  (applications were submitted between 20 June and 1 September 2026;
  the next semester starts in autumn 2026).
• courses_completed = courses passed so far in the current degree
  programme. The number of courses each degree requires is set out in
  the Grant Call ("Grant Period and Award Amount"). Awards cover the
  2027/28 academic year (funding starts 1 September 2027) and are
  pro-rated for students who finish during that year.

================================================================================
NOTES
================================================================================

• This is SYNTHETIC DATA created for research and educational purposes
• No real personal information is contained in this dataset
• All demographic information is randomly generated
• Financial figures are simulated and realistic but not actual
• Risk factors are assigned probabilistically based on country profiles
• family_income_chf = monthly support sent by family members;
  family_household_income_chf = combined monthly income of those family members
  (net, CHF equivalent). The supporting family may live in Switzerland (e.g. a
  partner or parents working here) or abroad; the statements say who they are
  and where they live.
• residency_permit "Pending" = permit application submitted, decision pending
  (applicants flagged with risk_undocumented_status)
