## 1. Before You Apply

### Check Your Eligibility

Before investing time in your application, please verify that you meet the basic eligibility requirements:

- [ ] I am not a Swiss citizen
- [ ] I have a valid residency permit (B, C, F, S, L, G, or equivalent), or my permit application is pending (see the Grant Call)
- [ ] I am accepted into or currently enrolled at an accredited Swiss institution
- [ ] I am pursuing a bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree (full-time or part-time)
- [ ] I can demonstrate genuine financial need
- [ ] I maintain or will maintain satisfactory academic standing
- [ ] I have no outstanding legal or immigration issues

If you checked all boxes, you are eligible to proceed with your application!

### Gather Your Documents

Before starting, assemble the following:

**Financial Documents:**
- Last 3 months of bank statements
- Income documentation for parents/sponsors (tax returns, pay stubs, employment letters)
- Documentation of expenses (rent agreement, utility bills)
- Proof of any other income or assets

**Academic Documents:**
- University acceptance letter (if not yet enrolled) OR current enrollment confirmation
- Transcript of academic records
- Copy of residency permit/visa
- Valid passport
- Any documentation of special circumstances affecting your studies

**Personal Documents:**
- Your CV/Resume
- Names and contact details of your two references
- Any additional documents you wish to submit supporting your application

### Plan Your Timeline

- **Week 1:** Gather all documents and prepare your financial information
- **Week 2-3:** Write your three statements (financial need, personal, declared circumstances) and contact your references
- **Week 4-8:** Complete and review your online application
- **By 1 September:** Submit your application

We recommend submitting early rather than waiting until the deadline.

## 2. How to Create Your Account

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Visit the Application Portal**
   - Open EduAll's online application portal
   - Click "Create New Account"

2. **Provide Basic Information**
   - Full name (as appears in passport)
   - Email address (check this regularly)
   - Create a secure password (min. 8 characters, numbers, uppercase, lowercase)
   - Confirm password

3. **Verify Your Email**
   - Check your email inbox for verification link
   - Click the link to activate your account
   - If no email arrives within 5 minutes, check spam folder or request resend

4. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Date of birth
   - Nationality
   - Country of residence (if different from Switzerland)
   - Preferred language for communication (English/French/German/Italian)
   - Phone number (for contact purposes)

5. **Start Your Application**
   - Click "Begin Application" to proceed to the application form
   - You can save your progress and return later
   - A completion percentage indicator shows your progress

## 3. Completing Your Application

### Section 1: Personal Information

**Fields Required:**
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Place of birth
- Nationality/Citizenship
- Mother tongue
- Languages spoken (with proficiency levels)

**Tips:**
- Use your full legal name as it appears in your passport
- If you go by a different name, note it as "Also known as"
- List languages you can communicate in at more than basic level

### Section 2: Contact & Residency Information

**Fields Required:**
- Current address in Switzerland
- Length of time at current address
- Residency permit type and expiry date
- Previous address(es) if in Switzerland less than 2 years
- Preferred contact method (email/phone)

**Documents to Attach:**
- Copy of residency permit (both pages)
- Copy of valid passport
- (Optional) Proof of address if recently relocated

### Section 3: Current/Intended University & Program

**Fields Required:**
- University/Institution name
- Campus location
- Degree level (Bachelor/Master/Doctoral)
- Field of study/Program name
- Program start date
- Expected completion date
- Current semester/year of study (if already enrolled)
- Current study mode (full-time or part-time)
- Planned study mode for 2027/28 (if you plan to change, explain why in your Financial Need Statement)

**Documents to Attach:**
- **If not yet enrolled:** Official acceptance letter from institution
- **If already enrolled:** Current enrollment confirmation from university registrar's office
- Academic transcript (most recent semester/year)

**Tips:**
- Use the official name of your institution as it appears on official documents
- Be precise about your field of study
- If you haven't received your acceptance letter yet, indicate "awaiting acceptance" and submit it as soon as available

### Section 4: Academic History & Performance

**Fields Required:**
- Previous degrees obtained (high school, bachelor's, etc.)
- Academic qualifications
- Grade point average (GPA) or equivalent
- Current academic status

**Documents to Attach:**
- Transcript of previous degrees
- Proof of high school completion/equivalency
- Any language proficiency certificates (TOEFL, IELTS, etc.)

**Tips:**
- Convert your GPA to a 4.0 scale if using a different system
- Include any honors, distinctions, or awards
- If your academic record shows challenges, explain them in your personal statement

### Section 5: Financial Information

This is a critical section. Provide thorough, honest information.

**Personal Financial Information:**
- Do you have personal income? (employment, part-time work, scholarships)
- Amount of monthly income: _______ CHF
- Expected monthly personal income in 2027/28: _______ CHF (for example, if you will reduce your working hours or change to full-time study)
- Do you have savings/assets?
- Approximate amount: _______ CHF

**Family Financial Support:**
- Monthly amount your family sends you: _______ CHF
- Combined monthly income of the family members who support you (parents, spouse/partner or other relatives): _______ CHF (equivalent; state the exchange rate used)
- Are parents able to support your education financially?
  - [ ] Yes, fully
  - [ ] Yes, partially
  - [ ] No, not at all
  - [ ] Not applicable/No contact
- Siblings or dependents supported by parents: _______
- Number of children you support yourself: _______
- Other family members dependent on parents' income?

**Household Expenses in Switzerland:**
- Monthly rent: _______ CHF
- Utilities (electricity, heating, internet): _______ CHF
- Food/groceries: _______ CHF
- Transportation: _______ CHF
- University fees/tuition: _______ CHF/year
- Health insurance: _______ CHF/month
- Other essential expenses: _______ CHF

**Other Funding Sources:**
- Do you receive any scholarships or grants? [ ] Yes [ ] No
  - If yes, please specify: _________________ (CHF/year)
- Do you work or plan to work? [ ] Yes [ ] No
  - If yes, approximate income: _______ CHF/month
  - Hours per week: _______
- Do you have loans? [ ] Yes [ ] No
  - If yes, monthly payment: _______ CHF

**Documents to Attach:**
- Bank statements (3 months)
- Rent agreement or proof of housing cost
- Utility bills (1-2 recent months)
- Income documentation (employment letter, contract, pay stubs, tax returns)
- Proof of any other funding (scholarship letter, loan agreement)
- Parents' income documentation if applicable
- Any other financial documentation supporting your application

**Tips:**
- Be honest and comprehensive—incomplete financial information weakens your application
- If your family cannot provide financial information, explain why
- Convert all amounts to CHF for consistency
- If you have significant debts or financial obligations, disclose them
- The form calculates your **monthly financial need** automatically: monthly expenses minus family support and personal income. Other scholarships and savings are shown separately; the committee takes them into account when setting the award amount.
- Awards from the 2026 call cover the 2027/28 academic year (from 1 September 2027). Only study time from September 2027 onwards can be funded, and awards are pro-rated if you finish during that year (see the Grant Call).
- If you study part-time, or plan to change your study mode or working hours in September 2027, enter your expected personal income for 2027/28 and explain the change in your Financial Need Statement. The committee assesses your need for the grant period, not only your current month.

### Section 6: Financial Need Statement

**Instructions (as shown in the form):** Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).

**Key Points to Address:**
- Why is paying for your education in Switzerland difficult?
- What specific costs are most challenging for you?
- What would happen to your studies without financial support?
- Have you exhausted other funding options?
- How will EduAll support help you achieve your educational goals?

### Section 7: Personal Statement

**Instructions (as shown in the form):** Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).

See "Writing Your Personal Statement" below for a recommended structure.

### Section 8: Declared Circumstances

**Checklist:** Tick every circumstance that applies to you.
- Risk factors: first-generation student; refugee/asylum status; disability; housing insecurity; family emergency; language barrier; student parent/dependents; undocumented or pending residence status
- Resilience/strength factors: volunteer experience; work experience; leadership role; community engagement; overcame significant adversity

**Statement of Declared Circumstances — Instructions (as shown in the form):** For each circumstance you have ticked in the checklist (risk factors and resilience/strength factors), briefly describe your situation and how it affects or has shaped your studies. You may attach supporting documents. If you have not ticked any circumstance, you may leave this section blank (max. 300 words).

**Tips:**
- Be factual and concise; you do not need to share details you are not comfortable with
- If your residence permit application is pending, explain its status here and attach proof of the pending application

## 4. Document Preparation Guide

### Financial Documentation

**Bank Statements:**
- Print or download the last 3 months
- Ensure your full name appears
- Redact sensitive information (account numbers) but keep transaction descriptions visible
- Save as PDF

**Income Documentation:**
- Employment letter from employer (on company letterhead)
- OR recent pay stubs (3 months)
- OR tax returns (if self-employed)
- Should clearly show gross income and employment dates

**Expense Documentation:**
- Rental agreement showing monthly rent
- Utility bill showing estimated annual costs
- University tuition invoice or fee schedule

### Academic Documentation

**Acceptance Letter:**
- Original or official copy
- Should include: program name, start date, degree level, institution contact

**Enrollment Confirmation:**
- Obtained from university registrar's office
- Should verify: student status, program, enrollment period
- Many universities provide this online; print with authentication watermark if possible

**Academic Transcript:**
- Official or certified copy
- Should show: courses taken, grades received, GPA or equivalent
- Most recent semester/year included

**Residency/Visa Documents:**
- Photocopy of valid residency permit (both sides)
- Photocopy of valid passport (information page)

### File Preparation Standards

**Format:**
- Use PDF format when possible
- JPG/PNG acceptable for photographs
- Max file size: 5 MB per document
- Clear, readable scans (not blurry photos)

**Naming Convention:**
- Use clear descriptive names: "Bank_Statement_June2026.pdf"
- Avoid special characters or spaces if possible
- Don't use "Document1," "Scan," or similar generic names

**Language:**
- Documents in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, or Portuguese acceptable
- Official documents in other languages should be accompanied by certified translation

**Redaction:**
- You may redact sensitive information (ID numbers, account details)
- Keep essential information visible (dates, amounts, verification)

## 5. Personal Statement & References

### Writing Your Personal Statement (500 words maximum)

This is your opportunity to tell your story in your own words.

**Structure Recommendation:**

Your financial situation belongs in the separate Financial Need Statement; the personal statement is about you.

**Who you are (about 100-150 words):**
- Where you come from and what shaped you
- What you are studying or plan to study

**Your goals (about 150 words):**
- What are your educational goals?
- How will this degree help you achieve them?
- What will you do with your education after graduation?

**Challenges (optional, about 100 words):**
- Personal challenges or circumstances you wish to share, and how you dealt with them

**What education means to you (about 100 words):**
- Why this education matters to you and to the people around you

**Writing Tips:**

- **Be authentic:** Write in your genuine voice, not a formal tone
- **Be specific:** Use concrete examples rather than generalizations
- **Be honest:** Describe difficulties plainly; you do not need to dramatise them
- **Be concise:** The word limits are firm
- **Avoid clichés:** "I come from a poor country" is less effective than specific details
- **Check grammar:** Use spell-check and read aloud for flow
- **Ask for feedback:** Have a friend or teacher review before submitting

**Example Opening (for reference):**
*"Growing up in [city], I spent my evenings in my grandmother's small pharmacy, reading the labels on medicine boxes. No one in my family had been to university. Now, in the second year of my master's in Pharmaceutical Sciences at [University], I want to help bring affordable medicines to towns like the one where I grew up."*

### Securing References

**What We Need:**
- Two references: one academic and one personal (both required)
- Names, email addresses, institutional affiliation
- References submit their letters directly through our portal

**Choosing Your References:**

**Academic Reference:**
- Professor or instructor from your current/previous studies
- Someone who knows your academic work and potential
- Can speak to your abilities and commitment to learning
- Examples: Course instructor, thesis advisor, department head, academic mentor

**Personal Reference:**
- Someone who knows you well but is NOT family
- Can speak to your character, resilience, and values
- Examples: Mentor, counselor, community leader, volunteer coordinator, employer

**How to Request a Reference:**

1. **Ask in advance:** Don't surprise your references
2. **Provide context:** Explain the grant, your application, deadline
3. **Share details:** Give them the application link and reference submission instructions
4. **Provide guidance:** Let them know what the committee is looking for
5. **Thank them:** Express genuine gratitude for their support

**Sample Email:**

```
Dear [Professor/Mentor],

I am applying for the EduAll Foundation Grant (Application Deadline: 1 Sept 2026).
The foundation supports international students in Switzerland who are in
financial need.

I would be honored if you would serve as a reference for my application.
Your perspective on my academic abilities/character would be invaluable.

The application portal is at: [link]
You can submit your reference letter directly through the portal.
The deadline is September 1, 2026.

Please let me know if you need any additional information.

Thank you for considering my request.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**What References Should Include:**

- Your relationship to the applicant
- How long they've known you
- Specific examples of your abilities/character
- Assessment of your potential and commitment
- Why they believe you deserve support
- Confidential comments (if applicable)

## 6. Technical Support

### Common Technical Issues

**I can't create an account:**
- Clear your browser cache (Settings > Clear browsing data)
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
- Ensure email address is unique (not used for another account)
- Contact support if issue persists

**I forgot my password:**
- Click "Forgot Password?" on login page
- Enter your email address
- Check email for reset link
- Reset password and log in with new password

**My documents won't upload:**
- Check file size (max 5 MB per file)
- Ensure file format is supported (PDF, JPG, PNG)
- Try renaming file with simpler name
- Try different browser
- Check internet connection stability

**I can't see my saved application:**
- Log out completely and log back in
- Wait a few minutes and try refreshing
- Use same browser/computer where you started application
- Contact support if still not visible

### Getting Technical Help

**Help Form:** In the application portal (Help > Contact support)  
**Response Time:** Within 24 business hours  
**Online Chat:** Available Mon-Fri, 10:00-16:00 CET (on application portal)  

**When Contacting Support, Please Provide:**
- Your application reference number (if available)
- Description of the problem
- What you've already tried
- Screenshots if helpful
- Your browser and device type

### System Information

- **Recommended Browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari (latest versions)
- **Internet Connection:** Broadband recommended
- **Device:** Desktop or laptop recommended (mobile upload may have issues)
- **Screen Resolution:** 1024x768 minimum
- **JavaScript:** Must be enabled

### What Happens After You Submit

**September - November 2026:**
- Your application is reviewed for completeness
- Eligibility is confirmed

**December 2026 - February 2027:**
- Applications are assessed and ranked using our evaluation criteria

**March - April 2027:**
- Top candidates (approximately 50%) invited for interviews
- Interviews conducted via Zoom (approximately 30-45 minutes)
- Interview confirmation sent 1-2 weeks in advance

**June 2027:**
- Final selection committee meets
- All applicants notified of decisions by 30 June 2027 (by email)
- Acceptance deadline for recipients: 31 July 2027

**1 September 2027:**
- Grant disbursed to recipients' accounts; the grant period begins
- Welcome package and orientation materials sent

If your circumstances change between submission and the decision (for example new funding, a job, or a change in your study plan), inform us before June 2027.

### If You're Selected

**Congratulations!**

Upon selection, you'll be asked to:
1. Confirm acceptance of the grant award (online form)
2. Provide banking information for disbursement
3. Sign the grant agreement
4. Attend an optional orientation session
5. Complete an annual progress report

**Ongoing Responsibilities:**
- Maintain enrollment status
- Maintain satisfactory academic progress
- Notify EduAll of significant changes (address, program, etc.)
- Complete annual renewal application if seeking second year of funding

### If You're Not Selected

- You'll receive an email decision notice
- You may request feedback on your application (optional, by email)
- You may appeal the decision within 30 days (formal appeal process)
- You're welcome to reapply next year if circumstances have changed
