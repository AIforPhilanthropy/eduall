## 1. General Grant Terms

### 1.1 Nature of Grant

A grant awarded by EduAll Foundation (hereinafter "Grantor") is a **non-refundable financial award** provided to support eligible international students (hereinafter "Recipient" or "Grant Recipient") in their pursuit of higher education at accredited Swiss institutions.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Non-Repayable:** Grants are not loans and do not require repayment
- **Non-Employment:** Receiving a grant creates no employment obligation
- **Non-Transferable:** Grants are awarded to named individuals and cannot be transferred
- **Conditional:** Grants are subject to ongoing conditions and requirements

### 1.2 Grant Award

Upon selection, the Grantor will:
- Notify Recipient of grant award amount in CHF
- Provide written grant agreement specifying terms
- Establish disbursement schedule
- Assign a grant coordinator for support and monitoring

The Recipient acknowledges receipt of this notification and the grant agreement.

### 1.3 Award Amount & Duration

**Bachelor's Studies:**
- Maximum annual award: CHF 8,000
- Maximum duration: 4 years
- Maximum total support: CHF 32,000

**Master's Studies:**
- Maximum annual award: CHF 6,500
- Maximum duration: 2 years
- Maximum total support: CHF 13,000

**Doctoral Studies:**
- Maximum annual award: CHF 5,000
- Maximum duration: 1 year (renewable up to 3 years under exceptional circumstances)
- Maximum total support: CHF 15,000

**Actual Award Amount:** The specific amount awarded to each Recipient is determined by the selection committee based on:
- Demonstrated financial need
- Available funding
- Number of approved recipients
- Individual circumstances and requirements

**Grant Period:** Each award covers one academic year (for the 2026 call: September 2027 - August 2028). If the Recipient completes the programme before the end of the grant period, the award is pro-rated to the months of study remaining.

The award normally does not exceed the Recipient's documented shortfall for the grant period, taking into account other scholarships and available savings. Support is limited to the time the Recipient still needs to complete the degree, assessed against EduAll's standard programme requirements (bachelor's degree: 40 courses; master's degree: 12 courses; doctorate: 12 courses; thesis or dissertation included). The Grantor reserves the right to adjust award amounts if total requests exceed available budget.

## 2. Eligible Use of Funds

### 2.1 Approved Expense Categories

Grant funds may be used solely for the following purposes:

**A. Direct Education Costs:**
- University tuition fees and charges
- Registration and administrative fees
- Examination and assessment fees
- Mandatory study materials, textbooks, and learning resources
- Laboratory equipment, software, or materials required for program
- Art supplies, musical instruments, or specialized tools (if required by program)
- Student union or association fees (if mandatory)

**B. Essential Living Expenses:**
- Accommodation costs (rent, utilities, internet)
- Food and groceries
- Local public transportation passes
- Personal hygiene and health items
- Clothing and footwear for study
- Health insurance premiums not covered by other sources
- Mandatory disability insurance or other required insurance

**C. Exceptional/Emergency Expenses:**
- Medical treatments or medications not covered by insurance
- Disability-related accommodations or equipment
- Emergency travel for family emergencies (limited)
- Other essential expenses (requires prior approval from Grantor)

### 2.2 Prohibited Uses

Grant funds **CANNOT** be used for:
- Travel to or from applicant's home country (except in rare emergency circumstances, with prior written approval)
- Childcare or dependent care expenses
- Tuition in non-accredited institutions
- Professional certifications, training, or courses outside degree program
- Internships, co-op placements, or voluntary service programs
- Recreational activities, entertainment, or leisure
- Luxury items, expensive hobbies, or non-essential purchases
- Repayment of personal loans or debts
- Family members' or dependents' expenses
- Subscription services not required for studies
- Fines, penalties, or legal fees
- Parking, vehicle registration, or vehicle maintenance (except public transportation)

### 2.3 Restricted Uses

The following uses require **prior written approval** from EduAll Foundation:
- Research expenses or conference attendance
- Study abroad programs or exchange semesters
- Language courses beyond those in main degree program
- Therapeutic or counseling services
- Employer-paid courses or programs
- Any expense exceeding CHF 5,000

## 3. Disbursement & Financial Management

### 3.1 Payment Method

Grants are disbursed directly to the Recipient via:
- **Bank Transfer:** To Swiss bank account in Recipient's name (preferred)
- **Credit towards University:** Direct payment to institution (in some cases)

The Recipient must provide banking information on the Grant Agreement form.

### 3.2 Disbursement Schedule

**Timing:**
- Awards from the 2026 call: Disbursed on 1 September 2027 (after grant acceptance)
- Subsequent year awards: Disbursed in September each year (upon renewal)

**Conditions:**
- Disbursement occurs after Recipient signs grant agreement
- All required documentation has been received
- Continued enrollment is verified
- No disqualifying issues have emerged

The Grantor reserves the right to withhold or delay disbursement if:
- Grant agreement has not been signed
- Recipient's status has changed
- Investigation into grant misuse is pending
- Recipient is unresponsive to Grantor communications

### 3.3 Partial Disbursement

If Recipient has received other scholarships or grants not previously disclosed, the Grantor may:
- Reduce the grant award to avoid duplication of support
- Request repayment of amounts exceeding total approved aid package
- Adjust future disbursement schedules

### 3.4 Financial Accountability

The Recipient agrees to:
- Use grant funds only for approved purposes
- Maintain records of expenses for 2 years
- Disclose any additional funding received
- Cooperate with any audits or reviews
- Report any suspected misuse of funds

The Grantor may request:
- Receipts or documentation of expenses
- Bank statements showing fund utilization
- Certification of expenses
- Reimbursement if funds were misused

## 4. Conditions of Grant

### 4.1 Academic Requirements

The Recipient must:
- Maintain enrollment in an accredited Swiss institution
- Maintain satisfactory academic progress (normally a GPA of at least 2.5/4.0 or equivalent)
- Complete courses and work toward degree completion
- If on academic probation, agree an improvement plan with the grant coordinator
- Not be at risk of expulsion
- Provide academic transcripts upon request

**Failure to Meet Academic Requirements:**
- First warning: Written notice requiring improvement plan
- Continued non-performance: Grant suspension or termination

### 4.2 Legal & Residency Requirements

The Recipient must:
- Maintain valid residency status in Switzerland
- Comply with all Swiss laws and regulations
- Maintain good standing with university and relevant authorities
- Immediately report any changes to immigration status
- Not have outstanding legal charges or serious violations

**Changes in Status:**
- Recipient must notify Grantor within 10 days of any legal/immigration changes
- Non-disclosure may result in grant termination and repayment demand

### 4.3 Program & Enrollment Status

The Recipient must:
- Remain enrolled in the originally approved program
- Maintain the study mode (full-time or part-time) declared in the application, or notify Grantor of any change
- Not exceed approved program duration
- Notify Grantor before changing programs, institutions, or study level
- Continue pursuing degree toward completion

**Program Changes:**
- Changes require prior written approval from Grantor
- Grants may not be transferred to significantly different programs
- Grant may be terminated if program change is not approved

### 4.4 Financial Transparency

The Recipient must:
- Disclose all additional funding sources (scholarships, grants, loans)
- Report significant changes in financial circumstances
- Maintain honest, accurate financial information
- Not fraudulently misrepresent financial need
- Repay any funds obtained through misrepresentation

### 4.5 Annual Reporting & Renewal

The Recipient must:
- Complete annual progress report by specified deadline
- Provide updated financial and academic documentation
- Participate in optional alumni networking events (if time permits)
- Allow Grantor to contact institution to verify enrollment

**Non-Compliance:**
- Failure to complete annual report may result in grant suspension
- Missing reporting deadline may delay future disbursement

## 5. Grant Renewal

### 5.1 Renewal Process

Grants are awarded on an annual basis. Continuation to subsequent years is **not automatic**.

**To Renew Your Grant:**
1. Submit annual renewal application (separate form)
2. Provide updated financial documentation
3. Provide academic transcript
4. Complete progress report
5. Verify continued enrollment and academic standing

**Renewal Eligibility:**
- Recipient must meet all conditions of grant
- Recipient must demonstrate continued financial need
- Recipient must maintain satisfactory academic performance
- Recipient must remain enrolled in approved program
- No grant violations or policy breaches

### 5.2 Renewal Deadline

- Renewal applications due: 31 May each year
- Decisions announced: 31 August
- Funds disbursed: September, at the start of the renewed grant year

**Late Renewal:**
- Applications received after deadline may not be considered
- Recipient should contact Grantor if renewal delayed due to circumstances beyond their control

### 5.3 Reasons for Non-Renewal

A grant may not be renewed if Recipient:
- Has dropped below minimum academic performance
- Is no longer enrolled in approved institution/program
- Has engaged in academic dishonesty
- Has violated grant conditions
- Has misused grant funds
- Has received funding that exceeds reasonable total support
- Has not completed required reporting
- No longer meets eligibility criteria

## 6. Termination & Repayment

### 6.1 Grounds for Immediate Termination

EduAll Foundation may terminate a grant immediately if:

**Academic Misconduct:**
- Plagiarism, cheating, or academic dishonesty
- Submission of false documents
- Misrepresentation in application

**Legal Issues:**
- Criminal activity or conviction
- Serious violation of laws
- Breach of residency/immigration requirements
- Expulsion from institution

**Grant Misuse:**
- Use of funds for prohibited purposes
- Fraudulent expense claims
- Failure to maintain records of expenses
- Refusal to cooperate with audits

**Non-Compliance:**
- Withdrawal from institution without approval
- Failure to maintain enrollment status
- Unresponsiveness to Grantor communication

### 6.2 Repayment Obligations

In cases of grant termination or misuse, the Recipient may be required to **repay all or part of grant funds**:

**Repayment Scenarios:**
- Use of funds for prohibited purposes: Immediate repayment of misused amount
- Early termination: Possible repayment of unused/allocated funds
- Fraudulent information: Repayment of entire grant
- Non-compliance: Partial repayment at Grantor's discretion

**Repayment Terms:**
- Amount: Specified in termination notice
- Timeline: Due within 30 days of notice (or agreed payment plan)
- Method: Bank transfer to EduAll Foundation account
- Consequences: Legal action may be pursued for non-payment

### 6.3 Appeal & Dispute Resolution

If grant is terminated, Recipient may:
1. **Request Explanation:** Written explanation of termination within 5 business days
2. **Provide Response:** Submit written response to stated reasons within 10 days
3. **Request Review:** Ask for independent review by Grants Committee (within 15 days)
4. **Formal Appeal:** Submit formal appeal with supporting documentation

**Appeal Process:**
- Appeal reviewed by independent panel (not involved in original decision)
- Recipient may provide additional documentation
- Decision made within 30 days
- Appeal decision is final

## 8. Confidentiality & Data Protection

### 8.1 Data Privacy

EduAll Foundation is committed to protecting Recipient's privacy in accordance with Swiss data protection laws (DSG/LPD 2020).

**Personal Data Collected:**
- Contact information
- Financial information
- Academic records
- Medical information (if disability accommodations required)
- References and other supporting documents

### 8.2 Use of Personal Data

Personal data is used solely for:
- Processing grant application
- Making award decisions
- Managing grant disbursement
- Monitoring grant compliance
- Evaluating foundation impact
- Communicating with Recipient

### 8.3 Data Sharing

Personal data is **not shared** with:
- Other students or applicants
- External organizations (except as required by law)
- Marketing or third-party vendors
- Social media platforms

Data is shared **only with:**
- EduAll staff (need-to-know basis)
- Selection committee members (confidentially)
- Swiss authorities (if legally required)
- Auditors (for compliance purposes)

### 8.4 Data Retention

- **Active grant period:** Data retained during grant
- **After termination:** Data retained for 5 years
- **Alumni records:** Limited contact information retained indefinitely (with opt-out option)
- **Destruction:** Data securely destroyed after retention period

### 8.5 Rights & Access

Recipient may:
- Request access to personal data held by Grantor
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of data (after grant period)
- Withdraw consent for contact (opt-out)
- File a complaint with Swiss Data Protection Authority

## 11. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

### 11.1 Applicable Law

These terms are governed by Swiss law, specifically the laws of the Canton of Geneva.

### 11.2 Dispute Resolution

**Step 1: Negotiation**
- Parties attempt to resolve dispute informally
- Written communication encouraged

**Step 2: Mediation**
- If unresolved, parties may request mediation
- Neutral mediator appointed
- Mediation conducted in Geneva

**Step 3: Courts**
- If mediation is unsuccessful, either party may bring the dispute before the courts (see 11.3)

**Costs:**
- Each party bears its own legal costs
- Mediation costs are split equally

### 11.3 Jurisdiction

- Swiss courts have jurisdiction over disputes
- Venue: Canton of Geneva courts
- Recipient consents to jurisdiction of Swiss courts
