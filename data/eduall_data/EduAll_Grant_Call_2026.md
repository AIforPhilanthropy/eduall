## Executive Summary

EduAll Foundation is pleased to announce the 2026 International Student Grant Call. We are offering **CHF 500,000** in financial support for talented and motivated international students pursuing studies at accredited Swiss higher education institutions.

This call is open to all non-Swiss citizens who are legally residing in Switzerland and who are experiencing financial hardship in funding their tertiary education. We invite applications from prospective and current students at the bachelor's, master's, and doctoral levels.

**Total Available Budget:** CHF 500,000 (an estimated 140-180 awards in total). Most awards are below the maximum: in 2025 the average award was CHF 3,345.

### Grant Period and Award Amount

- Awards from this call cover the **2027/28 academic year (September 2027 - August 2028)** and are paid on 1 September 2027 (in one instalment, or in two instalments in September and February on request).
- Only study time from September 2027 onwards can be funded. Applicants who complete their programme before September 2027 cannot receive an award from this call. Applicants who complete their programme during the grant period (for example in January 2028) receive an award pro-rated to the months of study remaining from September 2027.
- The award amount is set by the selection committee and normally does not exceed the applicant's documented shortfall for the grant period (monthly expenses minus family support and personal income, taking into account other scholarships and available savings), up to the maximum for the study level.
- Full-time and part-time students are eligible on the same terms, and the maximum award is the same for both. Because many part-time students work alongside their studies, and some plan to change their study mode, the application form asks for the planned study mode for 2027/28 and the expected personal income for 2027/28. The shortfall for the grant period is assessed on this basis.
- EduAll only funds the time an applicant still needs to complete the degree. For this assessment, EduAll uses the following standard programme requirements, including the thesis or dissertation: **bachelor's degree: 40 courses; master's degree: 12 courses; doctorate: 12 courses**. Applicants report in the application form how many courses they have completed so far.
- Renewals for later years are decided through the annual renewal process (see Terms & Conditions).

## Eligibility Criteria

### Primary Eligibility Requirements

1. **Citizenship:** Non-Swiss citizen
2. **Residency Status:** Legally residing in Switzerland (valid permit: B, C, F, S, L, G, or equivalent; see below for pending permits)
3. **Academic Status:** 
   - Accepted or currently enrolled in an accredited Swiss higher education institution
   - Valid enrollment status maintained throughout grant period
4. **Financial Need:** Documented genuine financial need for education and living costs in Switzerland
5. **Academic Standing:** Satisfactory academic performance, normally a GPA of at least 2.5/4.0 (or equivalent). Applicants below this threshold may still be considered if they explain the reasons in their application and show how they are improving.
6. **Study Program:** Pursuing a bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree, full-time or part-time (part-time study must be formally recognised by the institution). Please note that under Swiss immigration rules, holders of a residence permit issued for study purposes (EU/EFTA and non-EU citizens alike) must study full-time, with studies as the main purpose of their stay, and may work at most 15 hours a week during semesters.
7. **Legal Compliance:** No outstanding legal or immigration issues; in good standing with all institutions

### Applicants with a Pending Residence Permit

Applicants who have applied for a Swiss residence permit (first permit or renewal) and whose application is still pending may apply. Cantonal migration offices generally grant a student permit only on proof of sufficient financial means (around CHF 2,600 per month). Such applicants must explain their situation in the Statement of Declared Circumstances and attach proof of the pending application. Eligibility is assessed case by case, and any award is conditional on the permit being granted.

### Special Conditions for Doctoral Studies

Applicants for doctoral grants must additionally demonstrate:
- Enrollment in a doctoral programme at an accredited Swiss university
- Documented lack of funding from university, advisor, or supervisor
- Exceptional circumstances making university funding unavailable
- Clear timeline for degree completion (typically 3-4 years)

### Priority Considerations

While all eligible candidates are welcome to apply, the following groups are considered priority:
- Students from low-income countries (GNI per capita below USD 3,000)
- First-generation university students
- Students from underrepresented regions
- Students demonstrating exceptional resilience and determination

## Ineligible Categories

The following candidates are **not eligible** to apply:

- Swiss citizens
- Students with permanent residency (Permit C) and significant family financial resources
- Students in distance-learning programmes
- Students in non-accredited or private institutions without official recognition
- Students with outstanding legal or immigration issues
- Individuals with previous grants from EduAll Foundation within the last 2 years
- Students in programs lasting less than one academic year

## What Can Grants Cover?

Grant funds may be used for:

### Education Costs
- University tuition fees
- Registration and administrative fees
- Examination fees
- Student union fees
- Mandatory study materials and books
- Laboratory or workshop materials (if required by program)

### Living Expenses
- Accommodation (rent, utilities, internet)
- Food and groceries
- Transportation (local and occasional travel within Switzerland)
- Personal healthcare and insurance costs
- Clothing and personal necessities

---

## What Cannot Be Covered

Grant funds cannot be used for:

- Tuition in non-accredited institutions
- Travel to/from applicant's home country
- Professional certifications outside degree program
- Internships or voluntary service programs
- Entertainment, recreational activities, or luxury items
- Family members' expenses
- Debt repayment
- Childcare or dependent care (separate program available)

## Funding Terms & Conditions

### Grant Award Conditions

1. **Verification:** Grant recipients may be required to provide verification of enrollment and academic progress
2. **Renewal:** Grants are awarded on an annual basis and are renewable subject to:
   - Continued enrollment in approved program
   - Maintaining satisfactory academic performance
   - Evidence of continued financial need
   - Compliance with all grant terms
3. **Changes in Circumstance:** Recipients must immediately notify EduAll of significant changes in financial, legal, or academic status
4. **Reporting:** Recipients must complete annual progress reports
5. **Accountability:** Funds must be used solely for approved purposes

### Financial Obligations

- Grants are **not loans** and do not require repayment
- Recipients are not obligated to work for EduAll Foundation or any affiliated organization
- Recipients should declare any additional funding from other sources

### Maximum Support

- No student shall receive more than CHF 8,000 per year (or program-specific maximum)
- Maximum total support: CHF 32,000 for bachelor's; CHF 13,000 for master's; CHF 15,000 for doctoral (CHF 5,000 per year, renewable up to 3 years)
- The same annual and total maxima apply to full-time and part-time students
- If student receives other scholarships/grants, EduAll support may be reduced to avoid duplication

### Application Materials Required

All applicants must submit the following:

1. **Completed Application Form** (provided in online portal)
   - Personal information
   - Educational background
   - Current/intended study program details, including study mode (full-time or part-time) now and planned for 2027/28
   - Financial situation overview

2. **Financial Documentation:**
   - Recent bank statements (last 3 months)
   - Parents' or sponsors' income documentation (if applicable)
   - Proof of assets and liabilities
   - Rent agreement or housing cost documentation
   - Any other evidence of financial constraints

3. **Academic Documentation:**
   - University acceptance letter (if not yet enrolled) OR
   - Current enrollment confirmation
   - Transcript of academic records (last completed semester/year)
   - Proof of visa/residency permit
   - Valid passport copy

4. **Written Statements** (completed in the online application form; each statement appears in the form with the instructions below):
   - **Financial Need Statement (300–400 words).** *Instructions:* Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).
   - **Personal Statement (max. 500 words).** *Instructions:* Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).
   - **Statement of Declared Circumstances (max. 300 words).** The application form includes a checklist of circumstances (first-generation student, refugee/asylum status, disability, housing insecurity, family emergency, language barrier, student parent/dependents, undocumented or pending residence status; volunteer experience, work experience, leadership role, community engagement, overcoming significant adversity). *Instructions:* For each circumstance you have ticked in the checklist (risk factors and resilience/strength factors), briefly describe your situation and how it affects or has shaped your studies. You may attach supporting documents. If you have not ticked any circumstance, you may leave this section blank (max. 300 words).

5. **References (two required):**
   - **Academic reference:** Professor, supervisor, or academic advisor
   - **Personal reference:** Non-family member who can attest to your character
   - References should submit letters directly via the online portal

6. **Curriculum Vitae/Resume**
   - Education history
   - Work experience (if any)
   - Skills and languages
   - Volunteer experience or community engagement (if applicable)

## Selection Criteria & Process

### Evaluation Criteria

Applications will be evaluated based on:

1. **Financial Need (40%)**
   - Demonstrated genuine financial hardship
   - Lack of alternative funding sources
   - Ratio of living costs to available resources
   - Geographic/socioeconomic background

2. **Academic Motivation & Potential (25%)**
   - Clear educational goals
   - Academic progress and standing
   - Coherence between aspirations and study program
   - Evidence of commitment to studies

3. **Personal Resilience & Determination (20%)**
   - Evidence of overcoming challenges
   - Self-motivation and independence
   - Community engagement or volunteer work
   - Personal growth and development

4. **Contribution to Diversity (10%)**
   - Geographic representation
   - First-generation student status
   - Underrepresented background or perspectives
   - Potential to bring diverse viewpoints to campus

5. **Alignment with EduAll Values (5%)**
   - Understanding of grant principles
   - Commitment to equal opportunity
   - Interest in contributing to community post-graduation

### Selection Process

1. **Initial Screening:** All applications reviewed for completeness and eligibility
2. **Preliminary Assessment:** Applications ranked using evaluation criteria
3. **Interviews:** Top candidates (approximately 50% of applicants) invited for structured interviews
4. **Final Selection:** Selection committee makes final decisions
5. **Notification:** Applicants notified of decisions by 30 June 2027
6. **Acceptance:** Selected recipients confirm acceptance of grant award

- Appeals are reviewed by an independent panel not involved in initial selection

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I apply if I'm currently in the middle of my studies?**  
A: Yes. Applicants already enrolled are welcome to apply. You should describe your remaining time in the program.

**Q: What if my family is wealthy but won't support my education?**  
A: We evaluate financial need based on actual available resources. If family support is unavailable despite wealth, please explain this in your application.

**Q: Can I receive grants in multiple years?**  
A: Yes, grants are renewable annually. However, you must reapply each year and meet renewal criteria.

**Q: What if I don't have all required documents?**  
A: Submit what you have and explain in your application which documents are missing and why.

**Q: Are there grants specifically for refugee or asylum-seeker students?**  
A: We consider all eligible applicants. Refugees/asylum seekers with legal residency permits are encouraged to apply.
