# EduAll Foundation - Sample Candidate Profiles (2024-2025 Calls)

## Overview

The following profiles are based on real applications to EduAll's 2024 and 2025 grant calls. Names and identifying details have been changed. They illustrate the range of students EduAll supports and how the selection committee applied its criteria, including one application that was not selected and one that received partial support.

Until 2025, awards covered the academic year in which the call was published (for example, the 2025 call covered September 2025 - August 2026). From the 2026 call onwards, decisions are made in June and funding starts the following September: awards from the 2026 call cover September 2027 - August 2028. Continued support is decided through the annual renewal process.

## Profile 1: Amara - Master's Student from Senegal

**Background:**
- Name: Amara Diallo
- Age: 28
- Home Country: Senegal
- Current Status: Master's student in Public Health at the University of Lausanne (UNIL), entering her second year in September 2026
- Residency Status: Permit B (student)
- EduAll Status: 2025 Recipient; renewal for 2026/27 approved in August 2026

**Education History:**
- Bachelor's degree in Biology, Cheikh Anta Diop University, Dakar, 2019
- 5 years as a health educator in rural Senegal (2019-2024)
- Master's programme started September 2025 at UNIL

**Financial Situation (at application, 2025):**
- Personal income: CHF 800/month (part-time work as a research assistant)
- Family support: CHF 0 (both parents are primary school teachers; their salaries barely cover their own household and her younger siblings' schooling)
- Monthly expenses in Lausanne: CHF 2,200
  - Rent (room in shared flat): CHF 900
  - Food: CHF 400
  - Health insurance: CHF 320
  - Utilities and phone: CHF 150
  - Tuition and fees (CHF 580/semester): CHF 100
  - Transport: CHF 80
  - Study materials: CHF 100
  - Other essentials: CHF 150
- Monthly shortfall: CHF 1,400

**Why EduAll Selected Amara:**
- Clear financial need: her family cannot contribute, and her own income covers only a third of her costs
- Clear goals: she plans to return to Senegal to build maternal health programmes
- West Africa is a priority region for EduAll
- Five years of work in rural health show commitment to her field

**Grant Received:**
- 2025/26: CHF 6,500
- 2026/27: CHF 6,500 (renewal)

**Impact:**
Amara reduced her working hours and is writing her thesis on maternal health in low-resource settings.

## Profile 3: Yuki - Doctoral Student from Japan

**Background:**
- Name: Yuki Tanaka
- Age: 28
- Home Country: Japan
- Current Status: Doctoral student in Neuroscience at the University of Zurich (UZH), started September 2025
- Residency Status: Permit B (student)
- EduAll Status: 2025 Recipient (doctoral grant, exceptional circumstances)

**Education History:**
- Bachelor's degree in Biology, Osaka University, 2020
- Master's degree in Molecular Biology, University of Tokyo, 2022
- Research assistant, University of Tokyo, 2022-2025

**Financial Situation (at application, 2025):**
- Personal income: CHF 0. Yuki was admitted with a three-year fellowship from a Japanese foundation, which was cancelled in July 2025 when the foundation closed its international programme. Her supervisor has applied for project funding, but a salaried position will not be available before 2027.
- Family support: CHF 150/month (parents supportive but on a modest income)
- Savings: CHF 6,000 (used for her first months in Zurich)
- Monthly expenses in Zurich: CHF 2,100
  - Rent: CHF 950
  - Food: CHF 400
  - Health insurance: CHF 300
  - Utilities and phone: CHF 120
  - Transport: CHF 80
  - Study materials: CHF 100
  - Other essentials: CHF 150
- Monthly shortfall: CHF 1,950

**Why EduAll Selected Yuki:**
- Doctoral grants are only given when university funding is unavailable; the cancelled fellowship and her supervisor's written confirmation met this condition
- A clear timeline: funding from the supervisor's project is expected from 2027
- A bridging grant makes it possible for her to continue her doctorate until then

Yuki's case also shows that EduAll does not only fund students from low-income countries when there is a genuine, documented gap.

**Grant Received:**
- 2025/26: CHF 5,000 (renewable up to the 3-year maximum)

**Special Conditions:**
- Renewal only if university or project funding is still unavailable and her academic progress is satisfactory

**Impact:**
Yuki kept her place in the lab and completed her first-year doctoral courses.

## Profile 7: Hassan - Doctoral Student from Iraq (Exceptional Case)

**Background:**
- Name: Hassan Ahmed Al-Rashid
- Age: 36
- Home Country: Iraq
- Current Status: Doctoral student in Immunology at the University of Basel, started September 2025
- Residency Status: Permit B (recognised refugee)
- EduAll Status: 2025 Recipient (doctoral grant, exceptional circumstances)

**Education History:**
- Medical degree, University of Baghdad, 2014
- Physician in Baghdad, 2014-2019
- Left Iraq in 2019 for safety reasons; four years in the asylum process in Switzerland, recognised as a refugee in 2023
- Medical diploma only partly recognised; admitted to a doctoral programme in 2025

**Exceptional Circumstances:**
- Refugee without assets: he lost his home and savings when he left Iraq
- His parents and a sibling remain in Iraq and cannot support him
- His doctoral project is funded for laboratory costs only; the university has no salaried position for him before 2027

**Financial Situation (at application, 2025):**
- Personal income: CHF 0
- Family support: CHF 0
- Monthly expenses: CHF 1,800
- Monthly shortfall: CHF 1,800

**Why EduAll Selected Hassan:**
- Very high financial need, with no family support or savings
- Doctoral funding condition met: no salaried position available, confirmed in writing
- Resilience: rebuilt his career after years in the asylum process

**Grant Received:**
- 2025/26: CHF 5,000

**Special Conditions:**
- Annual renewal depends on enrolment and academic progress, up to the 3-year maximum
- Access to EduAll's emergency support fund if needed

**Impact:**
Hassan did not have to look for night work alongside his doctorate and could focus on his research on immune responses to infection.

## Summary of Diversity & Impact

**Geographic Distribution of 2025 Recipients:**
- Sub-Saharan Africa: 35%
- South Asia: 20%
- Southeast Asia: 15%
- Middle East/North Africa: 15%
- Eastern Europe: 10%
- Other: 5%

**Study Levels:**
- Bachelor's students: 55%
- Master's students: 40%
- Doctoral students: 5%

**Selection Criteria (Weighted):**
1. Financial Need: 40%
2. Academic Motivation & Potential: 25%
3. Personal Resilience & Determination: 20%
4. Contribution to Diversity: 10%
5. Alignment with EduAll Values: 5%

**Common Themes Among Selected Candidates:**
- A real shortfall between resources and costs
- Clear educational goals
- Evidence of having dealt with obstacles
- Backgrounds from priority regions or first-generation families

**Outcome Tracking (2025 Recipients):**
- Graduated or on track to complete: 98%
- Continuing to the next year: 97%
- Working in their field after graduation (earlier cohorts): 89%
- Mentoring or supporting other students: 45%
