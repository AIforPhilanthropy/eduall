## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty-five, a former high-school history teacher from Mariupol.

My parents were steelworkers at the Azovstal plant. Neither went to university. I was the first. For eight years I taught history in a school in Mariupol, and I loved it.

In 2022 my city was destroyed. My school was destroyed. My parents survived the siege in the basement of the plant and now live with my sister in Lviv. I left with my wife, and we came to Switzerland.

In Lausanne I began a master's in history in 2026. My research is about how cities destroyed in war rebuild their memory, comparing Mariupol with European cities after 1945.

I want to teach again, and to help preserve the memory of my city.
