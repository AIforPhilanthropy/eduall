## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first week in St. Gallen, I went to the Abbey Library. I stood in the room with thousand-year-old books and I thought about my grandmother, who could not read. She would have said that this room is for other people. I want to show that it is also for people like us.

My parents grew up poor. My father started working at fourteen in a furniture workshop, and twenty years later he owned it. My mother did the accounts. They gave me everything they did not have: time to study.

During my bachelor in Sofia, I was president of the sociology students' society. I volunteered for three years in a programme for Roma children, helping with homework and organising summer camps. I also worked as a waitress to pay my books.

My research is about discrimination against Roma in education. After my master, I want to work in social policy for the European Union or for an NGO.
