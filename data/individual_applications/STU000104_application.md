## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am twenty-eight, from Casablanca, and I am about to become the first chemist in my family.

My father was a fisherman in the port, my mother sold bread. Neither went to school for long. When I was a teenager, I worked summers at a fish-processing plant, packing sardines in ice. I became curious about why fish spoiled so fast, and why the ice needed salt. A teacher lent me a chemistry book. That was the beginning.

After school I worked five years at the same plant, then in the quality laboratory, measuring freshness and contaminants. My supervisor there pushed me to apply abroad. I learned German in Casablanca at the Goethe-Institut, and in 2025 I came to Zurich.

It has not been easy: German chemistry lectures, lab reports, and a very different style of learning. But I have passed all my courses.

My thesis is on catalysts for greener synthesis. After my bachelor I want to do a master in food chemistry and work on food safety, maybe one day back in Morocco's fishing industry.
