## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In fifteen years, I want to be the historian who writes the history of the Bugis sailors of Sulawesi, not from the records of the Dutch colonial officers, but from the voices of the sailors' descendants.

My parents were fishermen and never went to school. My grandfather sailed a wooden pinisi schooner to Singapore and Australia, and he told me stories that no book contained. I decided to study history to find them.

I studied history in Makassar and worked six years as a curator's assistant in a regional museum. On weekends I volunteered with a group recording oral histories of old sailors, and I organised a community archive in my village.

In Zurich I work at a museum and continue to collect stories, now from Indonesian communities in Europe.
