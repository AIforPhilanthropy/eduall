## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty-two, a Yazidi woman from Sinjar, and in January 2028 I will complete my Bachelor of Medicine in Basel.

In August 2014 my family escaped to Mount Sinjar with thousands of others when ISIS attacked. I was twenty. We spent nine days on the mountain without enough water. My parents, who are farmers and never went to school, carried my younger brothers.

For three years I lived in a displacement camp near Duhok, where I volunteered in the camp clinic, translating and helping women who had survived captivity to reach doctors. I also helped organise literacy classes for girls.

In 2018 I came to Switzerland to marry my husband, whom I had met through relatives and who had lived in Basel for many years; I received my residence permit through our marriage. I then learned German and prepared for medical school. German medical terminology is still very hard for me, and I study twice as long as my classmates. But I have not failed a single year.

I want to become a gynaecologist and work with survivors of sexual violence.
