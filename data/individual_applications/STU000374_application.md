*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Kibera, in Nairobi. My mother sold vegetables and my father drove a matatu until he lost his leg in an accident when I was eight. Neither went to school beyond primary level. I studied under a single bulb shared by three families.

I won a scholarship to a boarding school and then to the University of Nairobi, where I studied mechanical engineering and worked as a research assistant, co-authoring two papers on solar pumps. On weekends, I taught mathematics at a community school in Kibera and helped organise a youth sports programme.

EPFL is the place where I can learn to design these pumps properly.
