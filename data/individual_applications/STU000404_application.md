## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The first time I saw the Rhône glacier, on an ETH field trip in 2025, I stood at the edge of the ice and thought of the Senegal River, which in my childhood flooded every rainy season and now barely reaches the fields. The glacier and the river are the same story, told on two continents.

My parents are farmers in the Fouta region and never went to school. At fourteen I left for Dakar to work as a domestic helper for a family who, in return, let me attend school. Later I worked for four years for an agricultural cooperative, where I rose to lead a team of five field agents supporting farmers with irrigation.

At twenty-four I decided to study the science behind the changes I had seen.

I want to work on water management for farming communities in the Sahel.
