## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

My monthly costs are CHF 1,780. The main items are rent (CHF 531 for a room in a student residence), food (CHF 413) and ETH fees (CHF 243). The rest is health insurance, transport, phone, books and other small costs.

My aunt, who lives in Zurich and works as a housekeeper in a hotel, supports me with CHF 1,163 a month. Her household income is about CHF 6,800, so she is giving me a sixth of what she and her husband earn, while they also send money to our family at home. I do not have a job. I have no savings and no other scholarship. Each month I am short CHF 617.

To be honest, I have tried to work, but physics at ETH has a very heavy load, with exercise sheets every week in every course, and my grades dropped in the semester when I tried to combine study and a weekend job. My GPA is 2.72. It is not where I want it to be, but it has been going up since I stopped working.

I also know that my aunt cannot continue at this level for two more semesters. Her husband's hours at a restaurant were reduced this year.

I am applying so that I can finish my bachelor in July 2028 without taking money from my aunt that she needs for herself.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am 25, I study physics at ETH Zurich, and I grew up in Davao, on the island of Mindanao.

My parents are coconut farmers. They did not go to secondary school. I was the first in my family to finish high school, and a teacher lent me her own university textbooks in physics because the school library did not have any.

When I was nineteen, I became involved in a student group that documented land conflicts in our region. Some members of the group were threatened, and one was killed. My family decided it was not safe for me to stay. I came to Switzerland in 2021, where my aunt already lived, and I was granted protection. This was the most painful decision of my life, and I still miss my parents every day.

In Zurich I learned German and passed the university entrance exam. I started at ETH in autumn 2025. It is very hard, and I am honest that I am not the best in my class. But when I solve a problem after hours of work, I feel something I have never felt before.

I help at a Filipino community centre in Zurich, where I give homework support to children on Saturdays, and I am part of a group that helps newly arrived Filipino workers with administration and German.

I want to become a physics teacher, maybe one day in Mindanao. Education for me means that where you are born does not decide what you can understand.
