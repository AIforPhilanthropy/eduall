## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Why do some children learn to love school and others learn to fear it? I asked this question for the first time at fifteen, in my first month in a Basel school. I did not understand one word of German. The teacher spoke fast and never looked at me. I sat at the back and felt invisible.

In the second year I had a new teacher. She sat next to me for five minutes every morning, with a picture book. After three months, I was reading. After one year, I was one of the best in the class. One teacher changed everything.

I came to Switzerland in 2010 with my mother and my sisters, to join my father, who worked here. Later my parents went back to Erbil. I stayed, finished school and did an apprenticeship as a childcare worker. For five years I worked in a daycare, and I volunteered every Saturday in a homework club for refugee children.

In 2023 I started university to become a teacher. My thesis is about how teachers welcome children who arrive without the language. I want to be the teacher who sits next to the child in the back.
