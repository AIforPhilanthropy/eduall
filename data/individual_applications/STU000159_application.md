## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What does a person do when everything is taken by the water? In November 2013 the typhoon Haiyan destroyed Tacloban, my city. I was nine. Our house was gone, my father's small shop was gone, and my grandmother died. For one year we lived in a tent city.

In the tent city, I went to school in a tent, and I helped my mother to carry water and food for our block. Children grew up fast there; it was simply necessary.

My parents are teachers. They rebuilt slowly. My sister went to work abroad to help the family. After secondary school I stayed two years to help my parents rebuild the house and to care for my father when he became ill. My family needed me at home.

Years later, a friend showed me data maps of the typhoon damage, made by volunteers from satellite images. I understood that data can save lives: where to send water, where to send doctors. I decided to study data science. In 2025 I came to Lausanne.

My studies go well, GPA 3.69. I want to work in disaster response with data, for organisations that help after typhoons and floods.
