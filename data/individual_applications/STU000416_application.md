## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

How much does it cost to be the first? In my family, it has cost everything we had, and I am grateful and afraid at the same time.

I began the Bachelor in Physics at the University of Bern in September 2025. My costs are CHF 2,231 each month; my room is CHF 1,102. My uncle, a shopkeeper in Sylhet (household income around CHF 670), sends me CHF 190, more than a quarter of what they have. I receive a scholarship of CHF 7,543 a year from a foundation for students with disabilities, which I am deeply thankful for.

I do not work. My visual impairment makes most student jobs impossible, and the first year of physics in German takes every hour I have.

A grant from EduAll would let me continue this path, which my whole family has carried with me.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What does a physicist see when their eyes do not see well? Patterns, equations, and the hands of the people who helped him. I was born with severe myopia and nystagmus; I read with a magnifier pressed to the page. In my village near Sylhet, teachers told my parents, farmers who never went to school, that I would never study.

My uncle refused to believe them. He paid for a special school in Dhaka. There I founded a science club for visually impaired students, and I volunteered teaching mathematics by voice and touch to younger children.

In 2022, floods destroyed our village. We lost our house and my father's fields. I sat my final exams in a shelter.

German is my fourth language, and with my eyes, reading German texts is very slow. But I will not stop.
