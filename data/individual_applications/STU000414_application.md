## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Let me introduce myself: I am twenty-four, from Mombasa, the first in my family to go to university, and soon an architect.

My parents are fishermen who never went to school. I grew up in a house of coral stone in the old town of Mombasa, and I spent my childhood drawing its carved doors. At seventeen, a Swiss architect working on the restoration of the old town saw my drawings and encouraged me to study.

It took years to prepare: learning German, passing the ETH entrance exam. At ETH I was elected representative of the international students in the architecture department, and I organised an exhibition on East African coastal architecture.

German is still a barrier in theory courses. But in the studio, my drawings speak for me.

My diploma project is on climate-adapted housing using coral stone techniques.
