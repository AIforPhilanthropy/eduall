## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What would I be doing if the volcano had not erupted? I ask this sometimes. In 2010, when I was six, Mount Merapi erupted near my village. We lost our house and our cows. For one year we lived in a temporary shelter.

My father was a farmer, but he had studied agriculture at university before he took over my grandfather's land; my mother is a teacher. After the eruption they rebuilt everything.

After school I worked three years in a car parts factory in Surabaya to help my family repay the debts from the rebuilding. In the evenings I read physics books and watched university lectures online.

At twenty I finally came to Basel to study physics. It is the hardest thing I have done, and the best.

I want to study volcanology and geophysics, and one day work in the volcano monitoring service in Indonesia.
