## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first week of clinical rotations in Fribourg, a Pakistani woman came into the emergency room. She spoke no French and no German. The team was looking for an interpreter. I stepped forward and spoke to her in Urdu. I saw her face change, from fear to relief. The senior doctor said, "Now we can start." I thought: this is why I am here.

I grew up in a two-room house in Rawalpindi. My father drives a rickshaw and my mother sews at home. Neither went to school. When I was accepted to study medicine in Switzerland with a scholarship for my first year, the whole street came to our house.

The first years were hard: French and German, the cold, the loneliness, and money always short. I worked on weekends in a care home from my second semester. I have never failed an exam.

After my master I want to specialise in family medicine, in Switzerland, where there are too few general practitioners, and one day to help build primary care in poor neighbourhoods of Pakistan.
