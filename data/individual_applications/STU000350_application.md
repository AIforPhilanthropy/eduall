## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I want to be honest, because I think honesty is the only thing I have that is completely mine.

My costs are CHF 2,238 a month. HSG tuition alone is CHF 522. I receive a scholarship of CHF 7,271 a year from a foundation for women in technology, which I am very thankful for. I work as a student assistant in the IT support office and earn CHF 530. My uncle in Lucknow, a clerk, sends me CHF 240 out of a household income of about CHF 960.

With the scholarship, the real gap is smaller than the CHF 1,468 in the form, but it is still several hundred francs every month, and I have no savings.

In March my father, a rickshaw driver, was hit by a truck. He survived, but his leg was crushed, and he will not drive again. My mother now works as a domestic helper. My uncle, who has always helped me, now helps them too. I can already see that his money for me will stop soon.

I have two years left. I do not want to go home before I finish.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first week in St. Gallen, in February 2025, I walked into the library and saw a whole wall of computers, free for students. I sat down and cried a little. In my town near Lucknow, I learned programming on my cousin's broken phone, and once a week in a cyber café.

My parents never went to school. I was the first girl in my family to finish high school. I won a national coding competition for girls, and that brought me a scholarship to St. Gallen.

In Lucknow, I volunteered for two years teaching coding to girls from my neighbourhood, and I helped organise a small library in our community centre. I also worked as a data-entry clerk to pay for my exams.

I want to become a software engineer and build tools that help women start their own businesses.
