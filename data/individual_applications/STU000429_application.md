I began the Master's in Philosophy at the University of Zurich in February 2026. My monthly costs are CHF 2,053, including CHF 1,120 for a small flat I share with my wife and our one-year-old son. My son's costs are not in my budget, but they are part of every decision I make.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was ten, in Homs, my grandfather, a baker who never learned to read, asked me to read him the newspaper every evening. One night he asked: "Why do people believe lies?" I did not have an answer. I have been searching for one ever since.

My parents ran a small shop and never went to school. In 2013, our neighbourhood was destroyed. We lived in Lebanon for four years, where I worked in a bakery before school and after. In 2017 we came to Switzerland.

I learned German, finished school, studied philosophy at the University of Zurich, and became a father at twenty-two. I also help run a reading group for young Syrians in Zurich, where we read philosophy in Arabic and German.

My research is on the ethics of truth and propaganda in wartime.
