## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I am in my second year of the Bachelor's in Chemistry at the University of Basel, and I am applying to EduAll because the support that made my studies possible has suddenly become uncertain.

My monthly expenses are CHF 1,733: a room in a shared flat for CHF 865, tuition and fees of CHF 142 a month, and modest costs for food, transport and insurance. In April my father, who worked as a chef in Basel, suffered a serious kitchen accident and lost part of his hand. He cannot work, and my family now lives on insurance payments and my mother's small income.

I then changed to part-time study and went back to my old job in a pharmacy distribution warehouse, now about 17 hours a week, where I earn CHF 1,885. My aunt in Chennai, a nurse with a household income of around CHF 1,240, still sends me CHF 90 a month. My budget is balanced, but it leaves nothing for my parents, and the laboratory courses, which are compulsory and take most afternoons, make every extra shift difficult.

An EduAll grant would allow me to continue my studies at a steady pace, finish in July 2030, and help my family while it recovers from this blow.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Chemistry matters because it is the science of making things safer. My father spent twenty years in restaurant kitchens, surrounded by gas, oil and cleaning chemicals, with no training in how any of them worked. In April, a gas leak caused a flash fire. He survived, but he will never cook again.

I grew up between Chennai and Basel. My parents came to Switzerland when I was twelve, and I have lived here since. I studied at a Basel gymnasium, worked for four years in a pharmacy warehouse to save money, and started chemistry in 2025.

I am interested in industrial safety and in green chemistry, the design of processes and products that are less dangerous for the people who use them. My father's accident has made this interest very personal.

I want to work in chemical safety, in industry or for public authorities, so that fewer workers pay for other people's ignorance of chemistry with their bodies.
