## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty-four years old, from Barisal, and for many years I was a cook.

My parents are farmers and never went to school. I left school at sixteen to work in a restaurant in Dhaka, and in 2014 I came to Europe, first to Italy, then to Switzerland. My asylum application was rejected, but for years I could not be sent back, because the Bangladeshi consulate did not issue me travel documents, and in 2019 I was provisionally admitted. I cooked in restaurants in Lausanne for many years. In my free time I volunteered at a food bank, and I helped new Bangladeshi workers with papers.

In the kitchen I used a small tablet to count orders and ingredients. I wanted to understand the numbers better. I started to learn programming online at night, and then I decided to study data science properly.

I want to work with data for food systems, for example to reduce food waste in restaurants and supermarkets.
