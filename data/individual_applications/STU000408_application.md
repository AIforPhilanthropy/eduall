*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In February 2026, in my first week in Fribourg, I slipped on the ice with my crutches in front of the university. A physics professor helped me up. Two weeks later he offered me a place in his laboratory. I still smile when I think how we met.

I have cerebral palsy, which affects my legs. My parents worked in a factory in Galați and did not go to university. They carried me to school on their backs when the buses were not accessible.

In Romania I worked as a tutor for mathematics and physics for five years, and I led a student group for disability rights at my university. In Fribourg I help in the parents' association of my son's crèche.

My thesis is about optical sensors. I want to become a researcher in photonics.
