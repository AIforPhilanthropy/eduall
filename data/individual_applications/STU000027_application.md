## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

I am in the first year of the Master's in Psychology at the University of Basel. My monthly costs are CHF 1,983, and the only regular money I receive is CHF 200 from my brother. The gap is CHF 1,783 a month.

My brother is a software tester in Pune. His household lives on about CHF 1,310 a month, and he also supports our mother. What he sends me is a real sacrifice for his family.

I do not have a job. I will be honest that I have never had a paid job: before coming to Switzerland I spent many years at home caring for my mother after my father died, and then studying. In Basel I have tried to find student work, but without experience and with limited German it has been very difficult so far. I am still applying.

I have CHF 1,109 left from what I brought with me. It will cover less than one month of the gap. My rent is CHF 769 for a room in a student house, which is already one of the cheapest options in Basel.

My master's ends in January 2028. Without support I don't see how I can stay for the remaining three semesters. With a grant I could finish, and I would continue looking for part-time work to reduce what I need.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was ten, my father was admitted to a psychiatric hospital in our district in Uttar Pradesh. Nobody explained to us what was wrong with him. The neighbours said it was a curse. My mother cried in the kitchen at night. I remember visiting him in a big room full of beds, and thinking that the people there were not bad or cursed, only very, very alone.

My parents did not go to university. My father worked in a sugar mill and my mother at home. After my father died when I was eighteen, I stayed at home to look after my mother, whose health was poor. For years, the only thing I did for myself was read, mostly psychology books from the library.

I did my bachelor's in psychology through distance learning at IGNOU, while caring for my mother. It took me longer than usual, but I finished at the top of my batch. When my brother's income became stable enough to take over our mother's care, I applied to Basel. I was admitted to the master's programme in 2026.

My first semester went well. I have a GPA of 3.7 and an average of 98.1. My professors say I am one of the best in my cohort. I will admit I am less confident about everything outside the classroom: I have no work experience and I have not been part of any clubs or volunteer work. This has been my life so far: caring and studying.

I want to become a clinical psychologist and work on reducing the stigma of mental illness in rural India. I think about my father often, and the big lonely room.
