## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

For two years I have helped run the Bangladeshi students' community in Zurich: welcome dinners, housing advice, exam-time study rooms. Now I am the one who needs help, and I want to explain it honestly.

I am a doctoral student in Materials Science at ETH Zurich, in my third year. My first two years were funded by a Bangladesh government scholarship that paid my living costs. In June it was suspended for all overseas scholars because of the country's budget crisis. My supervisor has confirmed that a salaried ETH position will be possible only from 2028.

Without the scholarship, I could not show the migration office the monthly means required to renew my student permit, about CHF 2,600. My renewal, filed on time in July, is pending. My only income is CHF 170 a month from my uncle in Chittagong, a school clerk whose household earns about CHF 840.

My costs are CHF 2,433. I am using my savings, CHF 7,717, to pay my rent of CHF 1,418. They will last about four months.

And in May my father had a stroke. He cannot work, and my family has no money to spare.

A grant would serve as proof of means for my permit and let me continue my research.
