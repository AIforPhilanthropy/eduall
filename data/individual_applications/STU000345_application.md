## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

For five years in Dhaka I coordinated a programme that taught garment workers their rights, often in the evenings after their twelve-hour shifts. I learned then that people with the least money are often the most generous with their time.

I have four semesters left in my Bachelor's in Sociology at the University of Bern. My monthly costs are CHF 1,603, including a room of CHF 613 and health insurance of CHF 235. I work as a teaching assistant for a German language programme for migrant women and earn CHF 353 a month. My sister in Dhaka, a garment worker (household income around CHF 360), sends me CHF 70, which is a fifth of what she earns. I also receive a scholarship of CHF 7,797 a year from a Swiss foundation for women's education.

My bachelor's thesis requires interviews in several cantons, and my final months leave little room for more work. A grant from EduAll would allow me to complete my degree on schedule, in July 2028.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My father pulled a rickshaw in Dhaka, and my mother cleaned houses. I began working in a garment factory at fourteen, sewing collars for twelve hours a day. In 2012, when I was sixteen, the Tazreen factory fire killed more than a hundred workers, some of them my neighbours. I decided that I would not let my life be only that.

I finished school in the evenings, and then joined a workers' rights organisation, where I trained and led a team of eight educators. We reached thousands of workers.

In 2023 I came to Bern with a scholarship. In Bern I volunteer with a migrant women's association and lead its discussion group.

I want to research labour rights in global supply chains.
