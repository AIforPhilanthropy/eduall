## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother died when I was eighteen. I was the eldest of four. My father worked on fishing boats and was away for weeks. So I raised my siblings. I read physics books at night. When the youngest finished school, my father had a stroke. I cared for him until he died in 2021.

Then my sister, who I had helped through nursing school, paid for my application and first year here.

GPA 3.5, 24 courses. Thesis in condensed matter physics.
