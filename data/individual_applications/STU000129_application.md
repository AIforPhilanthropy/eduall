## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Nursing matters because it is where health care actually touches people. From the age of fifteen I served as a volunteer health helper with a church outreach programme in Lagos, visiting sick and elderly people in their homes in Ajegunle, one of the poorest neighbourhoods of the city. I cleaned wounds, checked blood pressure, explained medicines, and sat with people who were dying alone. I had no formal training. I learned from the nurses who supervised us.

My father was a secondary school teacher and my mother a trader. They wanted me to become a doctor, but after school I could not afford medical studies, and I gave my time to the outreach programme instead.

In my last two years I helped coordinate a community health day twice a year, bringing free check-ups to hundreds of people, and I led a youth group that organised meals for elderly neighbours.

In 2024, with the support of friends from my church in Switzerland, I came to Lausanne, learned French and began to study to become the nurse I had been trying to be since I was a teenager. French is still a challenge, but I have passed all my first-year modules, with a GPA of 3.37.

After my degree, I want to work in community and home care, and later to return to Lagos to train health helpers in neighbourhoods like Ajegunle.
