## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Materials science matters because every technology we rely on is limited by what we can build it from. My interest began at fourteen, when I took apart my grandfather's old Soviet radio in Plovdiv and found that its components, made in the 1970s, still worked better than my three-year-old phone. I wanted to know why.

I came to Fribourg at four, when my parents took up positions at the hospital. I grew up bilingual and studied at the Collège Saint-Michel before beginning materials science.

For the past three summers I have worked as an intern at a precision engineering company in the Jura, testing the fatigue resistance of metal components. It taught me the difference between theory and what actually breaks in practice.

My thesis examines the recyclability of rare-earth magnets. After my bachelor's, I plan a master's in sustainable materials, with the goal of reducing Europe's dependence on newly mined critical materials.
