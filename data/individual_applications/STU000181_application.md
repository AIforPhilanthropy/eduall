## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years, I want to lead a research group in Vietnam on antibiotic resistance in aquaculture, the fish and shrimp farms that feed millions and use antibiotics without control.

I grew up in Can Tho, in the Mekong Delta, surrounded by fish ponds. My parents are both biology teachers. When I was eleven, our family's small shrimp farm was destroyed by disease in a single week. My parents lost their savings and took on debt that took them ten years to repay. I watched my mother cry over the empty ponds.

I studied microbiology in Ho Chi Minh City and worked four years at a national aquaculture research institute, where I published four papers on bacterial disease in shrimp. I also trained farmers in disease prevention.

In 2019 I came to Switzerland with my husband, learned French, and worked as a laboratory technician in Fribourg. In 2025 I began my doctorate on phage therapy as an alternative to antibiotics in aquaculture.

I want to bring these tools back to the Mekong, so that no family loses everything in one week.
