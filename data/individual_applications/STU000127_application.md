I would like to set out my finances honestly, including the parts that make my case weaker.

My monthly expenses are CHF 2,088, including CHF 925 in rent. I study part-time and work about 23 hours a week as a peer counsellor on a youth helpline, earning CHF 2,567 a month. My academic record is also not as strong as I would like: my GPA is 2.63. My first semester of the master's coincided with a family crisis, described below, and I spent many weeks at the hospital rather than at the library.

In February my younger brother was admitted to a psychiatric clinic after a severe crisis. He is now in outpatient treatment, and my mother has reduced her working hours to care for him. From September 2027 I begin my supervised clinical practicum, which is unpaid, and I will have to halve my helpline hours, to about CHF 1,280 a month. The resulting gap of CHF 558 a month is small, but my mother cannot cover it.

An EduAll grant would allow me to finish my master's by January 2030 while supporting my family through this period.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My brother is the reason I study psychology, and now he is also the reason it matters more than ever.

We came to Zurich from Casablanca when I was eight and he was three. My father, an engineer, worked here as a technician until he died when I was fifteen. My mother, who had trained as a nurse in Morocco, worked as a medical secretary to raise us alone.

My brother had difficulties from early on: anxiety, then depression, then this year a crisis that frightened us all. I have seen how hard it is for families like ours to find help: long waiting lists, few therapists who understand our culture, and the silence of the community.

For five years I have volunteered on a youth helpline, and this year I became a paid peer counsellor there. I also co-organise workshops on mental health for Arabic-speaking parents at a community centre in Zurich.

I want to become a psychotherapist specialising in adolescents from migrant families.
