## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Kanpur is an old industrial city on the Ganges, once famous for its leather and textile mills. Most of the mills are closed now. The river is grey, the air is heavy, and in summer the power goes off for hours. I did my homework under a kerosene lamp more often than under a bulb. Maybe this is why I became interested in physics: I wanted to understand light.

My father worked in a tannery until it closed, and my mother stitched school uniforms at home. Neither went to school beyond a few years. I was the first in my family to finish secondary school, with top marks in physics and mathematics.

After school I could not afford university in India, so I worked for four years: first in my brother's phone shop, then as a technician repairing solar panels in villages around Kanpur. This work taught me more than any book: how a photon becomes a current, and how a broken panel means a village in darkness.

In 2025 I was admitted to the University of Zurich. It has been the most exciting time of my life. My results are good, and I am thinking about a master's in condensed matter physics.

I want to work on solar cell materials, and one day bring better and cheaper solar technology back to the villages where I once repaired panels by hand.
