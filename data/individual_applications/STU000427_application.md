## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years, I want to work at a museum or archive, making the history of Southeast Asia accessible to the public in Europe.

I grew up in Yogyakarta. My parents sold batik in the market and never went to school. In 2010, when I was six, the eruption of Merapi covered our village in ash. We lost our house and lived for a year in a shelter.

I was diagnosed with epilepsy at twelve. At school I was often excluded from activities. I came to Lucerne at nineteen with a scholarship for my first year, and worked in a café until my seizures made it impossible. I also volunteered at the Lucerne historical museum, guiding school groups.

My thesis is on how the Dutch colonial archives describe the eruption of 1872.
