## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

For ten years I worked as a site architect in Aleppo, building apartment blocks, schools and a mosque. In 2012, I watched one of my own buildings collapse after a bombing. Since then, I have wanted to build differently.

I have four semesters left in the Bachelor's in Architecture at EPFL, and I will graduate in July 2028. My monthly costs are CHF 2,275, including CHF 1,020 in rent and CHF 243 in tuition. I work as a technical draughtsman two days a week, earning CHF 452. My brother in Gaziantep, a tailor whose household earns about CHF 550, sends me CHF 160.

My room is a sublet that ends in December, and I have not found a new one. With a diploma project that requires long nights in the studio, I cannot spend the autumn searching for housing.

A grant from EduAll would allow me to secure a place to live and complete my degree.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I came to Switzerland in 2015. My asylum application was rejected, but I have provisional admission, because a return to Syria is not possible. My Syrian degree was not recognised, so at forty-two I started again, as a first-year student at EPFL, among students who could be my children.

Outside my studies, I help organise the Syrian community association in Lausanne, where I give free advice to families on housing and repairs.

My diploma project is a modular housing system for reconstruction in Syrian cities, using rubble as building material.

I want to go back one day and rebuild what I saw destroyed.
