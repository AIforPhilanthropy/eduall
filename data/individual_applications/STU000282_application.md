## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Khulna is a city at the edge of the Sundarbans, the mangrove forest. Every year the cyclones come, and every year the salt water goes deeper into the land. My father was a shrimp farmer. When cyclone Aila came in 2009, our ponds were destroyed and my father fell into debt. He died the next year. I was ten, the only son, and soon I had to help support my mother and my three sisters.

My parents never went to school. From fourteen I worked, and I finished my school with evening classes. I worked ten years: in a garment factory, then in a mobile phone repair shop, and later as a technician for an internet company. There I learned programming, alone, from videos.

In Khulna I volunteered with a youth group that taught computer basics to girls in our neighbourhood.

In 2024 I came to Switzerland with my savings and a scholarship. Now I study computer science properly. I want to build software for disaster warnings in the coastal regions of Bangladesh.
