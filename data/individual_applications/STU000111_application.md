## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I came to Basel at twelve to live with my aunt, who had married here. My father could not afford my secondary school in Kenya, and my aunt offered to raise me. I remember arriving at the airport in winter, in sandals, not speaking a word of German. My father cried on the phone every Sunday for a year.

Nobody in my family had ever been to university. My aunt worked as a cleaner, my father as a clerk. At school I was put in the lowest track. My teacher told me I should do an apprenticeship "like the others". I did, as a commercial employee in a bank, and then did the maturité in the evenings. Then I started university, while working.

I chose economics because I want to understand why some countries grow and others do not, why my father worked all his life and never saved anything.

For seven years I have helped coach a youth basketball team in Kleinbasel, for kids from migrant families. Many of them are where I was at twelve. I tell them what nobody told me: the lowest track is not the end.

After my degree, I want to work in development finance. I owe that to my father, who gave me away so I could have a chance, and to my aunt, who took me in.
