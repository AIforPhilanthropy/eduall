## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In 2022, when I was seventeen, a missile hit a building near our house in Kharkiv. I lost most of the hearing in my left ear. For a long time I heard only a ringing.

My parents never went to university; they built their company from one truck. During the war, I volunteered at a humanitarian centre, sorting and delivering food, and later I coordinated a group of fifteen teenagers who helped elderly people in our district. In Zurich I help organise a Ukrainian youth group.

I came to Zurich in 2024 and learned German. Chemistry in German, with one good ear, is hard. I often miss things in lectures. But I do not want to give up.

I want to become a chemist working on water treatment for cities damaged by war.
