## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My parents are both teachers. I became one too. For seven years I taught in a primary school on a small island off Madura, where the boat to the mainland ran twice a week. In 2018 an earthquake destroyed our school. We taught in tents for a year, and I helped rebuild the school with the community.

My master's in Basel focuses on multi-grade teaching. I want to bring what I learn back to the islands.
