## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years, I want to be advising governments in Southeast Asia on labour and industrial policy, especially on how to raise wages without destroying jobs.

I grew up in Nam Dinh, a textile town. My parents worked in a state garment factory for thirty years and never went to university. As a boy I delivered lunch to my mother at the factory gate and watched thousands of women go in and out.

I studied economics in Hanoi and worked eighteen years in a Vietnamese garment company, the last eight as production director, leading 1,200 workers. I negotiated with buyers from Europe and the United States, and I saw how prices set in Paris or New York decided the lives of workers in Nam Dinh.

At forty I decided I wanted the tools to change that system from the outside.
