## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

At this moment my costs are covered. I say this first, so it is clear.

My costs are CHF 2,141 per month: CHF 896 for a room in a shared flat in Zurich, food CHF 418, health insurance CHF 355, and ETH fees CHF 243. The rest is transport, phone and books. My husband works as an electrician in Winterthur and earns about CHF 5,660. He gives CHF 2,390 for my part of the costs. I do not have my own income now, and no savings.

So why I apply? Because the situation of our family changed in spring. My husband's mother in Dnipro was injured when a missile hit her building. She lives now with us in Switzerland, and she needs physiotherapy and help every day. My husband took more hours to pay for her. I stopped my job as a software tester in May to take care of her in the mornings.

We manage, but only just, and I do not know how long my husband can work so much. I am honest that my grades suffered this year. My GPA is 2.42 and I am on academic probation. In a normal year I think I would have much better results; before, my grades were good, and I published two papers from my bachelor work.

EduAll support would make it possible that my husband works less, and that I can use the afternoons to study and bring my grades up.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am from Kryvyi Rih, a city of iron mines. My father worked in the mine for thirty years, my mother in the canteen. Nobody in my family went to university before me.

I was good in mathematics. A teacher prepared me for olympiads, for free, after school. With this I got a place at Dnipro University of Technology. Later I worked four years as a software developer in an IT company in Dnipro. In my free time I organised coding lessons for girls from mining towns, because I know that nobody tells them this career exists.

In 2022 I came to Switzerland. I continue the coding lessons in Zurich, now for Ukrainian girls, online and in person, with two friends.

In 2026 I started my master at ETH. It is very intense. The first semester was good. Then the family emergency came, and I could not concentrate. I had to repeat two exams.

I do not want to stop. My dream is to work on reliable software for medical devices, because in the war I saw how important it is that the machine in the hospital works.

For me education means I am the first one in my family who chose her work, and not only took what was there.
