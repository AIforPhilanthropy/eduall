*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Work first. In 2019 I started as a cleaner in a chemistry lab in Fribourg. I watched the students. I thought: I did this in Gaza. I can do it again.

I studied chemistry two years in Gaza, 2014–2016. Then war damage, closed university, no money. I worked in a pharmacy for two years.

I came to Switzerland in 2018. Asylum procedure, then recognised refugee, now permit B.

Hearing loss in one ear since an explosion in 2014. Second ear weaker too. In lectures I use an FM system. I read the slides before. Labs are loud; I adapt.

2023: started again from first semester. Now four semesters left. Not fast, but finished.

I translate Arabic for a family centre in Fribourg, one evening a week.

Goal: pharmaceutical quality control. Medicine that is safe. In Gaza I saw what fake medicine does.

Education: a second chance I took.
