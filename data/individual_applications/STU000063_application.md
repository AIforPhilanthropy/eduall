## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I am doctoral student in Materials Science at University of Fribourg since September 2025. When I started, my position was paid from a research project of my supervisor for one year. This project finished in July 2026 and the new project proposal is still in evaluation, the answer is expected only in spring 2028. My supervisor wrote a letter which confirm that at this moment there is no salary for me from the department, I attached it to this application. So since September I am practically without funding, but I want to continue because I am in the middle of my experiments.

My monthly costs are CHF 1,925. The biggest part is my rent, CHF 1,009 for a small studio plus CHF 126 for electricity and heating. I looked for a room in shared flat but in Fribourg many landlords don't want to rent to a student without a work contract. Also I pay CHF 70 each month for materials and samples which the lab cannot cover anymore after the project ended.

For income, I have CHF 357 per month as teaching assistant in the practical course for bachelor students (about three hours per week). My two older brothers live in Istanbul and work together in a textile workshop. Together they earn around CHF 760 and they send me CHF 170 each month. They have their own families, so it is really the maximum they can give. My parents are still in Homs and they cannot support me, in the opposite, sometimes they need help.

So I have a gap of CHF 1,398 every month and I have no savings. I applied for two other scholarships (one from the university, one from a private foundation) but the university one is only for bachelor and master students and the other one I did not receive answer yet.

Without support, I will have to stop my doctorate and search full-time work, and I will lose my permit as student. With EduAll grant I can bridge this period until the new project is decided, and continue my research. I will inform you immediately if I receive other funding.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I was born in Homs, Syria in 2002. My father is electrician and my mother is housewife, nobody in my family studied in university before me. During the war our neighbourhood was damaged and we moved two times inside the city. I remember that in many winters we have only few hours of electricity per day. Maybe this is why I became interested in energy and materials, I wanted to understand how we can store energy better and cheaper.

I studied Materials Engineering at Damascus University and after I came to Fribourg in 2023 for master in Chemistry with specialisation in materials. The system is very different from Syria, the exams also, but in research I felt immediately that I am in my place. My master thesis on porous polymer membranes became one article, and one conference paper, both with my supervisor.

Now in my doctorate I work on low-cost materials for sodium-ion batteries. Sodium is much more available than lithium, and I think this can be important for countries like Syria where the energy grid is broken and people need cheap solutions for storage.

Next to my studies I am active in the Syrian association in Fribourg. I help new arrived families with translation of letters from the commune and with registration of children in school. I know how it feels when you receive a letter and you cannot understand it.

For me education is the only way I see to rebuild something, for me and for my country later. I don't want to stop at the middle because of money. I am ready to work hard, I just need some time until the funding situation is clear.
