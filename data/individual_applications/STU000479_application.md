## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in the Balata camp, near Nablus. One square kilometre, more than twenty thousand people. Water came two days a week. When children got sick with diarrhoea, and they did very often, the mothers said it was God's will. The clinic said it was the water tanks.

My parents never went to secondary school. My father worked in construction in Israel when it was possible, and when it was not possible, he did not work. I am the first of seven children to go to university.

In 2014 I came to Switzerland for work through my uncle, who has a bakery in Zurich. I learned baking, and I learned German in evening courses. It took me eight years to be ready for university. Many times I wanted to give up. In 2024 I began the Bachelor of Public Health.

The language is still my biggest problem. I understand the content, but writing an exam of two hours in German is very difficult. I am improving. My last two exams were my best.

I want to work in water and sanitation programmes, maybe with an international organisation, maybe one day in the camps where I grew up.

For me education means that "God's will" can have a better answer.
