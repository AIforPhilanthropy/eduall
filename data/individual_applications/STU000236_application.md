## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In fifteen years, I want to be a historian of rural Peru who has given voice to the communities whose stories were never written down: Quechua-speaking villages, women farmers, land defenders.

My family comes from a village in the Apurímac highlands. My parents are farmers who never went to school. My father became a leader of the community's defence of its land against a mining company. In 2019 he was killed. My mother, my younger brother and I received threats and fled to Lima, and later to Switzerland, where we were recognised as refugees in 2021.

I had just begun my history degree in Lima. In Bern, I finished it while working in a café, and I led the student history association for two years, organising a lecture series on indigenous histories of the Andes. My first article, on oral histories of land conflicts, was published this year.

My thesis reconstructs, from archives and oral testimony, the history of land struggles in Apurímac since the 1960s. It is my father's story, and that of many others.
