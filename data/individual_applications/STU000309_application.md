## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In June 2022, in my first week in Lucerne, I walked across the Kapellbrücke and saw a group of Ukrainian children singing on the bridge, with a hat for money. I cried. Then I went to the refugee centre and asked how I could help.

In Kharkiv I was at home for many years, caring for my grandmother and then for my mother, and I volunteered with a hospice organisation. When the war came, I spent weeks in the metro station with neighbours, organising food and medicine.

Since 2022 I volunteer with the Ukrainian community in Lucerne, helping families with doctors and hospital visits. This made me want to study public health.

German is still hard for me. I study every day, and my grades are good (GPA 3.27).

I want to work in community health for refugees.
