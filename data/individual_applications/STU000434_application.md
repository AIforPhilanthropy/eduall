## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What does a nurse do when the hospital is bombed? You keep working in the basement. I learned this in Homs in 2012, when I was twenty. Our hospital was hit three times. We moved the patients underground and continued by torchlight.

My father taught mathematics and my mother was a midwife. I trained as a nurse in Damascus, worked five years in hospitals under siege, and volunteered in a mobile clinic that reached villages cut off by the fighting. In 2017 I came to Switzerland, learned French, and worked as a nurse in a care home while completing a master's degree.

In Lausanne I help lead a group of Arabic-speaking nurses who support refugee families with health information.

My doctoral research studies how nurses in conflict zones cope with moral distress. I want this knowledge to protect nurses in future crises.
