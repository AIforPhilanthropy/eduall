## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Microbiology is important because the smallest things make the biggest difference. For fifteen years I worked in a public health laboratory in Surabaya, testing water, food and patient samples. During the COVID pandemic, our lab worked day and night. We processed thousands of tests. I learned how much depends on a good laboratory.

My parents are both teachers, and they always encouraged me to learn more.

In 2023 I came to Zurich with my spouse, who found work here. I decided to do the master to learn modern methods in molecular microbiology. My thesis is about bacteria in hospital water systems, and one article with my group is already published.

After my master I want to work in a hospital laboratory in Switzerland, and later perhaps help to improve laboratories in Indonesia.
