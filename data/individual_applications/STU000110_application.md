## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I explain my money situation honestly. My costs are CHF 2,099 per month. I live in a room in a shared flat in Schwamendingen for CHF 862. The tuition at ETH is CHF 243 per month.

I work as a bicycle courier on weekends and earn CHF 593. My cousin in Istanbul, who works in a furniture workshop and whose household earns about CHF 1,720, sends me CHF 320. It is a lot for him.

So I miss CHF 1,186 each month. Until now I closed this gap with savings from my work in Germany, but they are finished since June. I have no savings now.

And there is a new problem. In March, my mother in Syria was badly hurt when part of our old house collapsed; it was damaged in the war and never repaired. She needs surgery and rehabilitation, and my cousin must now send money to her too. He told me he will have to reduce what he sends me.

I finish my bachelor in July 2028. The grant would help me to continue without leaving ETH in the middle.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Materials science is important because everything we build is only as strong as its materials. I understood this in Aleppo in 2013, when I was twelve and saw buildings fall. Some fell completely, others stood with holes. I wanted to know why.

My parents did not go to school, my father was a blacksmith. In his workshop I learned to feel metal, how it bends and breaks. In 2015 my family fled to Germany. I finished school in Hamburg, did an apprenticeship in metal construction, and worked as welder in a shipyard. I learned German there.

In Hamburg I also volunteered with an association that helped new refugees with language and papers. I know how it feels to arrive with nothing.

In 2022 I moved to Zurich for work, and in 2025 I was admitted to ETH. At twenty-four I began my bachelor, after years in workshops, with students who came straight from school. My GPA is 3.45. I am proud of this.

I want to work on materials for affordable and earthquake-resistant housing. Syria will need to be rebuilt, and it must be rebuilt stronger.
