## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Mosul was my city until I was six. Then ISIS came. I remember the black flags and the silence in the streets. My family escaped to Erbil, and in 2022 we came to Switzerland to join my uncle.

My parents did not go to university. My father repaired cars in Mosul. When I was fourteen in Zurich, I started to repair phones and computers for our neighbours, and I worked in a phone shop on weekends to help my family. I also volunteered in a homework club for younger Arabic-speaking children.

I finished school early and started computer science at seventeen. It is hard, but I love programming. I want to build software that helps refugees find information in their language.
