So I miss CHF 440 per month. It is not a very big amount, and I have covered it until now with my savings, but my savings are finished since the summer. I also must say that my grades are not very high, GPA 2.88. I work 6 hours each week and this has a cost on my studies.

My Master in Economics finishes in January 2028. The grant would cover the gap in the last semester and let me work fewer hours during my thesis.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My goal is to work for a development bank, on financing for small businesses in North Africa. In Morocco, most small enterprises never get a bank loan. They grow slowly, or they die. I want to change this, even a little.

I grew up in Meknes. My father sold spices in the medina and my mother did not work outside the house. My father died when I was fifteen, and I became the one who helped my mother with my younger brothers. I worked in the shop after school. Nobody in my family studied before me.

After my bachelor in economics in Fès, I worked seven years in a microcredit association in Meknes, first as loan officer and then as branch assistant. I met hundreds of women and men who had good ideas and no capital. I saw also the dangers of microcredit when it is badly designed.

I came to Neuchâtel in 2025 for the master, because the programme has a good focus on development economics and it is in French. I am writing now my master thesis on the effect of microcredit interest rates on small business survival in Morocco, with data from my old employer.

For me education is the tool to understand why some people succeed and others not, and to make the system more fair.
