## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I will be direct about my finances, including what works in my favour.

My monthly costs are CHF 2,142, including CHF 1,230 for a studio close to the CHUV, where my placements often start at 6:45. I earn CHF 1,147 as a care assistant in a nursing home, working weekends and some evenings. My parents, both teachers in Hebron with a household income of about CHF 2,100, contribute CHF 420. The monthly gap is CHF 575.

I also have savings of CHF 10,357, which I earned during two years as a nursing assistant in Jerusalem, living with my aunt, before coming to Switzerland. I have kept them as a reserve for my first months as a graduate nurse, when my licence and job applications will take time.

I am applying because my final placement, from October, is full-time in an intensive care unit with rotating shifts, and I will have to stop most of my care-home work. The gap will then grow considerably. If the committee concludes that my savings make my need less urgent, I will accept that.

When I was fifteen, my younger sister was hit by a car at a checkpoint. The ambulance was held for forty minutes. She survived, but I will never forget how helpless I felt beside her.

After school I trained as a nursing assistant and worked two years in a hospital in East Jerusalem. In 2024 I came to Lausanne to become a registered nurse. French was hard at first, but I have done well, with a GPA of 3.63.

I want to specialise in emergency and intensive care nursing, and one day to train nurses in Palestine.
