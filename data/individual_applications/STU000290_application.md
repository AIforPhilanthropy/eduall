## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

On 16 December 2014, I was sixteen, and my uncle, a laboratory technician, had taken me to his hospital in Peshawar for the day when the ambulances began to arrive from the Army Public School. I stayed and carried samples for hours. Some of them I had seen in our neighbourhood. I did not sleep for two days. I think that was the day I decided that I wanted to do something with my life that saves people, not only reports on them.

My father taught mathematics for forty years. He taught me that knowledge is a responsibility. After my master's, I became laboratory manager of a private diagnostic centre, leading a team of nine technicians, and introduced molecular testing for tuberculosis.

My doctoral research is on how tuberculosis bacteria survive antibiotic treatment. It is slow, careful work, and I love it.

I want to return to Pakistan one day and build a research laboratory for tuberculosis, which still kills tens of thousands of people there every year.
