## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am forty-one years old, from Gaza, and I study sociology in Neuchâtel.

For almost twenty years, I lived in Jabalia camp and raised my younger siblings after our mother died. I never had a paid job. But I was not idle. In 2010 I founded a women's committee in our block of the camp. We organised reading circles, trauma support groups and a small kindergarten. By 2023, the committee had eighty members. I led it for thirteen years.

In 2023, everything we built was destroyed. The committee's house was bombed. Several of our members were killed. I left Gaza in early 2024 through Egypt, with a scholarship offer from a programme for scholars from Gaza, and I came to Neuchâtel in 2026.

I study how communities organise themselves after catastrophe, and my first research paper, on women's committees in refugee camps, was published this year.

Learning French at forty was very hard. Some days I cannot follow the seminars. But I did not survive what I survived to give up now.

I want to return one day and help rebuild the organisations that kept us alive.
