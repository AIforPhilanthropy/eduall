## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am 29 years old and I come from Multan, in the south of Punjab. My father was headmaster of a government school for 30 years. He taught me that the best thing a person can have is curiosity.

For five years I worked as a laboratory officer at an agricultural research institute in Multan. We worked on cotton, which is the main crop of our region. Every year I saw farmers losing their harvest because of the whitefly and the leaf curl virus. We tried many things, but our research was limited, we had old equipment and little training in molecular methods.

So I decided to learn this properly. I saved for three years and I was admitted to ETH Zurich in February 2026, in Master of Biology with focus on plant sciences. It is a dream but also very difficult. The level of the students is very high and I had to learn a lot of new methods, like CRISPR and bioinformatics, which I never used before.

My master thesis is about the resistance of cotton plants to leaf curl virus. My supervisor is very supportive and says my practical skills in the lab are strong.

After my master I want to go back to Pakistan and work in plant breeding for small farmers. Our farmers need seeds which can resist climate change and disease, and Pakistan needs people who can develop them.

For me education is not for my CV. It is for the farmers of Multan.
