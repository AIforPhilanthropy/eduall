## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Vidin is the poorest region of Bulgaria, maybe of the whole EU. My town has lost half its population since I was born. When I was a child, most of my friends' parents worked abroad, and the children stayed with grandparents. I was one of them. My parents left when I was eight, and my grandmother raised me.

My grandmother kept chickens, and one summer many of them died of a disease. Nobody knew what it was. The vet came from the city once, took samples, and never called back. I was thirteen and I read everything I could find about bird diseases on my phone. That is when I became interested in microbes.

I came to Basel in February 2025 to study microbiology. My parents were very proud: I am the first in our family to study at a university. But the first year was very difficult. My German was only B1 when I arrived, and the lectures were fast. My GPA is 2.7. I know this is not high, but it has improved every semester.

I have not yet had the time or the courage to join clubs or volunteer. I spend my days in lectures and in the library, and my evenings with German exercises.

I want to work in veterinary or food microbiology, maybe in a laboratory that protects animals and people from infectious diseases. I would like one day to go back to north-west Bulgaria, where such knowledge is badly missing.

Education for me means that, one day, my own family will not have to be split between countries to earn a living.
