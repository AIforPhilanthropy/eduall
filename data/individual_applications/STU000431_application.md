## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What makes a material fail? I asked this at fifteen, when a balcony collapsed at a neighbouring hotel in Varna and two tourists were injured. My parents, who never went to university and built their hotel from a small guesthouse, were terrified it could happen to them.

I came to Zurich at eighteen. I work as a laboratory helper on weekends and spent summers working in my parents' hotel, from the kitchen to the reception.

At sixteen I lost my older brother in a car accident. It took my family years to recover, and I think it is why I push myself so hard.

My thesis is on the fatigue of concrete balconies in coastal climates. I want to work on building safety.
