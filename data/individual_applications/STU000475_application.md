## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In March 2025, in my first month at university, a professor in accounting asked who in the room had ever done a real stock inventory. I raised my hand. I was the only one. Everybody else was nineteen or twenty. I was twenty-nine, and I had counted shelves and boxes for ten years.

I grew up in Tangier. My father is a taxi driver and my mother works in a school canteen. After my baccalauréat I started work in the stock room of a clothing factory. In 2019 I came to Switzerland with my wife, who had found work in Neuchâtel. I found work in a supermarket, first in the warehouse and then at the tills and in the shop.

I always wanted to study business, to understand the decisions that were made in the offices above the shop floor. Now I do.

Outside work and study, I have volunteered since 2021 at the food bank in Neuchâtel on Saturday mornings. I also help at the Moroccan cultural association, where I organise the French conversation evenings for new arrivals.

My goal is to work in the management of a social enterprise or a cooperative. I believe business can be done in a way that is fair to the workers.

Education, for me, is how I understand the whole picture, and not only my small part of it.
