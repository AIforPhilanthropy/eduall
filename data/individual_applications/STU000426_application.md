## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Why does the desert move? When I was ten, in a village near Aswan, the sand dunes covered the road to my school for a whole winter. My parents, who are farmers and never went to school, said it was God's will. I wanted to know the physics behind it.

I studied geology in Aswan, and worked four years for a mining company in the Eastern Desert. On weekends I volunteered teaching science to girls in my village.

In 2015 my father died in a road accident while taking vegetables to the market. I became responsible for my younger siblings until they finished school.

I came to Neuchâtel in 2025. My thesis is on groundwater in desert aquifers, and two papers from my earlier work are published. I want to study water resources in the Sahara.
