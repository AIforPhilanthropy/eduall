## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

For four years I worked in a phosphate mine near Taïba, in Senegal, first in the office, then as a sampler in the laboratory. I held the rocks in my hands every day and wanted to know their story: how old they were, how they were formed, why the phosphate was here and not somewhere else.

My parents are teachers, and they always told me to continue studying. But when my father fell ill, I had to work to support my younger brothers. I was nineteen.

In 2020 there was an accident in the mine: a wall of the pit collapsed. Two of my colleagues died. I was fifty metres away. After that, I decided I would understand the ground, not only sample it.

At twenty-three I came to Basel to study geology. It was very hard to start again, and German is still difficult for me. But I passed every year.

My thesis is on the stability of limestone slopes in the Jura.

I want to work in mining safety and geotechnics, in Senegal or in Switzerland.
