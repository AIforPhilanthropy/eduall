## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years, I want to work as a psychologist for women who, like me, arrived in Switzerland through marriage and found themselves alone, without language, without friends, without their own money.

I was born in a village in the Mekong Delta. My parents are rice farmers and never went to school. At twenty-two I married a Vietnamese man living in Switzerland, and I came to Fribourg. For fifteen years I worked in his restaurant, in the kitchen and at the till, without a salary of my own. I learned French slowly, from customers.

Many years later, I realised that I was not happy, and that many women I knew were not either. I started to read psychology books at night. At thirty-nine, I passed the passerelle and began my bachelor. It was the first thing in my life that I did only for myself.

These last months have been the hardest. But psychology has also helped me understand what was happening to me, and to make a decision I was afraid to make for years.

I want to help other women find that courage earlier than I did.
