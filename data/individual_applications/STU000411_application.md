## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My father taught economics at a college in Jaipur for thirty years. He was the one who taught me to read a graph. When he became ill with Parkinson's, I stayed at home for five years to help my mother care for him. He died in 2023. Before he died, he asked me to finish what he had started: to become an economist.

I came to Fribourg in 2025. My thesis is about the economics of care work in India, the work my mother and I did for five years, unpaid and invisible.

I know my CV is short. But I have learned patience and responsibility in a way no job could teach me.
