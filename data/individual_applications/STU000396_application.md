## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was nine, in Karachi, I watched my mother sell vegetables at the market from dawn to dark. One day she collapsed from heatstroke in the summer heat. Nobody knew what to do. I held her head until someone brought water.

My parents never went to school. My father left when I was small. I finished school on a scholarship, and studied biochemistry at the University of Karachi, where I was president of the science students' association and organised health awareness days in schools.

I worked two years as a laboratory technician before coming to Bern. One paper with my professors was published.

My thesis is on heat-stress physiology and public health. After my master I want to go back to Pakistan and work in that field.
