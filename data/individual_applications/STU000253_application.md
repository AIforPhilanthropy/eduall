## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My father was a baker in Mosul. Every morning at four he made bread for the whole street. When ISIS came in 2014, the bakery was closed, and we had to leave the city with nothing. I was seventeen. For two years we lived in a camp for displaced families in Kurdistan, still inside Iraq, and I taught English to the younger children in a tent, as a volunteer.

In 2016 we came to Switzerland, not as asylum seekers but through family reunification with my uncle, who is Swiss citizen and lives here since the 1990s. My parents did not go to school for long, but they want me to study more than anything. I learned German, worked in a warehouse, and did the passerelle.

In St. Gallen I study data science. It is hard, the mathematics and the programming in German, but I like to see patterns in chaos. My thesis is about predicting demand for a supply chain.

After my bachelor I want to work as a data analyst, and maybe one day help my father to open a bakery again, with good data!
