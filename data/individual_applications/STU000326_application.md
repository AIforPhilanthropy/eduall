## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Psychology is important because people suffer in silence. In my family in Iloilo, nobody talked about feelings. My father, a school principal, was very strict. My mother, a teacher, was always tired. When my older brother became depressed, nobody said the word. He left home, and we did not see him for years.

After school I stayed at home for many years to help my parents with my younger siblings and my grandmother. I did not have a job or other activities. I was shy and afraid of the world.

In 2019 I came to Switzerland with my partner. I learned French, and at thirty-seven I started psychology. It is the first thing I do only for myself, and it is very difficult for me. But I want to understand people like my brother, and like me.

I don't have a long list of achievements. I have only this wish to finish.
