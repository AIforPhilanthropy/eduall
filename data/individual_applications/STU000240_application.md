## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

On my first day at EPFL, in the Rolex Learning Center, I saw a group of students from many countries, sitting together on the slopes of the floor, discussing chemistry in five languages. I thought: here, nobody asks where you come from, only what you can do.

It was not always like this for me. I was born in Cebu. My father was a chemistry teacher, my mother a nurse. When I was nine, my father died suddenly of a heart attack. My mother went to work abroad to support us, first in Saudi Arabia, then in Switzerland. For three years I lived with my grandparents, and I saw my mother only on a screen. At twelve I joined her in Lausanne. I did not speak French. I felt very alone.

Chemistry helped me. It was the subject of my father, and in the laboratory I felt close to him. I finished the gymnase, did my bachelor at EPFL, and co-authored my first paper during my master's.

My thesis is about enzymes that can break down plastic. After my master I want to do a PhD and work on environmental chemistry.
