## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Water decides where people can live, and in my hometown the war decided it first. Mykolaiv's piped water network was destroyed by shelling in April 2022; since then the city has used desalinated seawater for everything but drinking. My thesis: low-cost purification units for cities whose water networks have been damaged by conflict.

Background: BSc Civil Engineering in Mykolaiv. One year as a volunteer engineer helping restore small water points in damaged neighbourhoods. One year in a water consultancy.

In Lausanne: member of the Ukrainian student association, organising a yearly water engineering workshop for high-school students from displaced families.

Plan: work on water infrastructure reconstruction in Ukraine.
