## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

I will put all my numbers on the table. My costs are CHF 2,186 per month, including CHF 1,002 rent, CHF 140 utilities and CHF 384 for health insurance, which is high because I am over thirty and must have the standard insurance. My only regular support is CHF 70 from my younger brother, a tailor's assistant in Khulna whose family lives on about CHF 430.

I have CHF 14,388 in savings. I do not want to hide this. It is what remains from my doctoral fellowship from the Bangladesh government, which paid my first two years and ended in June; I saved carefully from it. My supervisor has written to confirm that there is no salaried position for me until a new project starts in 2028.

Without other income, I lose about CHF 2,116 each month. My savings will last about seven months. And this year, a part of them must go home: in April my mother was diagnosed with cervical cancer, and my brother cannot pay for her treatment alone.

My doctorate runs until July 2028. I am applying so that I do not have to choose between my mother's chemotherapy and the last year before my research funding restarts.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, in a village near Khulna, I found a piece of broken glass on the beach after a cyclone. The edges had been smoothed by the sea. I kept it on my windowsill for years and wondered how water could change something so hard. Materials science, for me, began there.

My parents were farmers and could not read. My father died when I was twelve. My mother raised four children alone, working in other people's fields. I studied with scholarships, first at a girls' school, then physics at Khulna University, and then materials engineering in Dhaka.

For six years I worked as a lecturer at a public engineering college, and I founded a women's science club for students from rural areas.

My doctoral research at Fribourg is on low-cost solar materials made from abundant elements.

I want to return to Bangladesh and build a research laboratory in a public university.
