## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What does a biologist do in wartime? In 2022, when I was twenty-two and in the last year of my bachelor's in Kyiv, the answer was: whatever is needed. I coordinated a team of students in our university building that sorted and distributed medical supplies to hospitals in the east. I learned to lead people under pressure.

My parents did not go to university; my father is a factory electrician and my mother trained as a nurse at a medical college. My mother came to Switzerland in 2019 for work. I joined her in 2023, after finishing my bachelor's.

French is still my weakest language. I understand lectures, but writing reports and presenting in French take me much longer than my classmates.

In Lausanne I help organise the Ukrainian students' association, which supports new students with housing and paperwork.

My master's research is on neural regeneration. I co-authored one article from my bachelor's thesis. After my studies, I want to work in regenerative medicine, perhaps one day helping people like my father recover from injuries.
