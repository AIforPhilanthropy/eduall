## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Physics matters because it explains why things break. For more than twenty years I repaired machines in factories, first in Brașov and then in Fribourg. I could fix almost anything, but I never understood the laws behind the metal I held in my hands.

In 2008 our factory in Brașov closed, and I lost my job. For two years we lived on my wife's salary and my odd jobs. We came to Switzerland in 2011.

For many years I have volunteered as a technical helper at a repair café in Fribourg, where people bring broken toasters and bicycles and we fix them together.

At forty-one I began physics. It is harder than any machine, but I love it.
