## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty-three, a historian from Marrakech, and I study the history of women traders in the Saharan caravan routes.

My father taught Arabic literature at university, and my mother was a school director. I studied history in Rabat, and led the students' union of the history faculty for three years.

In 2015 I came to Switzerland with my husband. The years that followed were very difficult: the marriage became violent, and in 2020 I left with nothing but a suitcase. I lived in a women's shelter for six months. Today I hold a C permit in my own name. Slowly, I rebuilt my life. I completed a master's in Zurich and began my doctorate in 2024.

My first article, on women merchants in nineteenth-century Timbuktu, was published last year.

I want to teach and research the history of the Sahara, and to show that women were at the centre of its trade.
