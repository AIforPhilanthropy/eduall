## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My parents were market traders and never went to school. I grew up in Mushin, one of the poorest areas of Lagos. At fifteen my mother died, and I raised my younger brother. I worked as a cleaner in the bank, then as a clerk, and studied accounting in the evenings.

In 2024 I came to Basel. I study data science because I want to make algorithms fair for poor people, who are the most affected and the least heard.
