## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am twenty-five, I will soon be a doctor, and I arrived in Switzerland at fourteen as a refugee.

My parents were active in a Catholic parish community in the Central Highlands of Vietnam that was repeatedly harassed by local authorities. When my father was arrested for the second time in 2015, we fled. After a long journey, we were granted protection in Switzerland. My father, who had been a school teacher, now works as a cook; my mother, a former accountant, cleans offices. They have never complained.

I learned French at fourteen and finished the gymnase with good results. I entered medical school in 2019. I failed the selective first-year exam once and had to repeat it, and later lost a semester when my mother was ill. It has been demanding: the volume of material, the language, and the constant feeling of having to prove that I belong.

My master's thesis examined outcomes of tuberculosis treatment among migrants in Vaud, and led to two co-authored publications.

After graduating I want to specialise in internal medicine and infectious diseases, with a focus on migrant health.
