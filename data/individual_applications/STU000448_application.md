## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

It was in March 2026, in the first week of the semester. The professor gave us a sample of a new polymer and asked what we would test first. I answered in German, slowly, but correct. After the class a classmate asked me where I learned polymer chemistry. I told him: in Kabul, ten years ago. He did not believe me.

I am from Kabul. I studied chemical engineering at Kabul Polytechnic University and then I worked five years as an engineer in a factory for plastic pipes, controlling the quality of material. I was proud of this work. Water pipes are important in a city where many people have no clean water.

After 2021 everything changed and the factory closed. In 2022 I came to Switzerland to join my sister, who lives in Basel since many years. I was 27 and I started from zero: new language, no recognised diploma, no work in my field. I cleaned offices, then I worked in a warehouse. In the evenings I learned German, from A1 to C1 in three years.

I moved five times in these years. Sometimes I slept at my sister's family, sometimes in a room for only one month. But I never stopped to study.

In Basel the university recognised my diploma, and I could start the master. My dream is to work on materials for water infrastructure, pipes that last longer and are cheaper. Maybe one day it helps in Afghanistan, maybe in other places with the same problem.

For me education is the thing nobody could take from me, when everything else was taken.
