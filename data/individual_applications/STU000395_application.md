I want to be honest about my money. My costs are CHF 2,146 per month. My room costs CHF 1,062. I work as a tutor for mathematics, a few hours per week, and earn CHF 352. My cousin in Mashhad, who works in a factory and whose household lives on about CHF 750, sends me CHF 225.

I also must say honestly: I could work more hours, but my master at ETH is very intensive, and I prefer to finish well.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Engineering matters to me because in Herat, most houses are built without any engineer. When the earthquake came in October 2023, many villages were destroyed, and thousands of people died under their houses.

My parents never went to school. My father drove trucks. I studied civil engineering in Kabul, and after 2021 I left for Iran, then came to Switzerland in 2022. My asylum application was rejected, but I was provisionally admitted, because a return to Afghanistan is not considered reasonable.

During my bachelor I worked for an NGO that built schools in villages. I also volunteered as a mathematics teacher for girls who could no longer go to school after 2021, online.

My master's thesis is about earthquake-resistant construction with local materials. I want to go back one day and build houses that stand.
