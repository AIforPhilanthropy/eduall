## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Every evening at six I collect my two children from their after-school care, cook dinner, check their homework and put them to bed. Then, from nine until midnight, I write my doctoral thesis. That is my life in Lausanne, and I love it, but it is held together by very little.

I am a doctoral student in Nursing Science at the University of Lausanne, in my second year. My research on care for older adults with dementia in migrant families had a salaried position for the first year, funded by a grant that ended in February. My supervisor has confirmed that no salary is available before the next funding round in 2028. I now have a small teaching mandate paying CHF 411 a month.

My sister in Surabaya, a nurse, sends me CHF 360 out of a household income of about CHF 1,250. My husband stayed in Indonesia with his elderly parents and sends what he can; our children live with me. My costs are CHF 2,460 a month, including our rent and the children's food and clothing.

The gap is CHF 1,689 every month. I have saved over years of nursing work, and those savings are what keep us going, but they are shrinking fast.

I will not give up. I only ask for help to keep going until the funding returns.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Surabaya is a hot, loud port city of three million, where extended families live together and the old are cared for at home, by daughters. My grandmother lived with us until she died, and I watched my mother wash, feed and turn her for six years. Nobody helped my mother. Nobody even asked if she was tired.

I became a nurse at twenty-one and worked for eighteen years, first in hospitals and later as a nurse educator. In 2018 I earned a master's in nursing in Jakarta while working. In 2025 I received a doctoral position in Lausanne, and I brought my children with me.

My research asks how families from migrant backgrounds in Switzerland care for relatives with dementia, and how nurses can support them. It is my mother's story, told in a new country.

After my doctorate I want to build family caregiver programmes, in Switzerland and in Indonesia.
