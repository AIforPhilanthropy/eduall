## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What does a programmer do when the internet disappears? In Gaza, it happened often. In 2014, during the war, I wrote code on paper, by candlelight, and typed it when the electricity came back for two hours.

My parents never went to school. My father sold vegetables, and my mother raised seven children. I studied computer engineering in Gaza, and then worked three years as a developer and trainer at a tech hub, teaching coding to young women. I led the women's programme there, with forty participants each year. I also volunteered as a mentor for girls in secondary schools.

In 2023 our tech hub was destroyed. I was able to leave Gaza in 2024 with a scholarship offer, and in 2026 I started my master's at ETH.

In Zurich I am active in the Palestinian student community, and I help organise coding workshops for refugee women.

My German is still weak, and outside ETH daily life is hard. But in the lab, I am at home.

I want to build tools for communication in crisis zones, which work even when the internet does not.
