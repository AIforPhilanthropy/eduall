## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was seven, in Ghazni, I saw my older cousin come back from the prison where he was held for two years. He did not speak for months. He sat in the corner of our house and looked at the wall. Nobody knew what to do. People said it was jinn. Later I understood it was trauma, and that it can be treated.

My parents never went to school. In 2018, when I was fifteen, I came to Bern with my uncle's family. Our asylum applications were rejected, but we were provisionally admitted, because a return to Afghanistan was not considered reasonable. I did not speak German. I learned it in one year in the integration class and then went to gymnasium. I studied psychology at the University of Bern from 2021, and already in the bachelor I worked as research assistant; two articles came out of that work, about stress in young migrants.

Since 2019 I volunteer as mentor for newly arrived teenagers from Afghanistan in Bern, and I help to organise football and homework afternoons at a community centre.

German is still difficult in academic writing, and I need more time than my classmates. But I am proud of how far I came.

After my master I want to become a psychotherapist for young refugees. I want no child to sit looking at a wall for months, like my cousin.
