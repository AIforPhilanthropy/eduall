## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Jaipur is a city built of pink sandstone and courtyards, designed in the eighteenth century for a desert climate. Its old houses stay cool without machines: thick walls, shaded courtyards, jali screens that catch the wind. The new buildings around them are concrete boxes with air conditioners on every wall. Walking between the two, as a child, I understood that architecture is a choice.

I studied architecture in Jaipur and worked for six years in a firm that restored heritage buildings and designed low-cost housing. In my free time I helped organise heritage walks for school children in the old city, so that they would see their own streets differently.

In 2026 I came to EPFL to deepen my knowledge of climate-responsive design. French has been the hardest part: studio critiques move fast, and I often understand only half. But I learn every week.

My diploma project adapts traditional passive cooling to affordable housing in Rajasthan.
