## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In summer 2025 I stood for the first time on the Gemmi pass, at 2,300 metres, with a net in my hand and a cold wind in my face. Around me there were hundreds of butterflies, of species I knew only from books. I sat on a stone and I cried a little. Not from sadness. From the feeling that I finally arrived where I wanted to be.

The way to there was long. I grew up in Brăila, in eastern Romania. My father was an engineer at the shipyard and my mother a chemistry teacher, but after the shipyard closed my father started to drink, and our home became a place I did not want to be. At seventeen I left and moved to my aunt in Bucharest. I finished high school while working in a supermarket in the evenings.

I studied biology in Bucharest and worked in parallel all the time: in a pet shop, in a laboratory for water analysis, later as field assistant in a project on the Danube Delta. There I discovered insects, and how they tell us everything about the health of an ecosystem.

After some years of work I decided to do a master abroad. I came to Bern in 2025. My GPA is 3.35. My thesis is about how the butterflies of the Alpine meadows react to the earlier melting of snow.

After my master I want to do a PhD and work in conservation of insects, maybe in Switzerland, maybe back in the Danube Delta.

For me education was always the way out, and now it is the way forward.
