## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What would I have become if I had stayed in the mines? At fifteen I left school in Cerro de Pasco, one of the highest mining towns in the world, and went to work underground, because my father had been injured and my family had no income. For six years I worked in the mine.

After a collapse in which two of my friends died, I left. I worked in Lima as a porter, a driver, and later as a logistics assistant, and finished school in the evenings. In 2016 I came to Switzerland with my wife.

In Lucerne I have worked in warehouses and in logistics for ten years, becoming a supervisor. I also help run a Spanish-speaking parents' and workers' association, organising information evenings about work contracts and rights.

I decided at thirty-three that I wanted to understand how companies are managed, and to help create fairer workplaces.
