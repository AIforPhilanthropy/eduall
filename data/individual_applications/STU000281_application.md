## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother never learned to read, but she kept every rupee of the household in her head. When I was a child in a village in Haryana, she would sit me down each evening and make me add the day's spending. "One day," she told me, "you will count money that is yours."

My parents were farm labourers. At sixteen, my family arranged my marriage. I did not want it, but I had no choice. For three years I lived in my husband's family's house, forbidden to study or work.

At nineteen, with the help of a women's organisation, I left. I came to Switzerland with a scholarship programme for women who had escaped forced marriages, learned German, and passed the university entrance exam.

My studies have been very difficult, especially at the beginning, but I have not given up. I chose economics because I want to understand how women can become financially independent.

After my degree, I want to work for an organisation that supports women's economic independence in South Asia.
