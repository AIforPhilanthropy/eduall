## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. I will tell you my situation as it is. I am forty-five, and I have nine semesters left in the Bachelor in Biochemistry at the University of Bern, which I study part-time. I graduate in January 2031.

My costs are CHF 1,657 a month. I live simply: my share of our flat is CHF 593. I work about 13 hours a week, Saturdays and two evenings, in a Bangladeshi grocery shop and earn CHF 1,463. My wife cleans offices and earns about CHF 3,400; she gives CHF 340 for my part.

Now it is enough. But from September 2027 my laboratory courses are in the afternoons and evenings, and I must give up the evening shifts. I will earn about CHF 1,040, and CHF 277 a month will be missing. It is not huge, but for us every franc counts. My wife works so hard. I want to finish without her carrying more.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In five years I want to be working in a laboratory that develops medicines, and to earn enough that my wife can stop cleaning offices at five in the morning.

I grew up in a village near Sylhet. My parents were farmers who never went to school. I left school at fourteen to work in the rice fields. At twenty I came to Europe, first to Italy, and then to Switzerland in 2008. For fifteen years I worked in restaurant kitchens, washing dishes, then cooking. I learned German slowly, from colleagues and from the radio.

In the evenings I read. First newspapers, then books, then school textbooks. At thirty-nine I passed the passerelle. My teachers could not believe it; neither could I.

Now my GPA is 3.64. German scientific language is still hard for me; I read every text twice. But when I stand in the laboratory with a pipette, I feel that my whole life led here.
