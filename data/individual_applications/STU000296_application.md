## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, in Nablus, my mother grew mint and sage in pots on our balcony. One summer the plants died, one after the other, because the water was bad. My mother said: "Even the plants are tired of the occupation." I wanted to understand why water kills plants.

My parents were shopkeepers and did not go to university. My mother died when I was fifteen. I studied biology at An-Najah University and worked two years as a laboratory technician testing water quality.

In 2025 I came to Basel. My thesis is about soil bacteria that help plants survive drought and salt.

I want to go back to Palestine and help farmers grow food with little and poor water.
