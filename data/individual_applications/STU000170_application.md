## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Let me introduce myself properly. I am forty-four, that I came to Switzerland twenty years ago, and that I spent most of those years cleaning offices at night.

I grew up in Accra. My father was a secondary school teacher and my mother a nurse. I was a good student, but at twenty-two I followed my husband to Zurich, and my studies stopped. The marriage later broke down, and I was alone, with no recognised diploma and little German. I cleaned offices and later worked in supermarkets. For years I thought that was my life.

One night, cleaning a software company's office, I found an old programming book in the recycling bin. I took it home. I started learning Python on a borrowed laptop after my shifts. It took me four years of evening courses to prepare for the university entrance exam.

In 2025, at forty-three, I started computer science at UZH. I am the oldest person in every room. But I am also the one who never gives up.

I want to work as a software developer, and to teach coding to women who, like me, never thought it was for them.
