## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In Arequipa, when I was fourteen, my mother, a nurse, took me to the hospital where she worked. I saw the laboratory, the microscopes, the petri dishes. A technician let me look at bacteria. I was fascinated by these small colonies, which can kill a person or save them.

My father was a teacher. After school I studied laboratory technology and worked two years in a hospital laboratory in Arequipa. I also volunteered in a health brigade that went to villages in the Colca valley, testing water and children for parasites.

In 2023 I came to Zurich to study microbiology. German is still difficult for me, especially in written exams, but I have passed all my courses.

My thesis is about antibiotic resistance in bacteria from rivers. After my bachelor I want to work in a hospital laboratory, and one day to do research on water quality in the Andes.
