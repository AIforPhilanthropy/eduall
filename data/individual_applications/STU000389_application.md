## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was seven, in Herat, my mother sold embroidered scarves from our front door. She could not read, but she knew exactly how much each scarf had cost her in thread and time. She taught me to count with those scarves.

My parents never went to school. In 2015, at fourteen, I left Afghanistan alone and came to Switzerland. I finished school here, then for four years I worked in restaurants and in cleaning, and I volunteered with a refugee organisation, helping new arrivals with translation.

I learned French, passed the admission exam, and in 2025 started business at the University of Lausanne.

I want to help women entrepreneurs, like my mother, to grow their businesses.
