## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Since I was three I walk with a limp. Polio came to our village near Chittagong in 1999, one of the last outbreaks. My parents are farmers, and they were told I would never go to school because I could not walk the four kilometres. My father carried me on his shoulders for two years.

I studied materials engineering in Dhaka and worked seven years in a factory producing ceramic tiles, as a quality engineer. I saw how much energy and material the factory wasted. I started to study how to make it better.

In Bangladesh I was also active in an association of people with disabilities. We fought for ramps in public buildings and for access to universities.

In 2026 I came to EPFL for the Master in Materials Science. It is the best school I could imagine. My thesis is on recycled glass in ceramic materials.

After the master I want to work on sustainable building materials, which is very important for Bangladesh, where construction grows very fast.

For me education is the road my father carried me on.
