## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

On my first day at the University of Zurich, in February 2026, the fire alarm tested at noon, and I froze on the stairs. In Kyiv, a siren means you go underground. Here it meant nothing, and everyone kept walking. I stood there for a full minute. That was the moment I understood how deep war goes into a person, and how much of my research would be about exactly that.

I grew up in Kharkiv. My parents are engineers. I studied sociology in Kyiv and worked for eight years at a research institute, running surveys on internally displaced people. From 2022, I coordinated a team of twelve interviewers across the country, sometimes working from shelters.

I also volunteered for three years with a hotline for displaced families, helping them find housing and documents.

In 2025 I moved to Zurich with my partner. My master's research is about how displaced people rebuild social ties, in Ukraine and among Ukrainians in Switzerland.

I want to contribute to Ukraine's recovery with research that listens to the people who lived through the war.
