## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Hama is a city of great wooden waterwheels, the norias, which have turned on the Orontes for seven hundred years. As a boy, I sat on the riverbank and watched them lift water to the fields. My father, a carpenter who could not read, told me that the norias worked because every piece of wood did its job. "Like laws," he said, "when they are fair."

I studied law in Damascus and worked as a court clerk. In 2013, a shell hit our street. I survived with a spinal injury. I walk with a cane, and long sitting or standing is painful.

In 2015 we came to Switzerland. Our asylum application was rejected, but we were provisionally admitted, because we could not be sent back to Syria. I learned German, had my degree partly recognised, and worked as an interpreter for eight years. I also volunteered with a legal information service for refugees and helped organise the Arabic-speaking parents' association at my children's school.

German legal language is still difficult for me, but my first semester went very well.

I want to become a lawyer in migration and asylum law.
