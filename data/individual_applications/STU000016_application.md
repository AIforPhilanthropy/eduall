## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Korail, one of the biggest informal settlements in Dhaka. My father pulled a rickshaw for thirty years and my mother worked as a domestic helper. Neither of them can read. I was the first person in my family, and one of the first in my lane, to finish secondary school.

What pushed me towards psychology was my brothers. They left for the Gulf when they were very young to support us. When they came home on leave, they were different: quiet, exhausted, sometimes angry. Nobody in our community talked about mental health. I wanted to understand what was happening to them.

With scholarships from an NGO school and later from the university, I studied psychology at the University of Dhaka. I worked as a research assistant in a lab studying the mental health of garment workers, and I co-authored two papers from that project. That is how I got the chance to apply to Basel.

Basel has been harder than I expected. I came in February 2025 with little money and almost no German. I have moved four times. But my grades are good (GPA 3.64), and my supervisor has encouraged me to think about a PhD.

I am also involved in the Bangladeshi community in Basel, helping newly arrived workers and students with forms and appointments.

I want to do research on the mental health of labour migrants, people like my brothers, who carry whole families on their backs and never get asked how they are.
