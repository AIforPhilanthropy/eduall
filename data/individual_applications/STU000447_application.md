At 19 I went to work in the factory quality lab. I tested dyes and fibres. I liked chemistry. No money for university.

At 24 I became head of the lab shift, 8 technicians. I learned to plan, to check, to explain. 

2016: moved to Switzerland with my partner. Learned German. Worked in a laboratory supply warehouse, later as lab assistant in a food testing company.

2023: started biochemistry in Zurich at 33. Hard. Hard to live. Moved three times in the first two years, once to a shelter for two weeks after a landlord ended the lease without notice.

Still here. Four semesters left.

Goal: work in industrial biochemistry, safer dyes for the textile industry. The factory in Nam Định still uses the same chemicals my mother breathed.

Education means: from testing the problem to solving it.
