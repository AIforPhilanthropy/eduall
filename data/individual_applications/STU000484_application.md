I study my master part-time and work about 21 hours a week as a junior developer in a small software company, earning CHF 2,327. My partner works as a software engineer in Neuchâtel and our household income is about CHF 9,500; my partner pays CHF 320 a month towards my costs. So why am I applying? Mainly because I do not want my studies to depend on my partner. We are not married, and if something changed in our relationship I would have only my part-time salary. My scholarship is also only confirmed until December, and from September 2027 I will reduce my hours to about CHF 1,730 to write my thesis. For the rest of my master's, until January 2029, I have nothing secured on my own.

I know this is not the most urgent situation EduAll will see. If the committee thinks my application is less of a priority, I understand. But support for my final year would give me some independence while I finish my thesis.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in a small town in Vaslui county, one of the poorest areas in Romania. My parents worked on a collective farm and later in a canned food factory until it closed. Nobody in my family went to university. When I was twelve, my parents went to work in Italy and I stayed with my grandmother. We spoke on the phone on Sundays.

At fifteen I found an old computer at the town library and taught myself to program from online tutorials. I won a regional informatics competition, and a teacher helped me apply for a scholarship to a high school in Iași.

In 2016 I came to Switzerland, to Neuchâtel, where my aunt lived. I finished my school here, which was hard in French, and did my bachelor in computer science. In 2026 I started the master.

Since 2019 I have volunteered at a coding club for teenagers, and I help at the Romanian community association, mostly with translation and paperwork for new arrivals. Many of them are young people like I was, who grew up with grandparents while their parents worked abroad.

After the master I want to work on software for public administrations, to make paperwork simpler. For me, education was the way out of a town that everyone left.
