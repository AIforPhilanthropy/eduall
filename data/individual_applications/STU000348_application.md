## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am twenty-seven, a Yazidi from Sinjar, and in four months I will be a doctor.

I was fifteen when ISIS attacked Sinjar in 2014. My family escaped to the mountain and then to a camp near Duhok. My parents, farmers who never went to school, lost everything. In the camp, I helped the nurses of a small clinic with translation and registration, and I decided that I would become a doctor.

I came to Switzerland in 2017, where my uncle lives, and applied for asylum. I was not granted refugee status, but I was provisionally admitted, because a return to Sinjar is not considered reasonable. I learned German, completed the maturité and studied medicine in Basel. During my studies I worked in a hospital kitchen and later as a care assistant. I also help organise the Yazidi women's association in Basel, which supports survivors.

After graduating, I want to specialise in gynaecology and work with women who have survived violence.
