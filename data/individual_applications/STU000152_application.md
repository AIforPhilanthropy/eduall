I would like to present my finances honestly, including the parts I am less proud of.

I began the Master's in Computer Science at ETH Zurich in February 2026. My monthly expenses are CHF 1,938, including CHF 851 for a room and CHF 243 for tuition. I earn CHF 398 a month as a tutor for first-year programming. My older brother, a shopkeeper in Mazar-i-Sharif, gives me CHF 90, out of the roughly CHF 530 the household has; my parents in Winterthur have no money to spare. The gap is CHF 1,450 a month. I have no savings.

My first semester did not go as well as I hoped: my GPA is 2.87. I underestimated the level of the master's courses, and I spent too much time on my tutoring job and on my student association. I have adjusted my priorities for the coming semester.

My housing is also insecure. I rent a room from a family who have told me they need it back in December. I have not yet found a replacement I can afford.

An EduAll grant would allow me to find stable housing and to focus on my studies in a way I could not in my first semester.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was seven, in Mazar-i-Sharif, my uncle brought home an old laptop that did not work. I spent weeks taking it apart and putting it back together, until one day the screen lit up. My whole family came to look. It was the first computer in our street.

My parents never went to school. My father worked as a porter at the market. In 2018, when I was fifteen, we joined him in Switzerland after he had been working here for several years. I did not speak German. Within three years I completed the gymnasium and was admitted to ETH.

German is still my weakest point. My master's courses are in English, but administration, housing and many social situations are in German, and I often feel slow and clumsy.

During my bachelor at ETH I worked in a supermarket and later as a tutor. I was elected president of the Afghan students' association in Zurich, and I organise free coding workshops for Afghan girls, online, since girls in Afghanistan can no longer attend secondary school.

I want to work on secure communication tools for people living under authoritarian regimes.
