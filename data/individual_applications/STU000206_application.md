## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother gave birth to me at home in a village near Bogra. There was no doctor, only a neighbour. My mother almost died. She told me this story many times, and every time she ended with: "If there had been a doctor..."

My parents were farmers and could not read. I was the first in my family to finish school. I became a nurse, and for four years I worked in a public hospital in Dhaka, in the maternity ward. I saw thousands of births. I saw women die who should not have died.

I also volunteered for four years with an NGO training village birth attendants in hygiene and emergency signs.

At twenty-seven I decided to study medicine, because I wanted to be able to do more than assist. People told me it was too late. Perhaps. But I am here, and I have passed every year so far.

After my studies I want to specialise in obstetrics and work in maternal health, in Switzerland first, and later in rural Bangladesh.
