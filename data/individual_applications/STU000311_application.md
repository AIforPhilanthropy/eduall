## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Ruse, on the Danube, is a city that once had factories, a port and a university full of chemistry students. When I was growing up, the chemical plant closed, and with it half the jobs in our neighbourhood. My parents, who never went to university, started a small trucking company with one truck. I spent my childhood summers in the cabin with my father, crossing Europe.

When I was sixteen, my father had a serious accident on the road in Romania. For a year the company nearly collapsed, and I helped my mother keep the accounts and schedule the drivers, after school every day. We survived, and my father drives again.

I studied biochemistry in Sofia, where I was elected president of the student chemistry society, and worked part-time in a clinical laboratory.

My doctoral research is on enzymes that break down industrial pollutants. I want to help clean up places like my hometown.
