## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Let me introduce myself: I am twenty-one, from Kumasi, the son of a cloth seller, and the first in my family to go to university.

My father died when I was eleven. My mother raised four children on what she earned at the market, and I helped her every day after school, carrying bolts of kente cloth and counting change. I won a national science competition at sixteen, which brought me a scholarship to a boarding school, and later the chance to apply abroad.

Before coming to Switzerland, I worked for a year as a laboratory assistant in a hospital in Kumasi and volunteered with a youth health club that taught teenagers about malaria prevention. In Lausanne I help organise a weekly football game and meal for young African students, many of whom feel as lost as I did at first.

French is my third language, and EPFL's pace is unforgiving. But I have never been afraid of hard work.

I want to become a researcher in biochemistry and work on affordable diagnostics for diseases like malaria.
