## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother was married at thirteen and widowed at thirty. When my father died, his brothers took our land, and she had no way to fight them. She could not read the documents they made her sign with her thumbprint. I was nine. I remember her crying at night, and saying: "If I had known the law, they could not have done this."

My parents never went to school. I was married at sixteen, and for many years I lived in my husband's family's house without the right to study or work. When my husband died in 2016, the same thing happened to me that had happened to my mother: his family took everything.

This time, with the help of a women's legal aid group, I fought and won. I then spent years in that group, helping other widows understand their rights. In 2021 I came to Switzerland. My asylum application was rejected, but I was provisionally admitted, because as a widow without family protection I would be in serious danger if I were sent back.

I study law to become the lawyer my mother never had.
