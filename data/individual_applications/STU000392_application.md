## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Data science matters to me because data decides who is counted. In 2014, when ISIS came to our town near Sinjar, my family was not on any list. We were Yazidis, farmers who had never gone to school, and no one knew where we had gone. We lived for three years in a camp near Duhok, uncounted.

In the camp I volunteered as a registration assistant for an aid organisation, helping families register for food and medical care. I learned how a missing name can mean a missing meal. Later I worked for that organisation as a data officer, and I organised a youth group that taught English to children in the camp.

In 2019 I came to Switzerland to join my partner, whom I met when they worked as a nurse for a medical team in the camp; I received my permit through family reunification. German has been hard, and it still is. I learned programming at night.

I want to build humanitarian data systems that never leave anyone uncounted.
