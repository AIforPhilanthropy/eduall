## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Duisburg, in the Ruhr valley, where my father worked at a steel plant until it shut down the year I turned eleven. He never found steady work again, and he died in a scaffolding accident on a casual building job when I was seventeen.

My parents never studied beyond school. After my father died, I left school for a while to help my mother, working in a scrapyard sorting metal. I finished school at twenty, late, in evening classes, and studied physics in Bonn, the only subject that had ever made sense to me as a child, lying on our balcony naming the few stars you can see above a steel town.

I worked for three years as a physics teacher and then as a planetarium educator, trying to give kids in towns like mine a sky worth looking at.

My thesis is on exoplanet atmospheres. After my PhD, I want to help build better astronomy and science education in places the economy has left behind, in Germany and beyond.
