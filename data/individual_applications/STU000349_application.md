I began the Bachelor's in Physics at EPFL in September 2025, and I am applying to EduAll to be able to continue with the concentration this demanding programme requires.

My partner, a software engineer in Lausanne with an income of about CHF 8,600, contributes CHF 1,210 to our household and my costs. I work as a tutor for Ukrainian children in mathematics and earn CHF 723. My own costs are CHF 1,419.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Kharkiv is a city of physicists. The institute where Landau worked is there. My parents were factory workers, but my grandmother cleaned the corridors of that institute for thirty years, and she told me stories about the scientists she saw every day. She kept a signed photograph of Landau in her kitchen.

I never had the chance to study physics as a young person. I left school at seventeen to work in a factory, like my parents. For five years I worked in production, and later as a quality inspector. I also volunteered on weekends in a children's shelter, teaching mathematics.

In 2022 my city was bombed. I spent months helping organise food and medicine distribution in my district, before I came to Switzerland.

At twenty-five I started physics at EPFL. It is harder than anything I have done, and I am proud of every exam I pass.

I want to become a physics teacher.
