In December 2025 the foundation informed all scholars that it closes its programme because a main donor withdrew. My father works as a cleaner in an office building and my mother as care assistant; together they earn around CHF 7,300, and they have my two younger brothers at home.

My expenses are CHF 2,029 per month. My rent is CHF 849 for a room I found in a hurry after my shared flat ended in spring, and I will need to move again, because it is only until February. My health insurance is CHF 500. I have type 1 diabetes, and my insulin pump supplies are expensive, even with insurance.

I am missing CHF 492 each month. With EduAll support, I can continue my doctorate without increasing my teaching hours, which already take time from my research.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Chemistry for me is the science of what can be made better. When I was diagnosed with type 1 diabetes at fifteen, I learned that insulin was once extracted from pig pancreas, and today it is made by bacteria, designed by chemists. This changed my life, literally. From that time, I wanted to understand how molecules are designed.

I was born in Tamale, in northern Ghana, and came to Zurich at twelve, when my parents got work here. Nobody in my family went to university. In school in Zurich I had to learn German fast, and then I did the gymnasium, and later I studied chemistry at ETH and at UZH.

During my studies I always worked: in a supermarket, then as laboratory assistant in a company in Dübendorf. I learned there how chemistry is applied in industry.

My doctoral project is about new catalysts for the synthesis of pharmaceuticals with less waste. I have already three publications, two from my master thesis and one from my doctorate. My supervisor is very satisfied with my progress.

After my PhD I want to work in pharmaceutical research, and I hope to contribute to making important medicines cheaper, also for countries like Ghana, where insulin is still very expensive for many people.

For me education is the proof that the child of a cleaner can become a scientist.
