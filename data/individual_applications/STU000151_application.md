*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In Geneva my father works as a dishwasher, my mother as a cleaner. I learned French in the welcome class and finished school early. I started university at seventeen, maybe too early.

Since then I have worked every year, in shops and supermarkets. My results suffered. I know.

I study data science because I want to work with numbers that tell the truth, like my father tried to with words.
