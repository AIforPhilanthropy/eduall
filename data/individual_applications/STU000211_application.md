Their costs are not in my budget, but of course our family's money is one money. We live in a sublet studio for CHF 1,097, which ends in December; after that we have nowhere arranged, and landlords do not like families without a Swiss salary.

I am not in crisis today. But I can see the crisis coming, and I want to be honest about both.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am twenty-five, a geographer from Dakar, a father, and a student again.

I grew up in Rufisque, on the coast just east of Dakar, where the sea has eaten the old cemetery and the fishermen's houses. Every year the beach is narrower. My grandfather, a fisherman, showed me the place where his house stood when he was young. It is under water now.

I studied geography in Dakar and worked three years for the national water agency, mapping coastal erosion and flooding. In my neighbourhood, I helped start a community group that plants filao trees along the beach to hold back the sand. We planted more than two thousand.

In February 2026 I began my master's in environmental science at Lausanne. I want to learn to model coastal change, so that cities like Rufisque can plan instead of just losing.

My daughter was born the year I decided to apply. Every time I doubt myself, I think of her, and of the beach she will inherit.
