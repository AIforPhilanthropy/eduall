## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am 27 years old, from Rajshahi in Bangladesh. I love to make computers do useful things.

My father sells mangoes and my mother makes clothes at home. Nobody in my family studied at university before me. When I got a place at Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology, the whole street came to our house.

After my bachelor, I worked two years as a junior developer for a mobile banking company in Dhaka. I built features for people in villages who have never had a bank account. This is when I understood what software can do.

In 2025 I came to St. Gallen for the master. The first year was not easy with housing. My first room was a sublet that ended after two months, and for some weeks I stayed with a family from my community, sleeping in their living room. Now I have a stable room.

I volunteer with a group that teaches basic computer skills to older migrants in St. Gallen, every Wednesday evening. Many of them want only to video-call their grandchildren. When it works the first time, they are so happy.

After my degree I want to work in fintech, on financial tools for people with low income.

For me, education means that the daughter of a mango seller can write the code that a bank runs on.
