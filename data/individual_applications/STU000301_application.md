## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Education matters because it is the one thing that changed my family's fate. My parents never went to school; my father was a tailor, my mother embroidered clothes at home. My older brother and I were the first to finish secondary school, and we did it by studying under a streetlight near our house in Lahore, because the electricity was cut every evening.

For three years I taught in a low-fee private school in Lahore, and on weekends I volunteered in an adult literacy programme for women in my neighbourhood. I saw grandmothers write their names for the first time.

In 2023 I came to Bern to study education formally, to understand the theory behind what I had done by instinct. My research interest is adult literacy and second-language learning among migrant women.

After my degree, I want to work in adult education, in Switzerland and in Pakistan.
