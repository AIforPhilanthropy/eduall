## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Is it right for me to apply? I asked myself this many times, and decided to be honest and let you judge.

My parents, both engineers in Cluj, earn about CHF 4,130 a month and send me CHF 1,170. My costs are CHF 2,128, so CHF 958 is missing every month. I have no savings; until now my parents have sent extra money every few months, but they cannot do this regularly.

I apply for two reasons: the gap, and my thesis. My master's thesis will be a field study of mountain lakes in the Carpathians and the Alps, and it needs sensors, travel and a summer field season that my department cannot fund. I do not want to ask my parents for more; they are already paying for my brother's medical studies in Cluj.

If there are students who cannot pay their rent, please give it to them first.
