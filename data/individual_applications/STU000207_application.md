## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Can a person think freely in a place where speaking freely is dangerous? This was the question that brought me to philosophy.

I grew up in Minya, in Upper Egypt. My father was a farmer and my mother never went to school. I was the first girl in our extended family to finish secondary school. At university in Cairo, I studied journalism and started writing about women's rights. At twenty-five I was the editor of a small online magazine, leading a team of eight young writers. In 2019, after the magazine published a series on forced marriages, we were threatened, our office was closed, and two of my colleagues were arrested.

I left Egypt in 2020 and came to Switzerland, where I married and slowly rebuilt my life. I learned German, worked in communications, and in 2025 I started studying philosophy.

My thesis is on freedom of expression in the work of Hannah Arendt and contemporary Arab thinkers.

After my degree, I want to do a master's in political philosophy and work on human rights and freedom of expression.
