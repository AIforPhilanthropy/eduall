## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Mariupol was a city of steel and sea. Two huge metallurgical plants, and the smell of the Azov sea. As a child I saw orange smoke over the city every day. Many people, including my grandmother, had lung disease. Nobody talked about it; the plants gave everyone work.

In 2022 the city was almost completely destroyed. I left in March through a humanitarian corridor, after four weeks in a basement. My parents stayed and I did not hear from them for two months. They survived, and now live with relatives in Dnipro.

Before the war, I worked for twelve years as an environmental inspector for the city. I led a team of five people measuring air quality, and I organised a citizens' group that published pollution data online, because official data was often hidden. It was not popular with the plants.

In 2023 I came to Zurich, where my husband found a job. I learned German, and in 2026 I started studying environmental science properly, to get the scientific foundation I never had.

In Zurich I help organise the Ukrainian community's Saturday school, where children keep their language.

I want to work on air quality and industrial pollution, and one day help rebuild Mariupol as a cleaner city.
