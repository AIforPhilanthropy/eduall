## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Hunedoara was built around a steel plant. When I was a child, the plant employed twenty thousand people, including my father. When it closed in the 2000s, the town emptied. My father never found another stable job. My mother worked as a cleaner. Neither of them had studied.

I watched a whole town lose its purpose, and I wanted to understand why. That question led me to business and economics. I studied in Timișoara, worked five years as an analyst in a consulting firm in Bucharest, advising companies in restructuring, and then came to Lausanne for my doctorate.

My research is about how firms and regions adapt when a major employer closes: which workers find new jobs, and which do not. I have published two articles, one on Romanian steel towns and one on retraining programmes.

I want to become a researcher and teacher, and to advise governments on how to protect workers and towns like mine.
