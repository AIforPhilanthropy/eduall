Let me be transparent. My costs are CHF 2,184 a month. ## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, in Saint-Louis, I watched the fishermen of Guet Ndar repair their pirogues on the beach. They knew wood better than any engineer: which planks would bend, which would crack. I asked my father, a fisherman, why some boats lasted twenty years and others only two. He did not know. I wanted to.

My parents never went to school. I was the first in my family to finish secondary school. I came to Zurich at nineteen, not knowing a word of German.

During my first years, I worked as a kitchen assistant in the ETH canteen. I was elected president of the African Students' Association at ETH, and I organised a mentoring programme for new students from Africa.

My thesis is on bio-based composite materials for boat building. I want to help fishermen in West Africa build boats that last longer and cost less.
