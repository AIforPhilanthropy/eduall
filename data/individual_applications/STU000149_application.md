## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

I will say it clearly: I do not have a monthly deficit. My parents send me CHF 610 per month; they are both doctors in Varna, with a combined income of around CHF 3,580. I earn CHF 670 as a physics tutor for gymnasium students, and I receive a small scholarship of CHF 1,322 a year from a Bulgarian science foundation. My costs are CHF 2,275.

The reason I apply is housing. My room in Oerlikon, at CHF 1,264, is a sublet that ends in October. The tenant is coming back from abroad. I have been looking for two months. Zurich is very difficult: I have applied for more than fifty rooms. If I find nothing, I will have to take a short-term furnished room, which costs even more, or live in a hostel for the last months of my bachelor.

I have no savings, because my parents send me what I need each month and I use my tutoring income for materials and some help to my younger brother.

I understand if the committee decides that my need is less urgent. But I wanted to explain that stable housing is my real problem.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was nine, I watched a meteor shower from the roof of my grandmother's house near Varna. My grandmother told me that the stars I saw were already dead, that their light had been travelling for thousands of years. I did not sleep that night. I have wanted to study physics since then.

But my path was not simple. At fifteen I was diagnosed with severe depression after years of bullying at school because I did not fit into what boys or girls were supposed to be. I spent months in hospital. With therapy and the support of my parents, I recovered. The experience taught me how important it is that someone reaches out.

At eighteen I came to Zurich to study physics. I volunteer with a helpline for LGBTQ teenagers, and in my second year I was elected president of the physics students' association. My grades are average, GPA 2.62; physics at UZH is hard, and I spend a lot of time on tutoring and on the association.

After my bachelor I want to do a master in astrophysics.
