## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Saint-Denis, on the northern edge of Paris, in a tower block where the lift had been broken for most of my childhood. My parents, who came from Guadeloupe, never studied beyond school; my father worked security shifts, my mother cleaned offices. We were, officially, French. In practice, my postcode decided most things for me, including which lycée would and would not look at my application.

In 2014, when I was twelve, my father was badly hurt in an assault during a night shift. He has not worked since. From then it was mostly my mother's income, and later mine.

I taught myself to code on library computers, because the school's own computer room was shared between four hundred students. I worked two years as a web developer, remotely, around my last years of school, to help at home.

I left for Geneva in 2024, with a modest scholarship, mostly to be somewhere my address would not be the first thing anyone knew about me.

Goal: secure communication software, the kind that keeps working when the usual channels do not.
