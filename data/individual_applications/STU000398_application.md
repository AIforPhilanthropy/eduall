## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Chittagong is a port city where ships go to die. On the beaches of Sitakunda, men break apart giant ships with their hands. My father was one of them. He lost two fingers when I was nine. He never went to school, and neither did my mother.

I studied history in Dhaka on scholarships, writing about the shipbreaking workers and their families, and I volunteered with an organisation that documents accidents on the shipbreaking yards. I also helped run a reading circle for workers' children.

In Zurich German is still hard for me; my courses are partly in English, but archives and sources often are not.

My research is on the history of labour in South Asian ports. I want to give the workers of Sitakunda a place in history.
