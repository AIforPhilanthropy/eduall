*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Nursing matters to me because nurses were the people who stayed. When my grandmother was dying in the hospital in Lausanne two years ago, the doctors came and went. It was a nurse, Isabelle, who sat with her at night, held her hand, and explained to me, sixteen and terrified, what was happening. I decided then.

I was born in Plovdiv and came to Lausanne at four. My parents are both engineers by training, but their diplomas were not recognised here, so they built their own company from nothing, cleaning offices at night when I was small. From fifteen, I helped them on weekends.

This year I started nursing. At the same time, my family life became very difficult, and I had to leave home. I am not going to go into detail, but I am safe now, and I am determined to continue.

I want to become a nurse in palliative care.
