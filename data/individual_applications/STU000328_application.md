## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The first time I saw snow in Lausanne, in December 2024, I stood outside the Rolex Learning Center and laughed like a child. In Herat it snows rarely. For a moment I forgot everything: the war, my family far away, the exam I had failed that morning.

My father was an engineer and my mother a teacher. In 2021, when the Taliban took Herat, I was working as a data officer for an international NGO, mapping schools and clinics. My name was on lists of people who worked with foreigners. I hid for four months, moving between relatives' houses, before I could leave through Iran.

For four years in Herat I volunteered teaching computer skills to girls, and I helped organise a community library. In Lausanne I help Afghan newcomers with administration and translation.

My thesis is on using satellite data to estimate access to health services in Afghanistan. One paper has already been accepted.

I want to work in humanitarian data science.
