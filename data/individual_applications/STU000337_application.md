## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

For three years I worked as a paramedic in Iași, in eastern Romania. I drove ambulances through villages where the nearest hospital was an hour away. I learned to keep people alive in the back of a moving vehicle, and I learned how much more I wanted to know.

My parents are both teachers, and they taught me that it is never too late to learn. In 2024 I came to Lausanne with my partner and began medicine at twenty-five.

French is still my greatest challenge. Medical French is precise and fast, and I study with recordings every evening. But I love the clinical courses; there, my years in the ambulance are an advantage.

I want to become an emergency physician and, later, to help improve pre-hospital emergency care in rural Romania.
