## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty-two, a nurse for eight years in two countries, and now a master's student in nursing science.

My mother was a midwife in a rural health centre near Sylhet, and my father taught at a primary school. I grew up watching my mother leave in the middle of the night to attend births in villages without electricity. I became a nurse because of her.

I worked for four years in a public hospital in Dhaka, mostly in the neonatal unit. I saw how often babies died from infections that simple hygiene could prevent. In 2022 I came to Switzerland and worked as a care assistant while learning French, before entering the master's programme.

My research focuses on infection prevention in neonatal care in low-resource settings. After my master's, I want to return to Bangladesh and train nurses in neonatal infection control.
