From September 2027 I want to study full-time, so that I finish in July 2028 and not a year later. My thesis needs all my time. I will keep only a few hours at the insurance office, about CHF 790 a month, and I will miss CHF 1,135 a month.

After that, I will work in my field, for the first time in my life. I only need to reach that day.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Every Wednesday afternoon for eight years I sat in a small room of the Arabic women's association in St. Gallen and helped women fill out forms. Tax declarations. Health insurance. School letters. Many of them could not read German, some could not read at all. I became the woman who understands the papers. At first I did it as a volunteer because I was lonely. Then I realised that it was the only place where I felt useful.

I grew up in Casablanca. My father was a docker, my mother a housewife. I was the first in my family to go to university; I studied mathematics and graduated in 2003. Then I married, and my husband did not want me to work. I followed him to Switzerland in 2008. For years I lived inside four walls, and my German stayed very poor.

When the marriage ended, I was thirty-nine. People told me to find a cleaning job. Instead, with the help of the women's association, I took a German course, then a bridge course in statistics, and in 2025 I was admitted to the Master in Data Science at HSG. I was the oldest person in the room, and my German was the worst. But mathematics came back to me like an old friend. My GPA is 3.2.

My thesis uses data from social services to understand why so many migrant women do not claim the benefits they have a right to. I know the answer from the Wednesday afternoons. Now I want to prove it with numbers.

For me, education is freedom. I found it late, but I found it.
