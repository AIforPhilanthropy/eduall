I began the Bachelor's in Geology at the University of Basel in February 2026, in part-time study, and I am applying to EduAll to secure my first years of study on a stable foundation.

I work about 15 hours a week in a garden centre, earning CHF 1,698. My parents, a truck driver and a care assistant in Liestal with a household income of around CHF 8,600, contribute CHF 640 a month. My monthly expenses are CHF 2,184, including CHF 1,183 for a studio close to the university.

An EduAll grant would allow me to take full part in them and complete my degree in July 2031.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The first time I stood on the Rhine bridge in Basel and looked at the river, at fourteen, I had been in Switzerland for three weeks and spoke almost no German. I remember thinking that this river had come from glaciers I had never seen, and that I wanted to see them. Five years later, I study the rocks those glaciers carved.

I grew up in Pernik, a mining town in Bulgaria. Both my parents studied mining engineering, but work was scarce; when I was eleven, my father lost his job at the mine, and we lived on very little for three years before my parents found work in Switzerland.

Arriving at fourteen without German was the hardest thing I have faced. I was placed in a class for newcomers, and I spent my first year mostly silent. By sixteen I had caught up and entered the gymnasium.

Since then I have worked every summer and weekend: first in a supermarket, now in a garden centre. I also help coach a youth football team in Liestal, many of whose players arrived in Switzerland as I did.

I want to become a geologist working on natural hazards in the Alps.
