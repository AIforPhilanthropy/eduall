## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

My financial situation is simple, and not good. My costs in Neuchâtel are CHF 1,864 per month. My younger brother, a teacher in Enugu, gives me CHF 199, out of the roughly CHF 1,060 the household has. I do not have a job at the moment.

I have savings of CHF 6,318, and I want to be honest about them. They are what is left from five years of work in Nigeria. I planned to use them for my whole master, together with a part-time job. But my French is not good enough yet for most jobs here, and after six months of searching I have not found one. At this rate, my savings will be gone in about four months, and my master lasts until January 2028.

I also have to say that my health insurance is expensive: CHF 255 a month. My room costs CHF 791.

I have enrolled in an intensive French course and I continue to apply for jobs. If I find one, I will inform EduAll immediately. But I do not want to be forced to abandon my studies in spring because my savings are finished.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty years old, a husband and a former bank credit officer, and this year I became a student again.

I grew up in Enugu. My father was a school principal and my mother a nurse. They taught me that education is never finished. I studied accounting, and worked five years in banking in Enugu, the last two leading the branch's team of six credit officers.

In 2021, armed men attacked my branch. Two of my colleagues were killed. I survived because I was in the vault room. For a long time after, I could not enter a bank. I resigned in 2023.

I decided to use this break to do something I had always wanted: study business properly, especially how small businesses are financed. In banking, I refused so many loans to good small entrepreneurs, only because the rules did not fit them.

For five years I also volunteered with a church programme teaching financial literacy to market women in Enugu.

My goal is to work in impact investing or microfinance for West African small businesses.
