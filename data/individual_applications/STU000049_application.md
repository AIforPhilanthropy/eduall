## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Goal: become a lawyer specialised in anti-discrimination law and represent Roma people in Europe.

I am Roma, from Sliven in Bulgaria. My parents collected scrap metal. Nobody in my family finished secondary school before me. At school, teachers put Roma children in the back of the class. Many of us left at twelve or thirteen.

I finished school thanks to an NGO scholarship and later worked for that NGO for six years, first as volunteer mentor for Roma pupils, then as a coordinator. I also organised a legal information day each year in our neighbourhood.

In 2020 I came to Switzerland and worked in cleaning and in a warehouse while learning French. I started Law at the University of Lausanne in February 2026. First semester: difficult, GPA 2.58. Legal French is hard. I will improve.

Education means that my name will be on a door where Roma people can come for help.
