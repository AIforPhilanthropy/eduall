## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In March 2024, a month after I started chemistry at the University of Zurich, I stood in the lecture hall at Irchel and could not understand a single sentence of the professor's German. I went home and cried. I was twenty-six. I thought I had made a terrible mistake.

I had come to Zurich in 2018 with a hotel school diploma from Morocco and very little German. My parents, a fisherman and a housewife from Larache, never went to school. For years I worked in hotel kitchens and restaurants, learning the language one shift at a time. I became involved in a Moroccan cultural association, where I organised cooking evenings to raise money for students.

What drew me to chemistry was the kitchen: why dough rises, why sauces break, why some oils burn. I started studying at night, passed the passerelle, and began university.

That first semester nearly defeated me. But I did not give up. I recorded lectures, studied with German dictionaries, and found study partners. Now I have completed 23 courses, with a GPA of 3.08, and next year I will write my thesis on catalysts for greener synthesis.

After my bachelor's, I want to do a master's in food chemistry and work on food safety.
