## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am 30 years old, a geologist, from Quang Ninh in the north of Vietnam. My hometown lives from coal. Every family knows someone who worked in the mines. My father was one of them. He died in a mine collapse when I was eleven. My mother, a primary school teacher, raised my brother and me alone.

After this, I wanted to understand the earth, how it moves, why it breaks. I studied geology in Hanoi and worked seven years for the national geological survey, mapping landslide risk in the northern mountains. In the last two years I was head of a field team of eight people. We made risk maps for more than fifty villages. Two of those villages were later hit by landslides, but the people had moved, because of our maps. This is the proudest thing in my life.

At ETH I want to learn modern methods, like satellite radar and numerical models of slope stability. My GPA is 3.15, and my thesis will be about landslides after typhoons in northern Vietnam.

After the master I want to go back and build a monitoring system for landslides in the mountains. People there have no warning at all.

For me, education is a way to protect people, not only to study rocks.
