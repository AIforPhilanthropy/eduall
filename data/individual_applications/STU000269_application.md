## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Chimbote was once the biggest fishing port in the world. Today it is known for the smell of fishmeal factories and for the barrios built on sand, where families put up houses of reed mats and then, over twenty years, brick by brick, turn them into homes. I grew up in one of those houses. My father built it himself, without drawings, and when the 2007 earthquake came, a wall fell on our kitchen.

My parents never finished school. My father died when I was fifteen. I worked for seven years, first in a fishmeal factory and later as a construction worker and then assistant to a small builder, while finishing school at night.

In my barrio I helped organise a community group that built a playground and a small library with recycled materials. That was my first architecture project.

In 2024 I came to Zurich, learned German, and passed the ETH entrance exam.

I want to design safe, affordable housing for the barrios of Peru, houses that stand when the ground shakes.
