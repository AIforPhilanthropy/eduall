How do you finish a master's degree when your eyes are slowly failing? You plan every hour.

I am in the second year of the Master's in Economics at the University of Zurich. My costs are CHF 2,213 a month. I work as an assistant at a migrant counselling office, earning CHF 576. My cousin in Kano, a teacher, gives me CHF 175, out of the roughly CHF 1,060 the household has.

The gap is CHF 1,462 a month. A grant from EduAll would allow me to finish in July 2028.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What makes a market woman in Kano pay a hundred percent interest on a loan? I asked this question at eleven, watching my mother, who sold groundnuts, borrow from a moneylender every week. She never went to school. Neither did my father, a night guard.

I studied economics in Kano and worked for four years as a loan officer in a microfinance bank. When I was twenty-six, I was diagnosed with glaucoma. I thought my career was finished. Instead, I decided to learn more.

I came to Zurich in 2021. German was a new world for me, and it still is. I help organise a support group for visually impaired migrants in Zurich.

My thesis is on interest rates in informal credit markets in West Africa.
