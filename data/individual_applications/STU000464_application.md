A grant from EduAll would cover this and let me finish my thesis on time.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My long-term goal is simple to say: I want to help hospitals in Pakistan detect antibiotic resistance faster. Today many patients receive the wrong antibiotic for days, because the laboratory needs too much time. I saw this for many years, and I want to change it.

I was born in Karachi. My father was a school teacher and my mother a nurse. I studied microbiology at the University of Karachi and then I worked three years in the laboratory of a large private hospital, where I helped set up the first quality programme.

In 2019 I came to Switzerland, because my wife got a position here as a researcher. I was twenty-seven and I had to start again. My diploma was only partly recognised, so I worked as a laboratory assistant, learned French in the evenings, and prepared for the master.

In 2026 I was admitted. After years in routine work, it is a joy to study again the science behind what I did every day. I passed all four courses of my first semester, and my thesis project will be on rapid tests for resistant bacteria.

For me, education is not only for young people. It is for anyone who still has a question.
