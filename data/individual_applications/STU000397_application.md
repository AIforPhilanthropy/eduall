## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In January 2024, on my first day of French class in Geneva, the teacher asked us to introduce ourselves. I said: "I am forty-three, I am a mechanic, and I want to be a computer scientist." Everyone laughed, kindly. I did too.

My parents were farm workers near Craiova who never went to school. I left school at sixteen to become a car mechanic, and for twenty-two years I repaired engines in Romania and later in Italy. Modern cars became computers on wheels, and I taught myself programming to understand them.

At forty-two I finished a bachelor's in computer science through distance learning, and this year I began the master's in Geneva.

I want to work on software for vehicle diagnostics and safety.
