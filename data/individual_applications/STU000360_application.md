## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The first day I entered the ETH architecture building, I was seventeen. Everyone looked older, more confident, more Swiss. I almost left. Then a professor looked at my portfolio, drawings of the houses of my grandparents' village, and said: "This is architecture." I stayed.

I came to Zurich at eleven. My parents never went to university; they came here to work, and they worked very hard so that I could study. German was very difficult for me at first, and still in written exams I am slower than others.

In 2013, typhoon Haiyan destroyed the village of my grandparents. I was eight, and I watched it on television. Houses made of wood and metal sheets flew like paper. This is why I study architecture.

On weekends I help at the Filipino community centre, teaching children to draw and build models. I also work part-time in an architecture office.

My diploma project is a typhoon-resistant house made of bamboo and local materials.
