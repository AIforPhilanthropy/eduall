## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am 19 years old, from Plovdiv in Bulgaria, and I want to become an engineer who builds bridges and tunnels.

At my school, a mathematics high school, I was president of the robotics club. We built a robot that could climb stairs, and we got second place at the national competition. I organised the team and the sponsors, and I learned that engineering is also about people, not only numbers.

In the summers of 2023 and 2024 I worked for a construction company in Plovdiv, first carrying materials and then helping the site engineer with measurements. It was hot and hard, but I saw a bridge grow from nothing.

I came to Bern in February 2026. The first weeks were difficult. My room was not ready when I arrived, and for one month I changed between three different places, a hostel, a friend's sofa and a short sublet. Now I live in a student house and it is stable.

The biggest challenge is German. I learned it at school, but lectures in engineering are fast and full of new words. I made my own dictionary with more than 800 technical words. After the first semester I passed all five courses.

For me education is a bridge: from Plovdiv to Bern, and later back, with something to build.
