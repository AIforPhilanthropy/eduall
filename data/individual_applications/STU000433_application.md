## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Let me introduce myself: I am twenty, from Saint-Louis in Senegal, a geology student, and the first person in my family to go to university.

My mother left Senegal when I was four to work in Switzerland. I stayed with my grandmother until I was ten, then joined my mother in Neuchâtel. I did not speak the French of Switzerland, and I did not know my mother. It took years to become a family again.

My grandmother's house in Saint-Louis was destroyed by the sea in 2018. That is why I study geology: to understand the coast that is disappearing.

I volunteer with a group that cleans the shores of Lake Neuchâtel every month.
