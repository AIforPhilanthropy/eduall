## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

I want to explain my situation honestly. My costs are CHF 2,280 per month. My room in a shared flat costs CHF 1,291, plus CHF 117 utilities. I know this is expensive. When I started my studies, I could not stay in my parents' flat any more, because it is too small for six people and I needed quiet to study. This was the only room I found near the hospital school.

My father, who works as a cook in a restaurant in Lausanne and earns with my mother about CHF 6,000, gives me CHF 992. I work some hours at a supermarket cash desk and earn CHF 284. So every month CHF 1,004 is missing.

I have no savings. Until now my parents gave more than they could, and I borrowed from my aunt. I also must say that I could work more hours, but nursing studies have many practical placements with changing schedules.

I will finish in July 2028.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Nursing is important to me because of my grandmother. When we lived in Erbil, she was sick for a long time with diabetes, and I helped her every day with her injections. I was ten. The nurse from the clinic showed me how to do it. She said I have "good hands". I never forgot.

We came to Switzerland in 2020, when I was thirteen. Nobody in my family went to university. My father was a baker in Erbil; my mother was at home. I learned French very fast, and I finished school two years early.

I help in the Kurdish community in Lausanne as an interpreter for older women at the doctor. I also work at a supermarket.

After my studies I want to work in diabetes care, and to help families from other cultures to understand the disease.
