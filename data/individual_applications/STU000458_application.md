## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Age seven, Arequipa. My mother held a newspaper very close to my face. "Can you read this?" I could not. That day we learned I had a degenerative eye condition. Low vision since then, getting slowly worse.

Parents: father drives a minibus, mother sells clothes in the market. No university in the family before me.

School: large-print books, a magnifier, a lot of patience from teachers. I discovered that numbers are easier than words when your eyes are tired. Maths became my language.

2020–2024: BSc Statistics, Universidad Nacional de San Agustín. Best of my year.

2024–2025: Data analyst, a health insurance company in Lima. I built dashboards with screen magnifiers and voice output. It worked.

2026: ETH. First semester: two courses passed.

This year my father had a road accident. He cannot drive for months. My mother now carries the family alone, and still sends me money.

Goal: accessible data tools. Charts and dashboards that work for people who cannot see them well.

Education: proof that the tools can be adapted to the person.
