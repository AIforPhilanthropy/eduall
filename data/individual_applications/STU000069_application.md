## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The first time I entered the EPFL Rolex Learning Center, in September 2025, I sat on one of the slopes with my laptop and did not open it for an hour. I was forty. Twenty years earlier, I had been admitted to engineering in Baghdad. I finally felt that I was continuing that story.

After my bachelor's in Baghdad, my life went in another direction. For many years I cared for my younger siblings and our household after our parents became ill, and later I lived in a family situation where work outside the home was not possible for me. During those years I was elected head of the parents' committee of my nephews' school and led a fundraising for a new science lab. It is the only "job" I had, and I did it with my whole heart.

In 2023 my brother helped me come to Switzerland. I learned French from zero. My courses are in English, but daily life, and many projects, are in French, and it is still difficult.

My GPA is 3.57. I published one co-authored paper from a semester project on heat exchangers. My thesis is about cooling systems for buildings in hot climates.

Baghdad is one of the hottest cities in the world, with almost no reliable electricity in summer. I want to design low-energy cooling for such cities.
