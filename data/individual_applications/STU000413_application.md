I will be fully candid about my finances, which are worse than they appear on paper.

I am in the first year of the Master's in Physics at the University of Bern. My monthly costs are CHF 2,434, including CHF 1,127 for a room and CHF 445 for health insurance. My only regular support is CHF 80 a month from my uncle in Kabul, a shopkeeper whose household earns about CHF 270.

I have no work history, and I do not currently work. I have a hearing impairment and use hearing aids, which makes many student jobs difficult for me. I realise my savings may make my situation look less urgent than it is; I wanted you to see the full picture.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My father taught physics at Kabul University for twenty-five years. When I was a child, he explained the stars to me on the roof of our house. My mother was a teacher.

In 2017, a bomb exploded near the university library where I was studying. I survived, but I lost most of my hearing. My father told me: "Physics does not need ears."

After 2021, my father lost his position and fell ill. He died in 2024. My younger brother, who stayed in Kabul, was arrested in March this year and is still detained. My mother depends on my uncle.

I left Afghanistan in 2023 with a scholarship. I study theoretical physics, which needs pen, paper and silence. I have all three.
