## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years I want to work for an organisation that helps young women in Egypt and the Arab world whose education stops when family duties begin. I know this road because I almost stayed on it.

My parents never went to university. My father repaired shoes in the market of Tanta, and my mother sewed clothes at home. When my mother died, I was fourteen. As the only daughter, I took care of my father and my younger brothers for six years, and I finished school at night. I kept the house, and I read at night: novels, history, sociology, everything I could find in the small library of our street.

When my youngest brother finished school, I finally had time for myself. At twenty, I decided to study. My brother in Kuwait helped me to apply, and I was admitted to Geneva.

My first semester was a big challenge, but I passed all my exams, with a GPA of 3.07. I feel like a new person.

Sociology helps me to understand my own life, and the lives of millions of women like me, whose talents stay inside the house. I want to change this.
