## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Education is the only thing that can break a circle of violence. I learned this in Baghdad, where I was a primary school teacher for twelve years. Many of my pupils had lost fathers or brothers. Some came to school with anger that nobody could reach. I had no training for this.

My parents were illiterate; my father was a street vendor. I was the first in my family to finish university. In 2020 I came to Geneva with my family. Our asylum application was rejected, but we were provisionally admitted, because sending us back to Baghdad was judged too dangerous.

In Geneva, I volunteer in an association that helps refugee children with homework, and since 2022 I work as a mediator between schools and Arabic-speaking families.

My thesis is about how teachers can support traumatised children in class. After my master, I want to train teachers, in Switzerland and in Iraq, to understand trauma and to stay with the child.
