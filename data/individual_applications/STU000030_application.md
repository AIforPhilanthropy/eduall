## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Medan is a big city in North Sumatra, hot and noisy, with many rivers that are very dirty. When I was a child, every rainy season my neighbours' children got diarrhoea, and some were very sick. My mother was a nurse in the public hospital. She came home and told us to always boil the water, always. I did not understand then why the water was dangerous when it looked clean.

After school I worked two years as assistant in a laboratory for water quality of the provincial health office in Medan. I tested water from wells, rivers and hospitals. It was good work, but I always wanted to understand more, not only to test.

When my husband got a job in Switzerland in 2021, I came with him and decided to use the chance. I learned German for two years and at twenty-two I started at ETH. People told me it was too difficult. Maybe. But I passed every exam until now, and my GPA is 3.37. I think my experience in the laboratory helps me very much, and I enjoy to help other students in the practical courses.

In my free time I volunteer at the Indonesian church community in Zurich, where I teach Indonesian to children of mixed families on Saturday afternoons.

My plan after the bachelor is a master in microbiology with focus on environmental microbes and water. Then I want to work for water safety, maybe in Switzerland or in Indonesia. Many children in Medan still drink water that is not safe.

For me education came after a detour, but it came, and I am very thankful.
