## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

My income now is a little more than my costs. I want to explain why I still apply.

My costs are CHF 2,176 a month. Rent is CHF 1,050 for a room in Neuchâtel, utilities CHF 126, food CHF 317, fees CHF 173, and the rest is health insurance, transport, phone and materials. I work as a nanny for a family in Peseux, and I earn CHF 623. My aunt, who lives in Geneva with her husband, gives me CHF 1,507 from their household income of around CHF 5,000. I have CHF 728 saved.

The problem is that my aunt is not able to continue like this. In July my mother in Iloilo was diagnosed with breast cancer. Treatment in the Philippines is paid by the family, and my aunt is her sister. From October she will send most of her help to my mother's treatment instead of to me. She told me she can give maybe half.

I will try to work more hours with the family where I am nanny, but the bachelor in education has school practice (stages) and lectures, and the family needs me in the afternoons, exactly when I have my classes. My savings are small and would last one or two months.

I am honest that my grades are not high (GPA 2.59). The first year in French was very hard for me. I am improving and I passed all my courses.

A grant from EduAll would help me to stay in the programme while my family pays for my mother.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Iloilo, in the Philippines. My mother sells fish in the market, my father is a tricycle driver. They did not study at university. I am the eldest of four children.

After high school I went to Switzerland as an au pair, at seventeen, for a family in Neuchâtel. I took care of three children, and I discovered I love to teach. I helped the children with homework in French, and I learned French at the same time as them.

After the au pair year I stayed as a nanny and prepared the university entrance. In 2025 I started the Bachelor in Education. I want to become a primary school teacher. It is a big thing for my family. When I told my mother I was admitted at the university, she cried in the market.

The first semesters were not easy. I read every text two or three times. But I like the practical part very much, in the classroom I feel at home.

Now my mother is sick, and I am far. I call her every day. She tells me to not come home, to finish the studies. I will do it for her.

For me education means to give children the same chance that the Swiss family gave to me.
