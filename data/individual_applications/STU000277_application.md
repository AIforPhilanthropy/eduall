## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Biochemistry is important for me because my own body is a biochemistry problem. When I was twenty, I was diagnosed with lupus, an autoimmune disease where the immune system attacks the body. The doctors explained it with molecules and antibodies, and I wanted to understand every word.

Because of my illness, my studies took many years. I finished my bachelor in biochemistry in Cebu, with long interruptions, and I could not keep a job because of my flares. My parents, both teachers, supported me through long years at home between my studies and the flares.

In 2026 I came to Geneva for the master. My thesis is about proteins involved in autoimmune inflammation, and one article with my supervisor is already published.

After my master I want to do a PhD in immunology. I want to help find better treatments for autoimmune diseases, which affect many young women and are often diagnosed too late in the Philippines.
