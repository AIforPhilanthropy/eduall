## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Can the law protect people who live where the state does not arrive? I asked this question first when I was sixteen, in 2017, in my neighbourhood in Lima, when a flood destroyed forty houses and the authorities came only after one week.

I studied law in Lima with a scholarship, and during my studies I worked as a paralegal in a legal aid office. I was the coordinator of the student volunteer group of the legal clinic, forty students who gave free legal advice in the poor neighbourhoods.

My doctorate is about the legal protection of informal settlements against climate disasters.

My father's illness this year was very hard. I went home in March for three weeks. I did not want to come back, but he said: "Go. Finish."
