## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I will be honest about my budget. My parents send me CHF 290 per month and I earn CHF 885 working in a bike repair shop in Kreis 5, about 9 hours per week. My expenses are CHF 2,087, so every month CHF 912 is missing. I cover it from my savings, CHF 2,667, which I brought from Vietnam.

But this situation will change very soon, and this is why I apply. In April my father had a stroke. He was civil engineer in Can Tho and his salary was the main income of my family, together with my mother who is a teacher. Before, they earned together about CHF 1,720 per month. Now my father cannot work anymore, maybe never. My mother told me that from January she can only send me a small part of what she sends now, because the hospital and the rehabilitation cost a lot, and in Vietnam the insurance pays not much.

I calculated: without the money from my parents I will miss more than one thousand francs every month. My savings will be finished before the end of the year. I can increase my work hours a little, but not so much, because my permit allows only 15 hours in semester and I need time for my study.

I still have four semesters until July 2028. I don't have any other scholarship. With the EduAll support I could continue my bachelor normally and not go back to Vietnam in the middle.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In April this year I was standing in the Zurich main station when my sister called me. She was crying so much that I did not understand her first. My father was in hospital. I remember I looked at the big clock in the station and I thought: I am 10,000 km away and I cannot do anything.

I flew home for two weeks. My father could not speak well, but he asked me about my exams. This is typical for him. He always said that I must study the environment because Mekong Delta is sinking and salt water comes into the rice fields, and our generation must solve it.

I grew up in Can Tho. Every rainy season our street was flooded, and every dry season the water from the tap was a bit salty. When I was 15, my family's rice land in the countryside was lost because of salt intrusion. My grandfather had to sell it. This is why I chose Environmental Science.

In Zurich I study for two semesters now. My grades are medium, GPA 2.9. The first year was very difficult with German and with the system of exams, and honestly I also worked too much. Now I understand better how to study here.

Besides study and work, I help in the Vietnamese community in Zurich. We organise Tet festival and I help new students from Vietnam with apartments and with the insurance, which is very complicated.

My goal is to do a master in water management and then go back to the Mekong Delta to work on adaptation projects. I don't want to stop now, when my father is sick and my family needs me to finish more than before.
