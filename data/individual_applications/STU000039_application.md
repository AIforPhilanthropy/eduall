I know that EduAll cannot pay for my family. I am asking for help with my own study and living costs, so that I can keep studying at this pace until my wife can start working again next year.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My grandfather had a pomegranate orchard outside Kandahar. When I was a boy, I spent every summer there. He showed me how to graft branches, and which insects were good and which were bad for the trees. I asked him why some trees gave fruit and others did not. He said, "Ask the soil." I think biology started there for me.

My parents never went to school. My father was a mechanic and my mother raised eight children. I finished school in Kandahar and worked as a laboratory assistant in a small agricultural office, testing soil and seeds for farmers. When the security situation became impossible, I left. After some difficult years, I came to Switzerland in 2019 to join my wife, who has lived in Basel since she was a child.

For five years I worked in a warehouse, full-time, and learned German in the evenings. I also volunteered as an interpreter for Afghan families at a migrant counselling office. In 2025, at thirty-one, I passed the university admission exam and started Biology at the University of Basel.

My first year went very well. I have a GPA of 3.66 and an average of 95.8. I love the laboratory work. I want to specialise in plant biology and soil science.

My dream is to help Afghan farmers one day, maybe through an international organisation. Afghanistan has good soil and hard-working people, but very little knowledge support.

For me education is the promise I made to my grandfather's trees.
