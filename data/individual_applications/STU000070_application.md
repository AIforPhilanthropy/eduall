## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Architecture is important because it decides how people live every day, even people who never meet an architect. In Cebu, most families in my barangay built their own houses, with what they had. When typhoons came, the houses fell. Nobody had designed them to stand.

My parents are fishermen and never studied beyond elementary school. I was the first in my family to go to college. I studied drafting technology and worked for four years as draftsperson, first in Manila and then in Dubai, drawing towers and malls. In my last year in Dubai I led a small team of three draftspersons. I learned a lot, but I was always drawing buildings for rich people.

In my free time in Dubai, I volunteered with a Filipino group that helped domestic workers who escaped abusive employers, giving them shelter and legal information.

At twenty-four I decided to study architecture for real. I was admitted to EPFL in 2024. My GPA is 3.31. My diploma project is typhoon-resistant low-cost housing for coastal barangays, using bamboo and local materials.

After graduation I want to work on disaster-resilient housing in the Philippines. I want the next typhoon to find houses that stand.
