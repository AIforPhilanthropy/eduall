## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in a village in Moldavia, in eastern Romania. My parents worked on a state farm, and later on their own small piece of land. Nobody in my family studied. When I was fifteen, my father went to work in Italy, and my mother and I stayed with my two younger brothers. I was the one who organised the household, the money, the papers.

I studied chemistry in Iași, and afterwards worked in a laboratory for water quality. During my studies and after, I led a volunteer group that tested wells in villages for nitrates, because many children in our region were sick from the water. We tested more than 300 wells, and the county started a programme to fix them.

In 2012 I came to Switzerland with my husband. My diploma was not fully recognised, so I worked in a factory and later as a laboratory assistant. I learned German. I also became active in the Romanian community in Bern: I organise Romanian lessons for children on Saturdays.

In 2025 I started the Master in Chemistry. My thesis is about pesticide residues in drinking water. It is the same question as in my village, but with better tools.

I want to work for a cantonal laboratory or a water agency. Clean water should not depend on where you are born.
