## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In March 2022, our apartment building in Chernihiv was hit during the siege. My husband and I spent five weeks in the basement with our neighbours. I organised water and food distribution for the forty people there, and I kept a list of every family's documents, because I knew that without them, nobody could prove what they had lost.

After we left, I helped Ukrainian refugees in Switzerland recover lost property documents and understand their rights, as an active member of a community centre in Neuchâtel.

My master's thesis will examine the legal protection of property rights of displaced persons. After graduating, I want to work on property restitution for Ukrainian families after the war.
