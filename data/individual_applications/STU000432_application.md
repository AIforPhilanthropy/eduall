## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The first time I entered the Bern historical museum, I stopped in front of an embroidered blouse. It had been bought in a village in Maramureș in 1910 by a Swiss collector. I recognised the pattern: my grandmother embroidered the same one. I stood there for an hour.

My parents are teachers in Cluj-Napoca. I studied history there and, for five years, I was president of an association preserving traditional Romanian textile patterns, helping village embroiderers document their motifs and sell their work fairly. We recorded the meanings of more than two hundred designs from the ie blouse tradition.

In 2023 I came to Bern. German has been the hardest part: every archive, every seminar. I read slowly, but I read everything.

My thesis is about Romanian folk textiles in European museum collections, and how they arrived there.
