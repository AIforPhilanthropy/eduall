## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I am in the first year of my Master in Educational Sciences at the University of Bern and will finish in January 2028.

My monthly costs are CHF 2,244. My room in a shared flat costs CHF 1,158 plus CHF 103 for utilities. My parents, who run a bakery in Arequipa and earn around CHF 1,990 a month together, send me CHF 500. They work very hard for it: they start at four in the morning, seven days a week.

I do not have a job at the moment. My programme includes a research internship at a school, and I spend the rest of my time on my thesis.

This leaves CHF 1,744 missing every month. A grant from EduAll would cover this and let my parents keep a bit more of what they earn with so much effort.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Every Saturday during my bachelor in Arequipa, I took a bus up to a village on the slopes of the Misti volcano, where I taught reading to children whose parents worked in the quarries. There was no library, so I brought books in a backpack. Some children had never held a book of their own. By the end of the first semester, the backpack was not big enough.

My parents never went to university. My father left school at fourteen to learn baking, and my mother joined him. Their bakery is small, but they made sure I went to the best school they could afford. When I got a place at university, my father closed the bakery for one day to celebrate. He had never done that before.

I finished my bachelor in education at twenty-one and came to Bern for my master's. I study how reading is taught to children from low-literacy families. I compare Swiss and Peruvian programmes in my thesis. My results have been very good so far, with a GPA of 3.76.

After my master's, I want to go back to Peru and design reading programmes for rural schools. Every child should have a backpack full of books.
