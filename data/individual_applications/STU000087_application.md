## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I came to Lausanne at fourteen from Kumasi, Ghana, to join my mother, who had been working here for six years. I spoke English and Twi, no French. Nobody in my family had been to university.

School in Switzerland was hard. I was put in the lowest track. Teachers were kind but did not expect much. At seventeen I started an apprenticeship in logistics. I finished it, and then I did the passerelle to go to university, while working.

I chose psychology because of what I saw in my community: young men from Africa who arrive alone, who have no one to talk to, who end up in trouble. I help run a football team for young African migrants in Renens, where we also organise talks with social workers.

Studying psychology in French is my biggest challenge. I understand the lectures, but writing essays and exams in academic French takes me much longer than others.

I want to become a psychologist working with young migrants.
