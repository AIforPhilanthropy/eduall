## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

My monthly costs are CHF 2,805, and my monthly resources are CHF 1,416: a gap of CHF 1,389. I want to be open about what that small number hides.

I began the Master's in Chemistry at the University of Zurich in February 2026. My rent is CHF 1,409 plus CHF 63 for utilities for an accessible studio near the university; because of my mobility impairment, I cannot live in the cheaper shared flats on upper floors without lifts. I earn CHF 556 as a teaching assistant. My two older brothers, both seafarers working on cargo ships, send me CHF 860 together; their households earn around CHF 4,290.

Their contracts are for nine months at a time, and between contracts they earn nothing. Last year, for four months, I received almost nothing and borrowed from friends. I have no savings.

I am applying so that I can build a small buffer for those months, and so that my brothers' families do not carry my studies alone.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I was born in Yogyakarta with a clubfoot that was never properly treated, because my parents, a fisherman and a vendor who never went to school, could not afford surgery. I walk with a brace and cannot stand for long.

At school, I was the child who sat in the classroom during sports. I read instead. I discovered chemistry at fifteen, and I have loved it ever since.

I studied chemistry in Jakarta on a scholarship and worked four years in a pharmaceutical quality laboratory, where I became team leader of five analysts. I also co-founded a support group for young people with disabilities in Yogyakarta, which campaigns for accessible schools.

In Zurich I want to specialise in medicinal chemistry. One day I want to work on affordable medicines for Indonesia.
