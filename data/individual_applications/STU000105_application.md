## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

How does gold change a village? I have asked this question all my life. I was born in Kédougou, in the south-east of Senegal, where gold mining is everywhere. When I was small, my uncles dug in artisanal mines with their hands. Some died in collapses. The rivers became brown and the fish disappeared. The money came fast and went fast.

My father was a school teacher, and he pushed me to study. I studied geology in Dakar and worked six years for the national geological service, mapping mineral deposits, and later advising on the safety of artisanal mines. I published one article about mercury pollution near mines.

My life was not easy. In 2020 my husband died in a road accident, and I kept our household alone, with my mother, while continuing to work. It took me a long time to feel strong again.

At thirty I decided to study again, to learn modern methods of environmental geology. In Zurich everything is in German and English, and my German is still very weak; the first semester was hard, but I passed (GPA 3.18).

I want to go back to Kédougou and help to make mining safer and cleaner for the people who live there.
