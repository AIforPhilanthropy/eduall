I want to be honest that with this scholarship my real gap is smaller than the CHF 1,567 in the form. But even together it is far from what the migration office requires.

My costs are CHF 1,737 per month. I live in a room in a student house, CHF 678. I have no savings.

I have never had a paid job, not in Pakistan and not here. I studied, and I helped my family in their shop without salary. I know this makes my situation weaker.

My master finishes in July 2028. If EduAll gives me a grant, I can show it to the migration office as proof of means, and I can finish my studies legally and in peace.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In May 2025, I stood in a laboratory at the University of Zurich and saw a protein structure on the screen that I had purified myself, after six months of failed attempts. My supervisor shook my hand. I went outside, sat on a bench at the Irchel park and called my mother. She did not understand what a protein is, but she understood that I was happy.

I come from Faisalabad. My parents never went to school. My father sold cloth in the bazaar, and after he died in 2010, my uncle took care of our family. I was the only one of five children who finished college. I studied biochemistry at the University of Agriculture in Faisalabad, and then a master's in Lahore.

For four years I could not continue because the family needed me in the shop. But in the evenings I taught free chemistry classes to boys and girls in our mohalla who could not pay for tuition. I also organised blood donation camps with a youth group.

In 2024, at twenty-eight, I got the chance to come to Zurich. German is still very difficult for me. My courses are in English, but everything outside, the migration office, the landlord, the health insurance, is in German, and I often misunderstand letters.

My research is on enzymes that break down plastic. I want to go back to Pakistan one day and work on the plastic waste problem, which is terrible in our cities.

For me, education is the thing my father could not have, and that he wanted most for us.
