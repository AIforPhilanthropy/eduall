## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty, a former senior accountant, and a second-year mathematics student at ETH Zurich.

I grew up in Hanoi. My parents were engineers and wanted me to become one too. I chose accounting because it was a secure profession, and for four years I worked in a Vietnamese manufacturing group, the last two as senior accountant, leading a team of three.

I was good at my job, but I always felt that the numbers I worked with were the smallest part of what mathematics could be. When my husband and I moved to Zurich in 2022, I had time, for the first time, to read mathematics for its own sake. I took online courses, passed the ETH entrance examination, and began my bachelor's in 2025.

I want to specialise in probability and financial mathematics.
