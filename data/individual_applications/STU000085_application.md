## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

My financial situation depends almost entirely on factors outside my control, and I am applying to EduAll to give my studies a foundation that does not disappear overnight.

I study my Master in Psychology part-time, and I contribute CHF 2,002 a month myself, working about 18 hours a week as a community interpreter for a refugee support organisation in Neuchâtel. The work is meaningful, but my contract is renewed every three months, depending on the organisation's funding and the number of new arrivals.

My father, a software engineer, stayed in Lviv when my mother and I left Ukraine in 2022. He works for an IT company that serves foreign clients, and together my parents earn around CHF 3,060 a month. They send me CHF 360. In the past year his company has lost two major contracts, and staff in his department have already been let go. My mother, who is with me in Switzerland, has not been able to work in her profession here.

My monthly costs are CHF 2,140, including CHF 1,034 in rent and CHF 173 in tuition and semester fees. From September 2027 I intend to study full-time, which would allow me to finish in January 2029 instead of a year later; I would keep only about ten hours of interpreting a week, around CHF 1,130 a month. The resulting budget is balanced only if every other source continues, which is exactly what I cannot guarantee.

There is also the question of status. Protection status S is renewed year by year, and my plans depend on decisions that are not mine. An EduAll grant would give my final year the stability to be completed regardless of what happens elsewhere.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was nine, my grandmother, a school psychologist in Kyiv, let me sit in the corner of her office while she talked with a boy who had stopped speaking after his parents' divorce. I did not understand much of what was said. What I remember is that, by the end of the hour, he was laughing. I decided then that I wanted to do whatever she was doing.

I completed my bachelor's degree in psychology at Taras Shevchenko National University in Kyiv, writing my thesis on adolescent anxiety. When the full-scale invasion began in February 2022, my mother and I left within weeks and arrived in Neuchâtel that spring. I finished my degree remotely, sometimes submitting assignments from the waiting room of the cantonal migration office. In February 2026 I began the Master of Science in Psychology at the University of Neuchâtel, and I have now completed my first semester.

My work as an interpreter has shaped my studies more than anything I have read. I sit in rooms with doctors, social workers and families who have lost their homes, and I translate not just words but fear, exhaustion and sometimes silence. I have seen how often trauma goes unrecognised simply because nobody shares a language with the person in front of them.

This is what I want to change. My goal is to specialise in clinical psychology, with a focus on trauma in displaced children and adolescents, and to develop culturally adapted, low-threshold interventions that do not depend on a patient speaking the local language. Whether I eventually return to Ukraine or remain in Switzerland, there will be a generation of young people who need this kind of support for many years.

Education has been the one continuous thread through a period in which almost everything else was interrupted. I would be grateful if EduAll could help me protect it, so that I can complete my degree and put it to use where it is most needed.
