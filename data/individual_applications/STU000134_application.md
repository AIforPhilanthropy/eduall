## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eleven, in Homs, my school was closed for a whole year because of the fighting. My mother, who had been a primary school teacher, gathered the children of our building in our living room every morning. She taught us reading, writing and mathematics with the few books we had. I sat in the front, and I helped the little ones. I think that was the moment I became a teacher.

In 2016 my family left Syria, and I came to Switzerland in 2019, at nineteen, to study. It was a hard beginning. I learned German from zero, and I worked in cafés and cleaning jobs to pay my way.

I volunteer every Saturday in a homework club for refugee children in Zurich-Altstetten, and I help organise a summer camp for Arabic-speaking children every year.

My teaching placements in Zurich schools have been the happiest days of my studies. The children call me "Frau Lehrerin" and I feel that I am where I should be.

I want to become a primary school teacher in Switzerland, specialising in children who arrive with a different language, like I did.
