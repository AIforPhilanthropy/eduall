## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My father could not read, but he could calculate faster than anyone in the bazaar of Mazar-i-Sharif. He sold carpets. When I was six, he let me sit beside him and add up the prices of the day. By eight, I was doing it faster than he was. He laughed and told everyone his child would be "a master of numbers".

He did not live to see it. In 1998, when I was seventeen, he was killed when the Taliban took Mazar. My mother, my brothers and I left for Pakistan. For years I worked in a carpet workshop in Peshawar to support my family, and taught myself mathematics from second-hand books in the evenings.

After 2002 I returned to Afghanistan, completed a bachelor's in mathematics in Kabul, and taught for many years at a teacher training college. In 2014 I came to Switzerland. My degree was not recognised, and I worked in a laundry and in a warehouse while learning German.

At forty-four I started my master's at ETH. The first semester was extremely hard, but I passed every course. My thesis is in number theory.

I want to teach mathematics at university level, and one day help rebuild mathematics education in Afghanistan.
