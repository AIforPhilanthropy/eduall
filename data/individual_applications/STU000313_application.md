Is it fair to apply when I do not have a monthly deficit? I asked myself this for a long time before writing. I decided to apply, and to be completely honest.

I study Mathematics part-time, because of my health, and I work about 14 hours a week as a bookkeeper for two small businesses, mostly from home. I earn CHF 1,619. I am applying because my scholarship ends in December, because in 2027/28 I will reduce my bookkeeping to about CHF 1,140 to protect my health during the heavier courses, and because my partner's support should not have to pay for everything, including my medical costs. If my case is not a priority, I understand.

What does a forty-four-year-old accountant with multiple sclerosis do in a mathematics lecture hall? Learn, slowly, and with great pleasure.

My parents worked in a tractor factory in Kharkiv and never went to university. I studied accounting at a technical college and worked for eighteen years as an accountant. I always loved the numbers themselves more than the balances.

In 2019 I was diagnosed with MS. In 2022, the war forced me to leave Kharkiv. In Neuchâtel, I decided that if my life had to start again, I would do what I really wanted: mathematics.

I volunteer with an MS patients' association in Neuchâtel, helping new patients with information in Ukrainian and Russian.

I want to teach mathematics in secondary school.
