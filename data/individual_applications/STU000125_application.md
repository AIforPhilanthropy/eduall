Since coming to Switzerland, I have been learning French intensively, and I am now looking for part-time work, but without experience or fluent French, it has been difficult.

I am applying to EduAll so that my father's legacy can carry me through my degree, rather than disappear in its first year.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, my father, a secondary school economics teacher, used to bring the newspaper home and read the market prices to me: yams, palm oil, cement, petrol. He would ask me why the price of petrol went up when the country was full of oil. I did not know. He said, "One day you will explain it to me." He died in 2023. I am still trying to answer his question.

After my marriage ended in 2021, I decided that I would not let the rest of my life pass without doing what I had always wanted. I came to Neuchâtel in 2025 and started my Bachelor's in Economics in February 2026. French is my great challenge; I study every evening with recordings of lectures. My first semester went well, with a GPA of 3.09.

For many years in Ibadan I volunteered with my church's women's group, teaching basic bookkeeping and savings to market women. Here in Neuchâtel I continue this with an African women's association.

I want to work on public finance and resource economics in Nigeria, where oil wealth has too often bypassed ordinary people. My father asked me the question. I intend to answer it.
