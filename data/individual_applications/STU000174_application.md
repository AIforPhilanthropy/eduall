## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What makes a child stay in school? I have asked this since I was ten, when half of my class in Eldoret disappeared before secondary school: girls married, boys herding, some just gone.

Both my parents are teachers. Our house was always full of children doing homework on the floor.

At the University of Neuchâtel I have studied how schools keep children from dropping out. My thesis compares mentoring programmes in Neuchâtel and in western Kenya.

Since 2023 I co-organise a monthly meeting of East African families in Neuchâtel, where parents share information about the Swiss school system.

Plan: master's in education, then work on school retention in East Africa.
