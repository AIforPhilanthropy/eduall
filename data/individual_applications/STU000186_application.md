## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In twenty years, I would like to lead a volcano monitoring team in Indonesia, the country with more active volcanoes than any other.

I was born near Yogyakarta, in the shadow of Merapi. In 2010, when I was eight, Merapi erupted. Our village was evacuated in the night. When we returned three weeks later, the ash was knee-deep and our house was damaged. My grandmother's farm was gone. I remember a volcanologist on the radio explaining the eruption, and I remember thinking that he was the calmest person in the country.

We came to Switzerland in 2014, when my father found work here. My parents never went to university. I learned German, finished the gymnasium, and worked in kitchens and later in a geotechnical office during my bachelor in earth sciences.

My master's thesis will study how volcanic ash deposits change landslide risk on Merapi's slopes.
