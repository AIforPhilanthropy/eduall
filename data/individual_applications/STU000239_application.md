## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Law matters most to people who cannot afford it. For fifteen years I worked as a court clerk in Onitsha. Every day I saw women lose land, children and inheritance because they did not know their rights and could not pay a lawyer. I typed the judgments. I could not change them.

My parents were traders. My father studied at a teachers' college but never taught. I married young, and when my husband died in 2016, his family took our house under customary law. I fought for three years in court and won. That is when I decided to become a lawyer.

In 2023 I came to Neuchâtel to study law. French was a mountain for me. I learned it at forty-one.

I want to return to Nigeria and work on women's inheritance rights.
