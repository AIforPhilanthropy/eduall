## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

For three years in Lahore I worked as a quality control chemist in a pharmaceutical factory. I lived with my parents and saved almost all my salary, and they added what they had saved from their provident fund. That is where my savings of CHF 12,045 come from, and I want to be honest that I have them.

My costs are CHF 1,925 per month. I work a few hours per week as a laboratory helper at EPFL and earn CHF 288. My parents, both retired government teachers in Lahore living on a pension of about CHF 490, send me CHF 140.

The gap is CHF 1,497 each month. For three years my savings have paid this gap, and they are going down. This year there is a new problem: in March my father was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease and needs medication and a caretaker. My younger sister cares for him, and I now send part of my savings every month for his treatment.

My bachelor ends in July 2028, in four semesters. I think my savings can cover either my father or my last semesters, but not both.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I was twenty-four when I left my job, my family and my country to start a bachelor's degree in Switzerland. My colleagues thought I was crazy. But in the pharmaceutical factory I only tested other people's molecules. I wanted to understand how they are designed.

My parents are both teachers and they gave me the love of learning. At EPFL my results are average, GPA 2.84, but I have completed 24 courses and I learned more than in all my life before.

In Lausanne I am active in the Pakistani women's group, where I help newly arrived women with language, administration and loneliness.

After my bachelor I want to do a master in pharmaceutical sciences and work in drug development, maybe for medicines that are affordable in countries like Pakistan.
