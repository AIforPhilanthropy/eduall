## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Education matters because it is the one thing that crosses checkpoints. In Hebron, where I grew up, my father was a school director who kept his school open through every closure, every curfew. He taught me that a classroom can be the safest place in a child's life.

I studied English literature and worked two years as a teacher in a girls' school in Hebron. In 2023 I came to Basel with my husband, who had a doctoral position here, and our baby daughter.

My thesis examines how schools support young children who have experienced trauma, in Palestinian and Swiss contexts.

After my master's, I want to train teachers in trauma-sensitive education.
