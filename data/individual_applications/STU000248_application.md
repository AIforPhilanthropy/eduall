## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I will be honest from the beginning: my income today covers my costs. My partner, a pharmacist in Bern, earns around CHF 9,500 a month and gives CHF 1,330 for my share of our life. I earn CHF 1,012 as a research assistant. My costs are CHF 2,189.

So why do I write? Because my research assistant contract ends in December, and my thesis in January needs full days in the lab. And because, for most of my life, I have depended on nobody. It is hard for me to ask my partner to carry my studies alone.

I know this is not the kind of need EduAll was created for. If you decide to give the support to someone else, I will understand, and honestly I will be glad.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In February 2026, on my first day at the University of Bern, I sat in the engineering lab and touched a 3D printer for the first time. I was twenty-seven, and most of my classmates had never worked a day on a building site. I felt like a stranger, and at the same time like a child in a toy shop.

My parents worked in a textile factory in Botoșani until it closed in 2003. Neither of them went to university. After that, my father left for Italy to work on construction sites, and I did not see him for years. At sixteen I left school and followed him. For five years I worked on building sites in Italy and Germany, first as a labourer, then as a crane operator, and in the last year as foreman of a team of eight.

At twenty-one I decided to study. I did an engineering bachelor's in Bucharest while working, and then came to Bern for the master's. I published my first paper this year, on structural health monitoring of bridges.

I want to work on safer construction. I have seen too many accidents on sites.
