## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What does it mean to be a doctor in a place without doctors? I asked this when I was twelve, in our village in East Nusa Tenggara, when my little sister had a high fever and the nearest doctor was six hours away by boat. She survived. Many children did not.

My parents are farmers and never went to school. I was the first in my family to finish secondary school. With a scholarship I went to a school in Surabaya, where I was the president of the student council.

In Geneva I work in a nursing home, and I have learned a lot about care and patience. I was also elected class representative in my first year of medicine.

I want to become a doctor and go back to the islands of eastern Indonesia, where doctors are very few.
