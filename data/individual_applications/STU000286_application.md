## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty-four, a chemist from Burgas on the Black Sea, and for eight years I worked in the quality laboratory of an oil refinery. I tested fuel, water and air. I saw how close the refinery's emissions came to the beaches where I swam as a child.

In 2023 my wife received a job offer in Geneva, and I decided to use the move to change direction: from monitoring pollution to finding ways to prevent it. I began my doctorate at the University of Geneva in 2025, on catalysts that capture sulphur compounds in refinery gases. I have already published two articles from my earlier work.

On Saturdays I teach chemistry to teenagers, many of them from families like mine, newcomers learning a new language and a new country. I want them to see that science is theirs too.
