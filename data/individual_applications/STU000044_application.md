## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother could not read, but she could count very fast. She sold eggs and vegetables at the market in our village near Savar, and she did all the calculation in her head. She always told me: "If you can count, nobody can cheat you. If you can read, nobody can lie to you." She died in 2015. I think of her every day.

I started to work in a garment factory when I was fourteen, because we needed the money. In 2013 I was working in Rana Plaza. When the building collapsed, I was on the third floor. I was under the concrete for eleven hours before they found me. Many of my friends died. I had injuries in my arm, but I was lucky.

After this, I decided I will not go back to the factory. With the help of an NGO I finished my school, and I started to work for them as a community health worker, visiting families of injured workers and teaching about hygiene and nutrition. I did this for six years.

In 2023, I came to Lausanne to study Public Health. It was very hard, because I did not speak French. I learned day and night. Now I have GPA 3.2 and I finish my bachelor this winter.

Also in Lausanne I am active in the Bangladeshi association, I help women who are new here to find doctors and to understand the health insurance.

I want to work in occupational health, for the safety of workers in factories. What happened in Rana Plaza should never happen again.
