## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Why do some bacteria survive when all the others die? This was the question of my bachelor thesis in Kumasi, about antibiotic resistance in poultry farms. It is still my question today.

I grew up in Kumasi, where my father was an accountant and my mother a nurse. I was diagnosed with epilepsy at twelve. At school some teachers wanted me to stop, because they thought it was contagious or spiritual. My mother fought for me. She explained to every teacher what epilepsy is.

I studied biology at KNUST, and my thesis on resistant E. coli in chicken farms won a national student research prize. After, I worked two years as a research officer at a veterinary laboratory. From that work, two articles were published.

At UZH, I work on phages, viruses that kill bacteria, as an alternative to antibiotics. My GPA is 3.14.

Now my mother, who fought for me all my life, is fighting cancer. I want to finish my master quickly and well, so that I can support her.

In the future I want to work on antimicrobial resistance in West Africa. Farmers there use antibiotics without control, and the resistant bacteria come to people.
