## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Pontianak sits exactly on the equator, on the banks of the Kapuas, the longest river in Indonesia. I grew up in a stilt house on its bank, where the river rose into our kitchen every rainy season and the smoke of burning peatland turned the sky orange every dry one. Children in my neighbourhood coughed for months. Nobody told us why.

My parents sold dried fish and had little schooling. I was the first in my family to finish secondary school, but university was out of reach. For twenty years I cared for my parents and younger siblings, and later for my father through a long illness. During those years I spent my free time in the forests along the river with a local conservation group, replanting mangroves and helping biologists count orangutan nests. They lent me their field guides, and I read every one.

After my father's death, I came to Switzerland in 2023 to join my partner. Learning French at forty was humbling, but it opened the door I had been waiting behind for most of my life. In Fribourg I volunteer at the natural history museum's education workshops for children on Saturdays.

I want to study the ecology of tropical peatlands and help restore them. My first semester has shown me how much I still have to learn. It has also shown me that I can.

Education, for me, is permission to ask the question my neighbours never had answered: why the sky turned orange.
