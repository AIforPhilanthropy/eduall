## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years I want to work in a laboratory that tests water for arsenic. In Bangladesh millions of people drink water from tube wells with arsenic, and they do not know it. My grandfather had spots on his hands from this water. When I was small I did not understand why.

I am from Khulna. My father has a small pharmacy and my mother takes care of the house. They did not go to university, but they always told me: study, study. I am the first in my family at university.

After school I worked one year in my father's shop. I learned to talk with people about medicine and illness, and I saw how many patients came with problems from the water. Then I got admission in Fribourg. I learned French in Dhaka before, and one more year here.

The first semesters were a big change. Cold winter, new food, new language in the lab. But I like it very much. Last semester I did a small project about bacteria in river water, and I understood that this is what I want to do.

This summer I worked on a farm, picking vegetables. It was hard work, but I learned many words in Swiss German and I made friends.

For me, education is a way to bring something back. My father sells medicine to people who are ill. I want to help that they don't get ill.
