## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was seven, in a village in Casamance, my little brother died of diarrhoea. The clinic was a day's walk away, and the water in our well was dirty. My mother never recovered from that loss. I did not understand then that a bacterium had killed him. Now I study them.

My parents are rice farmers who never went to school. I was sent to live with my uncle in Ziguinchor to attend secondary school. I was the top student of my region in the baccalaureate, which brought me a scholarship to study in Lausanne.

My results here have been excellent: a GPA of 4.0. But more than grades, I am proud of the Senegalese students' association, where I help organise a mentoring programme for new students from West Africa, so that none of them feels as lost as I did in my first winter.

After my bachelor's, I want to do a master's in microbiology and work on waterborne diseases in rural West Africa.
