## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I want to explain my situation honestly. I am 27 years old and I study Master in Microbiology at University of Geneva since February 2026. Before, I worked four years in Vietnam in a food laboratory. To get my student permit I had to show the money for my studies: I brought my savings and a loan from my family. But Geneva is much more expensive than I calculated, and the money was finished already after my first semester.

Now my monthly costs are CHF 2,385. The biggest problem is the rent, CHF 1,415. I know this is very high. It is a furnished studio which is rented only by short contracts of three months, because I could not find a normal flat. I sent more than 60 applications for rooms, but without Swiss salary and without guarantor, the agencies always say no. So I pay more than I should, and every three months I don't know if I can stay.

My income is CHF 363 per month from my job in a Vietnamese restaurant kitchen in Plainpalais, a few hours on weekends. As a student I can work maximum 15 hours per week, and I need the rest for the lab. My older brother has a small electronics shop in Hanoi. His family lives on about CHF 1,680 per month, and he sends me CHF 380. That is a lot for him: he also takes care of our parents, and he still pays back the loan he took for me.

Every month I miss CHF 1,642. I have no savings now. I also want to say that my grades are only average (GPA 2.98), because in the first semester I worked too many hours and was very tired.

There is one more reason why this is urgent. My permit must be renewed in February, and the migration office will ask again for proof that I can pay my living costs, about CHF 2,600 per month. Without support, I risk losing my permit before I finish.

If I receive the grant, I will use it first for the deposit of a normal room with a long contract, so my rent will be lower and more stable. Then I can finish my master in July 2028 without stopping.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Why does a person leave a stable job and start to study again, in a country where they know nobody? Many people ask me this. My answer is always the same: because of one sample.

In 2023 I worked as quality control technician in a seafood factory near Ho Chi Minh City. One day we found a Vibrio contamination which almost went to export. Nobody in the factory could explain where it came from, and the investigation was very poor. I understood that my knowledge was not enough, I only followed procedures. I wanted to understand the bacteria really.

My parents are both teachers, and they always told me that learning is the only thing nobody can take from you. Still, it took me two years to prepare: I studied French in the evenings, I saved money, and I applied two times before I was accepted in Geneva.

The study is harder than I expected. The level is high, and my classmates studied in Europe and know already the methods. But in the lab I feel useful, because I have practical experience they don't have. My project now is about Vibrio species in food chains from Southeast Asia, so I come back to the same question which brought me here.

After my master, I want to work for food safety, maybe in an international organisation or a laboratory which works with Asian exporters. In Vietnam, many small producers lose everything when one shipment is refused, and nobody explains them why. I want to be the person who explains.

For me education is my chance, and I don't want to lose it because of rent.
