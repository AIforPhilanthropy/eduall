## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

For three years I worked Saturdays and some evenings as a laboratory helper in a pharmaceutical company in Plan-les-Ouates. I stopped in June, because my bachelor thesis in organic chemistry needs full days in the lab, and I want to finish well.

My costs are CHF 2,182 per month. My rent is CHF 1,226 for a small studio. My parents, who own a pharmacy in Alexandria and earn about CHF 1,210 together, send me CHF 230. They did not go to university themselves; they bought the pharmacy with a partner who is a pharmacist. I also receive a scholarship of CHF 5,274 per year from an Egyptian science foundation.

With the scholarship, my monthly gap is smaller, but there is still a gap every month for the last semester. The grant would allow me to finish my thesis without to go back to work.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In the pharmacy of my parents in Alexandria, I spent my childhood between boxes of medicines. I read the names and asked my parents what they did. Often they did not know; they are business people, not scientists. But the pharmacist, Doctor Hany, explained me everything. I decided to be a chemist.

I came to Geneva at nineteen. French was my third language. The first year was very hard.

I was the treasurer and later the president of the Arab Students' Association at the University of Geneva, and we organised language tandems and help for new students with housing and permits. I also help at a community centre in the Pâquis, where children come for homework support in science.

My thesis is about new methods for greener synthesis of medicines. After my bachelor I want to do a master in pharmaceutical sciences and work in drug development.
