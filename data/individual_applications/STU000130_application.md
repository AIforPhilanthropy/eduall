## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Architecture matters because the shape of a city decides who belongs in it. In Manila, I grew up in Tondo, where families of eight live in rooms of ten square metres, and where every few years a fire or a demolition erases whole neighbourhoods overnight. I saw my own street disappear when I was twelve.

My parents sold fish at the market and never went to school beyond a few years. I trained as a draftsperson and worked for two years in an architecture firm in Makati, drawing condominiums for people who would never set foot in Tondo. In the evenings, I volunteered with a community association that helped informal settlers negotiate with the city for relocation sites close to their jobs.

That work made me a target. In 2020, after our association organised protests against a large demolition, I was publicly accused of being a communist rebel. In the Philippines this can be a death sentence. I left the country in 2021 and was granted asylum in Switzerland.

In Zurich I continued to support Filipino community initiatives, and in 2025 I began my studies at ETH. My first three semesters have been the most rewarding of my life, with a GPA of 3.76.

I want to design housing for people who live on the margins of cities, in the Philippines and beyond. Everyone deserves a home that cannot be erased overnight.
