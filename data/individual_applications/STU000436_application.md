## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Karachi is a city on the Arabian Sea, where fishermen go out at night with lanterns and come back with less fish every year. My mother, a nurse, and my father, a teacher, raised me there. I spent my weekends at the fish harbour, watching the prices change, and wondered why the fishermen always seemed to lose.

I studied economics in Lahore and worked for a year as a research assistant for a fisheries project on the coast. In Geneva, I continue this work: my thesis examines how fish prices and catches have changed with climate change and overfishing.

I want to work on sustainable fisheries policy in Pakistan.
