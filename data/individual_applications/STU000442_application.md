## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

How long can a doctorate wait for its funding? Mine began in September 2025, in the chemistry department of the University of Geneva, with a promise that a grant would pay my salary from 2026. The grant was delayed until 2028. My supervisor has written to confirm this.

For now I have a small teaching contract, CHF 605 a month, and a scholarship of CHF 1,648 a year from an Indonesian science foundation. My parents, both doctors in Bandung, earn about CHF 1,480 together and send me CHF 370, a quarter of what they earn. They also support my grandmother and my younger sister.

My costs are CHF 2,567: a studio for CHF 1,396 plus utilities, and CHF 70 for laboratory materials my project needs before the grant arrives.

I am so grateful to my parents. But I do not want my research to be paid for by my grandmother's medicine. A grant would carry me to the moment my salary begins.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In December 2004, I was three years old, visiting my aunt in Aceh when the tsunami came. I do not remember the wave. I remember afterwards: the smell, the silence, my mother's hand gripping mine so hard it hurt. My aunt's family survived on a rooftop. Many of their neighbours did not.

My parents raised me to believe that science is a way to care for people. I studied chemistry in Bandung and worked two years in a laboratory testing drinking water after floods.

My doctoral research is on materials that remove heavy metals from water. I want to return to Indonesia to make clean water cheaper after disasters.
