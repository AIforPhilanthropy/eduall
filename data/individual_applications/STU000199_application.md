## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I want to explain everything honestly. I began the Master in Economics at University of Zurich in February 2026. My costs are CHF 1,808 per month, with a room of CHF 709 and health insurance of CHF 260.

I work 4 hours per week in a hotel kitchen and earn CHF 448. My older brother, a tailor in Ziguinchor (household income around CHF 870), sends me CHF 190, which is more than a fifth of what his family has.

The gap is CHF 1,170 per month. I have no savings: I used everything for the first semester deposit and the German course.

I must also say: German is very difficult for me. My master is mostly in English, but some courses and all the administration are in German, and I lose a lot of time.

With the grant I could reduce the stress, and not ask my brother to send more.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Ziguinchor is the capital of Casamance, in the south of Senegal. It is green, full of rice fields and mango trees, but for thirty years it lived with a conflict between the army and separatists. When I was nine, my village was attacked. We ran into the forest and stayed there two days. After, we moved to the city with nothing.

My parents never went to school; my father was a rice farmer, my mother sold fish. I was the first in my family to finish school and go to university in Dakar. During my studies I worked in a call centre, and I volunteered on weekends with a youth group that organised peace and reconciliation activities between young people from different communities in Casamance.

After my bachelor I worked five years in a microfinance institution in Ziguinchor, giving small loans to women farmers.

In my master I want to study development economics, especially how conflict affects rural economies. I want to go back to Casamance and help rebuild the economy of my region.
