## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The first time I entered the University of Fribourg, in September 2024, I was forty years old. I sat in the lecture hall and my hands were shaking. A young student next to me lent me a pen. I still have it.

I grew up in Mindanao. My parents were farmers and never went to school. In 2013 I had to flee my village after armed men burned it; I worked for a church organisation that protected displaced families, and I became a target. I came to Switzerland and was recognised as a refugee.

For years I worked in care homes. I volunteered for a refugee support group and helped organise the Filipino community in Fribourg.

In the care home I saw how infections spread among old people. This is why I study microbiology.

I want to work in infection prevention in hospitals and care homes.
