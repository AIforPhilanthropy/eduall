## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Kochi is a port city on the Arabian Sea, crossed by backwaters and fishing boats. My mother, a pharmacist, and my father, a school headmaster, raised us to believe that service is the purpose of education.

Before medicine, I worked for three years as a physiotherapist in a hospital in Kochi. During the 2018 Kerala floods, in my last year of study, I coordinated a relief team of twenty student health volunteers in the camps. I also helped organise free health camps for fishing communities for several years.

These experiences convinced me to become a doctor. I came to Switzerland in 2021, completed the medical bachelor's bridging programme and entered ETH's master's.

My goal is to specialise in rehabilitation medicine and to build community rehabilitation services in coastal Kerala.
