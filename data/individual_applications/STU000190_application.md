## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

For four years I worked as a domestic worker, first in Hong Kong and then in Geneva, for a diplomat's family. I cleaned, cooked, raised two children who were not mine, and sent almost everything home to pay for my younger brothers' school.

In Hong Kong I also joined a union of Filipino domestic workers. I was elected chair of our district section and helped women whose employers took their passports or did not pay them. On Sundays, our day off, we organised free legal information in the park.

My parents are fishermen in Samar. When typhoon Haiyan hit in 2013, I was sixteen, and our house was destroyed. We lived in a school for months. Later, in Hong Kong, I decided that if I survived these years, I would study one day.

In Geneva I continued volunteering with a domestic workers' association, teaching English and computer skills.

At twenty-seven, I started sociology. French is still difficult for me; I write my essays three times.

I want to research the lives of migrant domestic workers, and to work for organisations that defend their rights.
