## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Materials matter because, when the water comes, the only thing that decides whether your house stands is what it is made of. In August 2022, the floods in Sindh put a third of Pakistan under water. Our town, Larkana, was surrounded for weeks. The mud-brick houses in the villages around us simply dissolved. The concrete houses stood.

My family was lucky: our house was damaged but not destroyed. Many of my relatives lost everything. For three months, I helped organise a relief camp at my school, helping to coordinate food distribution for four hundred families and teaching younger children in makeshift classrooms. I was seventeen.

I had just finished a bachelor's in chemistry in Hyderabad. The floods made me want to study materials: how to build homes that survive, from materials that poor families can afford.

In Basel I have found the programme I was looking for. I am also active in a student group supporting flood-affected communities in Pakistan, organising fundraising and awareness events.

After my degree, I want to work on low-cost, flood-resilient building materials for South Asia. The next flood will come. I want the houses to be ready.
