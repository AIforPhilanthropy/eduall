## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In February 2026, on my first day in Basel, I got lost for two hours trying to find my university office, because I could not read the German signs. A retired man walked me there himself. He said: "I also came here once, from Italy, without a word." I have thought about him every day since.

My father was a teacher and my mother a nurse in Rabat. At nineteen, I fell seriously ill with meningitis, and I had to leave university for two years to recover. I came back, finished my degree in sociology, and worked seven years as a researcher for an NGO studying migration from Morocco to Europe.

German is my fourth language. I study with recordings and a dictionary every evening.

My research is about the social networks of Moroccan migrants in Switzerland. I want to work on migration policy.
