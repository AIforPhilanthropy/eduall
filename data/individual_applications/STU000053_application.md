I study Mathematics part-time at the University of Fribourg, and I work about 22 hours a week in a supermarket, where I earn CHF 2,515 a month. My monthly costs are CHF 1,890. My parents, who live in Bulle, give me CHF 340. My father works in a cheese factory and my mother in a laundry; together they earn about CHF 7,600, and they still support my two younger sisters.

My room in Fribourg costs CHF 862. The owner is renovating the building and all tenants must leave in December, so I need a new place and a deposit in the middle of my exams.

From September 2027 I want to change to full-time study. With the supermarket I can only take half of the courses, and at this pace I would graduate in January 2030. Full-time, I can finish in January 2029. I will keep one day a week at the supermarket, about CHF 1,130 a month, which leaves CHF 420 a month to cover.

With an EduAll grant I could make this change and pay a deposit for a new room, without asking my parents for more.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What would have happened if I had stayed in Mosul? I ask myself this question sometimes. I was sixteen when I came to Switzerland in 2010 to join my father, who had been working here for some years. Four years later, Mosul was taken by ISIS. Many of my school friends who stayed are dead or scattered.

My parents did not go to university. My father left school at twelve. When I arrived in Bulle, I spoke Arabic and some English, no French. I was placed in a class for newcomers, and then did an apprenticeship as a logistician, because everybody told me that was realistic for someone like me. I worked for four years in a warehouse.

But I always liked mathematics. In the evenings I did the maturité for adults, and at twenty-nine I started university in Fribourg. I love it. My thesis is on graph colouring problems.

For many years I have volunteered with a football club for refugee children in Bulle, as a coach on Saturdays. Many of them are in the same situation I was in.

After my bachelor I want to do a master in mathematics education and become a mathematics teacher in a secondary school. I want to show children like me that the "realistic" path is not the only one.
