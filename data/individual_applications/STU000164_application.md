I also pay CHF 90 a month for field and lab materials that my project needs before the grant starts.

The gap is CHF 1,569 a month, until January. This summer my father was diagnosed with prostate cancer, and my parents' money must now go to his treatment. A grant from EduAll would bridge these months.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was ten, I spent every weekend at my grandfather's fish pond in Iloilo, watching the water change colour with the seasons. One year all the fish died in a single night. The water had turned red. My grandfather lost his income for a year. Nobody could explain it to him. Years later I learned it was a toxic algal bloom.

I studied biology in Iloilo and did my master's in marine science in Manila, on harmful algae in fish ponds. During my studies I worked as a research assistant in an aquaculture centre and as a lab technician.

In 2019, during my second year of university, typhoon Phanfone destroyed our house. We lived with relatives for a year, and I studied in a shared room with five cousins. I still finished at the top of my class.

My doctoral project in Zurich studies how warming water changes the toxicity of algae. I want to return to the Philippines to build early warning systems for fish farmers, like my grandfather.
