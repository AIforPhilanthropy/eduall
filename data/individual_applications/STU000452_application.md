## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Computer science is for me a way to open doors. When I was a child in Ayacucho, there was one computer in the whole school, and only the director could touch it. Today I write my master thesis about software that can read Quechua voice messages and translate them into Spanish for health workers. My grandmother spoke only Quechua. In the hospital nobody understood her.

My parents are farmers. They did not go to school after primary. I was the first in my family who finished secondary school and went to university. I studied systems engineering in Lima in the evenings, and it took me eight years, because my first child was born during my studies.

In 2019 we came to Switzerland because of my partner's family. The first years were very difficult. We moved four times in two years, one time we lived three months in one room with the two children, because no one gave a flat to a family without Swiss income history. I learned French with the children, from their school books.

Since 2020 I give free computer classes to Spanish-speaking women in Neuchâtel, every Saturday. Many of them clean houses and want to learn to write CV, use e-banking, talk with the school of their children by email. Some of them now have better jobs.

I want to work on language technology for indigenous languages. For me, education is the proof that a child from Ayacucho can also build the computer, not only look at it.
