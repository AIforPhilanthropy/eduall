## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was seven, my mother took me to her classroom on a Saturday. She taught at a primary school in Ibadan, and she needed to mark exercise books. I sat at a desk in the empty room and wrote my name on the blackboard, again and again, in large careful letters. She came and stood behind me, and she said, "One day you will stand here for real." I have never forgotten her voice.

My mother died of a stroke in 2024, suddenly, at fifty-one, in my first months in Bern. I flew home for the funeral, and when I came back I did not know if I could continue. The winter was very dark. I did not tell anyone at the university. I went to lectures and could not remember a word afterwards. What kept me going was the thought of her standing behind me at the blackboard.

Slowly, with the help of a counsellor at the university and a few kind classmates, I found my way back. This year I have passed every course, and I have started my master's thesis on how primary teachers in Nigeria and Switzerland support grieving children in their classes. I chose this topic because nobody knew how to support me, and I think teachers should.

I want to become a teacher trainer. Education, for me, is my mother's classroom. I want to stand in it for real.
