## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

Mathematics is important to me because it is the one place where I never needed my eyes. I want to explain my money honestly.

I study Master in Mathematics at EPFL since February 2026. My costs are CHF 2,329 per month. My studio is CHF 1,025. My health insurance is CHF 475.

My brother, who repairs bicycles in Pune and whose household lives on about CHF 460, sends me CHF 120.

I have no job, and I never had one. I am blind since birth. In India, nobody would employ a blind mathematician; I applied many times. In Lausanne, I also have not found work, because most student jobs need sight.

I have CHF 10,385 in savings. This is the money I received from a disability foundation's prize for my bachelor results, and from my community. I use it to pay the gap of CHF 2,209 each month. It will last five months more. My master ends in July 2028.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was six, my father, a vegetable seller, bought me a Braille abacus from a charity shop. He could not read, but he watched me move the beads for hours. He said: "You see with your fingers what we cannot see with our eyes."

My parents never went to school. I studied in a school for blind children in Pune, and then mathematics at the university, with Braille and a screen reader. It was very slow, and very lonely.

At EPFL everything is new: French, the speed, the mathematics notation that is not always accessible. I passed my first semester, but my grades are average, GPA 2.81. Many documents I must first convert myself.

I want to do a PhD in combinatorics and to develop accessible mathematics tools for blind students.
