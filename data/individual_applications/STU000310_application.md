## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was ten, my family lived in a two-room house in Lahore's walled city, with eleven people. On hot nights we all slept on the roof. I used to look at the other roofs, all connected, a city above the city, and think that architects must have designed it. Nobody had. People had built it themselves.

My parents never went to school. My father sold spices, and he died when I was fourteen. My uncle took us in.

At university in Lahore, I led a student group that worked with residents of informal settlements to design and build community spaces: a small library, a shaded courtyard, a water point. I also helped found an association of young architects working with low-income neighbourhoods.

In Zurich my diploma project is a housing model for dense, low-income neighbourhoods in Lahore.

I want to design with communities, not for them.
