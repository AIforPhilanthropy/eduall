## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother never went to school, but she taught me to count using the stars from our rooftop in Karachi. My father drove a rickshaw. When I was fourteen, I was hit by a car while walking home from school, and I spent a year in hospital. My right leg never fully recovered; I walk with a stick and cannot stand for long.

That year in hospital, I read every physics book the hospital library had. When I went back to school, I knew what I wanted to study.

For years in Karachi I helped run a free study group for children in my neighbourhood who could not afford tuition. In Bern I help at a community centre, supporting new students with administration.

German is still hard for me, but I love physics. I want to become a physics teacher.
