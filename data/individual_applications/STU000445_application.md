## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I believe that most people who die too early die of something we already know how to prevent. I learned this in Sylhet, in Bangladesh, where my father taught biology at a secondary school and my mother was a village nurse. When I was twelve, a cholera outbreak came to our town after the floods. My mother worked day and night. She came home and cried for the children she could not save.

After school I became a community health worker. For ten years I travelled by boat and on foot to villages that are cut off in the rainy season, weighing babies, giving vaccines, teaching mothers about clean water. In 2007 a cyclone destroyed the clinic where I worked and our family home. We rebuilt both. I learned then that I can start again from nothing.

In 2016 I came to Switzerland with my husband. I did not speak German. I cleaned offices, then became a care assistant, and every evening I studied. In 2023, at thirty-eight, I began my degree. I have completed twenty courses.

In Lucerne I also help run health evenings for Bengali-speaking women, about check-ups, vaccines and how the Swiss health system works. Many of them had never had a smear test.

I want to work in maternal and child health, in Switzerland or with an international organisation. For me, education is the way to do at large scale what my mother did in one village.
