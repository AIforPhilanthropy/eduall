## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, in a village near Multan, many children in my village became sick with typhoid. My little cousin died. The doctor came from the city only after one week. I asked my father why nobody knew what the sickness was. He could not answer; he never went to school.

My parents are farmers. I was the first in my family to finish school. From fifteen I worked: first in cotton fields, then in a textile factory, later as a laboratory helper in a hospital in Multan. There I saw bacteria for the first time in a microscope.

I also volunteered with a group that organised clean-water campaigns in villages, and I helped the Pakistani community in Bern with translations when I arrived in 2022.

At twenty-six I started microbiology in Bern. German is still very difficult.

I want to work in a hospital laboratory, and one day to help villages like mine to have clean water.
