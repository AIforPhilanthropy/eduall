## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Five years as a hospital porter and then nursing assistant in Germany. I watched doctors decide, and I knew I wanted to be one.

I grew up in a Roma family in Burgenland. My father did casual building work; my mother cleaned houses. Nobody in my family had studied beyond the minimum, and in our town that was not seen as a surprise. At school, teachers routinely placed Roma children in the slower track without much discussion; I was moved there at eleven and spent years fighting to be moved back.

In Germany I volunteered with a support centre, interpreting for Romani-speaking patients who had nobody else to explain what was happening to them.

Came to Zurich in 2025 for medicine. I know my grades are weak. I am older, tired, and I work. But I don't stop.

Goal: emergency medicine.
