## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, I sat in the back of a courtroom in Abeokuta where my father, a lawyer, was defending a market woman accused of theft. She could not read the charges. My father read them to her in Yoruba, slowly. She was acquitted. On the way home he told me: "Justice begins with understanding."

My mother is a teacher. After school I studied English and worked for three years as a translator and interpreter in Lagos. I volunteered for a legal aid clinic, interpreting for clients who spoke only Yoruba or Hausa.

In 2024 I came to Lausanne to study law. My French was weak at the start, but I have improved every semester, and my GPA is 3.38.

I want to work in migration and asylum law, where language is often the first barrier to justice.
