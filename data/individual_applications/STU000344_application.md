## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Chernihiv is an old city with golden domes, north of Kyiv. In 2022, when I was sixteen, it was under siege for a month. We lived in the basement of our building. There was no water, no electricity. I read the only book I had with me, a book of Skovoroda, a Ukrainian philosopher, again and again. He wrote that the world tried to catch him, but did not succeed. I thought: I will not be caught either.

We left in April. In Lucerne I learned German in one year and finished school. My parents did not go to university; in Ukraine my father was a driver, my mother a cook.

I work every weekend in a café. German philosophy texts are very difficult for me, I read them with a dictionary and a lot of patience.

I want to study the philosophy of resistance and freedom, and maybe one day teach it in Ukraine.
