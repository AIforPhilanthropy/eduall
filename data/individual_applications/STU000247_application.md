## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Khan Younis, in Gaza. My father was a fisherman, my mother at home. Nobody in my family went to university before me. Electricity came four hours per day. I did my homework by the light of my father's fishing lamp.

I studied physics at the Islamic University of Gaza. On weekends I volunteered to teach mathematics to children in our camp. In 2024 I got a scholarship offer and was able to leave for Zurich. My family is still there.

Physics is the only place where things are logical for me. My thesis is about the physics of semiconductors for solar cells, because in Gaza the sun is the only energy that never stops.

After my master I want to do a PhD, and later help to rebuild the universities of Gaza.
