## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first winter in Zurich, in December 2024, I went to the Rhône Glacier with my class. I touched the ice for the first time in my life. I cried a little, because I thought of the glacier above Huaraz that my grandfather showed me as a child, which is almost gone now.

My parents are both civil engineers, and they always wanted me to study. I studied geology in Lima and came directly to ETH for the master. My thesis is about how glaciers in the Andes are retreating, and what this means for water in the dry season.

I am not a person with a dramatic story. I am just someone who wants to understand ice before it disappears, and to help Peru prepare for a future with less water.
