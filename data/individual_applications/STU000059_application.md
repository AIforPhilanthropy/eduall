*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Five years of work: first as a farm labourer, then a driver for an NGO that dug boreholes. I learned where water is, and where it is not anymore.

My parents were teachers. My father died when I was fifteen. I left school to feed my siblings, then finished secondary school at night at nineteen.

At twenty-two I came to Zurich to study environmental science at ETH. Hard, but I am learning.

Goal: groundwater management for northern Ghana.
