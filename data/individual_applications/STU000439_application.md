## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Microbiology matters because, in the town where I grew up, nobody trusted the tap water either, even though officially it was "safe". Bischofferode, in Thuringia: the potash mine closed when I was a baby, and half the town left to find work in the west. The ones who stayed, like my parents, lived on short contracts and later on welfare.

My parents never studied beyond school. My father worked in demolition, on and off, and was often injured; my mother cleaned offices at night. There was no money for anything beyond the basics, and East German family poverty is not something people in Zurich seminar rooms usually picture when they hear "Germany".

For four years I worked as a laboratory technician at a regional hospital, the last two as head of the night shift, six staff. I saved what I could, but my parents' health kept taking it: my father's disability pension barely covers his medication.

I came to Zurich in 2023 for better training and, honestly, for air that did not smell like the old mine. I want to work on water safety, for towns like mine and for places with far less than mine has.
