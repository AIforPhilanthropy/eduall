I would like to use a grant to finish my thesis with calm, and to be independent from my family's business in the transition.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In March 2025, in the physics building at Irchel, I saw for the first time a real measurement from our cryostat: a curve that nobody had measured before, in a material that nobody had made before. My supervisor looked at it and said "Interesting." For a physicist, this is the highest compliment. I went home and could not sleep.

I grew up in Plovdiv. When I was fourteen, my father's company went bankrupt. We lost our flat and moved to my grandmother's two rooms, five people. My father worked on building sites of other people for two years, my mother cleaned offices. I started to give private lessons in mathematics to younger children, to help. We came out of it, slowly. My father built a new company, smaller and more careful. But I learned that nothing is guaranteed.

I studied physics at Sofia University and came to Zurich in 2025 for the master. My research is in condensed matter physics, on superconductivity in thin films. The level at UZH is very high, and I had to work a lot.

In Zurich I help organise the Bulgarian student community, we meet every month, and I help newcomers with apartments and insurance, which is always the biggest shock.

After the PhD I want to work in research, maybe in quantum materials. And I want to keep giving lessons, like I did at fourteen, because this was the first time I understood that knowledge can be a job and a help at the same time.
