## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

For three years I have worked in the mornings at a recycling centre in Zurich, sorting glass, metal and electronics before lectures. I still do, about 14 hours a week, and earn CHF 1,550. This is why I have studied part-time.

I should be transparent: my costs are covered. My parents, who live in Zurich, contribute CHF 360 a month; my father is a construction foreman and my mother a nurse, and together they earn around CHF 5,420. My expenses are CHF 1,402. I have no savings.

I am applying because from September 2027 I will study full-time to write my bachelor's thesis, which brings my graduation forward from January 2029 to July 2028. The thesis involves soil sampling at former industrial sites in Bulgaria, with travel and laboratory analyses not covered by my programme. I will keep only Saturday shifts, about CHF 680, and miss CHF 362 a month. I would prefer not to ask my parents for more, since they are also supporting my grandmother.

I understand if this is not considered a priority.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I came to Zurich at seventeen, when my parents found work here. I spoke no German. The first two years were the hardest of my life: I was placed in a class for newcomers, lost my friends, and felt I had lost my future. At eighteen I went through a serious depression. With help, I came through it, completed the maturité, and started environmental science.

I grew up near the Kremikovtsi steel works outside Sofia, where the soil is contaminated with heavy metals. As a child I played in that soil.

German is still harder for me than for my classmates, especially in written exams. But I have passed every year.

I want to work on soil remediation at contaminated industrial sites, in Bulgaria and in Switzerland.
