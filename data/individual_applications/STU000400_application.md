My monthly costs are CHF 2,321, and I cover all but CHF 1,077 of them myself. I want to explain that gap honestly.

I work nine hours a week as an assistant in an engineering office and earn CHF 1,034. My uncle in Beirut, a car mechanic, contributes CHF 210, although the whole household lives on roughly CHF 970. I have CHF 4,032 saved from summer work, which I have been using to cover the difference.

I have four semesters left in the Bachelor in Engineering at the Bern University of Applied Sciences (BFH). I want to finish in July 2028 without leaving my thesis to search for housing.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Aleppo. My parents ran a small grocery and never went to school. In 2012, when I was thirteen, our neighbourhood was bombed and we left for Lebanon. For three years I did not go to school. I worked in a sewing workshop and taught younger children in the camp to read.

In 2016 I came to Switzerland to join my uncle's family. I learned German, finished school, and started engineering in 2023. I also volunteer with a Red Cross youth group and help organise an Arabic-German language café.

German engineering vocabulary is still hard for me, but I have passed every year.

I want to design water systems for cities destroyed by war.
