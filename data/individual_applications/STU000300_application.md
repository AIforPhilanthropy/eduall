## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The first time I saw the Fribourg cathedral lit up at night, in winter 2012, I was eighteen and had arrived in Switzerland a week earlier. I stood on the bridge and felt, for the first time in years, that nobody was watching me with hatred.

I grew up in a small town in Punjab. I am transgender and non-binary, and at sixteen, when my family discovered who I was, my life became dangerous. My father and uncles threatened to kill me. With the help of my aunt, I left India, and in Switzerland I was recognised as a refugee.

The years after were hard. I had to learn French, finish school, and survive depression. For a long time I could not work or study at all. What saved me was a community: a small group of LGBTQ refugees in Fribourg, which I later helped to organise and which now meets every week.

In 2024, at twenty-nine, I finally started university. Biology is my home now. I love the patience of plants.

I want to work in plant research, maybe on crops that grow in poor and salty soils, like the fields of Punjab.
