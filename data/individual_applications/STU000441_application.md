## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, the river Teesta flooded our village near Rangpur. The water came in the night. My father, a school teacher, carried me on his shoulders to the embankment. We watched our house fall into the river.

I studied geology in Dhaka and worked two years as a field assistant for a river research institute, measuring erosion along the Teesta and Jamuna rivers. One article from that work was published.

My master's research is on river erosion and flood risk. This year, my parents lost their house again, to the same river.

I want to go back to Bangladesh and help villages prepare for floods.
