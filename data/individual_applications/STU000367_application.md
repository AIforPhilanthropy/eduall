## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My parents are rice farmers in Quang Nam, in central Vietnam. Neither went to school beyond primary level. Every autumn, floods cover our fields; every few years, landslides close the mountain roads. In 2020, a landslide buried a village near ours and killed twenty people, including two of my classmates' families.

I studied geology in Hanoi and worked four years for the national geological survey, mapping landslide hazards in the central highlands. I was the first person from my village to become a scientist.

French is my third language, and I learned it only in 2024. Seminars and administration are still difficult for me, but my research is going well.

My doctoral research uses satellite radar to detect slow slope movements before they fail. I want to return to Vietnam and help build an early warning system for mountain villages.
