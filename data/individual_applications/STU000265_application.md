## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Chemistry matters to me because it saved my mother's life. When I was ten, she had cancer. The chemotherapy made her very sick, but it worked. I asked the doctor how a medicine can know which cells to kill. He laughed and said: "It does not know. That is the problem." I decided to be the chemist who makes medicines that know.

At fifteen I lost my older sister in a car accident. My family almost broke. I studied every night, because it was the only thing that I could control.

I came to EPFL at nineteen. Everything was new: French at university level, the cold, being alone. In the first year I failed one exam and passed it the second time.

My thesis is about targeted drug delivery. After my bachelor, I want to do a master in chemical engineering and work in pharmaceutical research.
