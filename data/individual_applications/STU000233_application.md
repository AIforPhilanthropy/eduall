## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty-four, from Basra, and I study data science in Bern.

My father was a fisherman on the Shatt al-Arab and my mother sold bread. Neither of them went to school. I was the first in my family to finish secondary school. In 2003, when I was eleven, the war came to Basra. My school was closed for a year, and I helped my father on the boat.

After school I worked for eight years in the port of Umm Qasr, first as a loader, then as a shift supervisor for twenty workers. I volunteered with a youth group that organised football and English lessons for children in our district.

In 2021 I came to Switzerland with my wife, who found a job here. I learned German and passed the university entrance exam.

I want to work in data analysis for logistics and ports. I know how ports work from the inside; now I want to understand them in numbers.
