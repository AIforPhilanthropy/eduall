## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Smolyan is a small town in the Rhodope mountains, with forests, snow, and not enough doctors. When I was a boy, the ambulance took ninety minutes to reach our village. My grandfather died waiting for it.

My parents worked in a lumber mill; neither went to university. After school, I became a volunteer with the mountain rescue service. For twenty years I carried injured hikers and skiers down the mountains, and for the last eight I led the volunteer rescue team of our region. I never had a paid job: my parents helped at first, and later my wife's salary supported us.

In 2019 we moved to Zurich for my wife's work. I learned German, and at forty I decided to become the doctor our mountains never had.

After my studies I want to work in emergency medicine, and one day return to the Rhodopes.
