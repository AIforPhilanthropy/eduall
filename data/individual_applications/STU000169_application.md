## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was a child in Huaraz, in the Andes, I walked with my grandmother to the glacier above our village every year for the festival of the Señor de la Soledad. Every year the ice was farther away. My grandmother said the mountain was sad. I did not understand then that she was talking about climate change.

My parents are farmers and could not go to school for long. I was the first of six children to finish secondary school. I worked for twelve years in Lima, as a domestic worker and then in a textile factory, and I volunteered on Sundays with a women's group in my neighbourhood, organising a community kitchen.

In 2017 I married and came to Switzerland. I learned German, did the passerelle for adults, and at thirty-eight I began Environmental Science at the University of Bern.

My thesis is about glacier retreat and water availability for villages in the Andes. I want to work with communities in Peru to plan their water for a future without glaciers.
