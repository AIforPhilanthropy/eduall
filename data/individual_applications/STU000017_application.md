## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What makes a person continue after the doctors tell them the worst news? This question is with me since I am 27 years old.

In 2018, when I was in my final year of nursing studies in Plovdiv, I was diagnosed with Hodgkin lymphoma. I had one year of chemotherapy and radiation. I lost my hair, my place in the study program and many of my friends, who did not know how to talk to me. But I met nurses in the oncology department in Sofia who changed how I see my profession. They were not only giving medicine. They were giving courage.

I am healthy now since six years. After my treatment I finished my nursing diploma. During my studies and after, I was the president of a patients association for young adults with cancer in Bulgaria, which I co-founded in 2020. We organised support groups in four cities and we made a campaign for fertility preservation information before chemotherapy, which was a big problem in Bulgaria.

In 2023 I came to Lausanne with my partner. I chose the master in Nursing Science because I want to do research on psycho-oncology nursing, how nurses can support young patients to continue their life plans during and after cancer. My GPA is 3.3.

In the future I want to work between Switzerland and Bulgaria, to bring evidence-based supportive care into oncology departments in Eastern Europe, where it is still missing.

For me education is my answer to the illness. It took one year of my life. I want to give many years back.
