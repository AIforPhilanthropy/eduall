## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Nyalenda, an informal settlement in Kisumu, where cholera came almost every rainy season. My mother sold vegetables in the market and my father was a boda-boda driver who died in a road accident when I was ten. Neither had gone to secondary school.

When I was sixteen, I started volunteering with a community health programme, going door to door explaining how to treat water and recognise dehydration. At twenty I was elected youth coordinator for the programme, leading forty young volunteers. We reduced cholera cases in our area by more than half in two years, and I co-authored a short paper with a researcher from the university who evaluated our work.

I studied public health in Kisumu on a scholarship while working as a data clerk at the county health office.

In Geneva I want to learn how to design and evaluate programmes at scale, so that what worked in Nyalenda can work in other settlements.
