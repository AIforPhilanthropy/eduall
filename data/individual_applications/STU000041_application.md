## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Herat is an old city of blue tiles and gardens, and for a short time in my life it was a city where girls could study. I was lucky to be young in that time. My father was a teacher of literature and my mother a midwife, and they wanted all their daughters to go to university.

I studied economics in Herat. In 2017, during an internship in Kabul, a bomb exploded near our building. I was injured in my back and my left leg. Since then I have chronic pain and difficulty walking long distances. After my degree I worked for three years for an international organisation, managing small loans for women's businesses.

After 2021, women in Afghanistan could no longer work or study. I continued to teach girls in secret in my home in Herat, economics and English, for one year. Then it became too dangerous. In 2022 I was admitted to a preparation programme at the University of Fribourg, and I came to Switzerland with a student permit.

Studying in a new language, with pain, was very hard. But I am close now. My thesis examines how women's employment changes after conflicts, comparing Afghanistan, Iraq and Bosnia.

I hope to work for an international organisation or a research institute on gender and economic policy. When Afghanistan opens again for women, I want to be ready.
