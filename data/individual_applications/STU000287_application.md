## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was seven, my grandmother told me the story of the night in December 1984 when the gas came in Bhopal. She woke up coughing, took my mother, then a baby, and ran. Half of our street did not wake up. My grandmother lived another thirty years with damaged lungs. She could not read, but she remembered everything.

My parents were rickshaw pullers and domestic workers. I was the first in my family to finish school. As a teenager I volunteered with a women's organisation that documents the lives of gas survivors, and in my last school years I helped their oral history project record more than two hundred testimonies.

In 2024 I came to Lucerne with a scholarship to study history. I want to become an oral historian, so that the memories of people like my grandmother are not lost.

In Lucerne I help organise a women's group for migrant mothers at a community centre.
