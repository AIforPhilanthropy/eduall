*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am from Kharkiv. It is a city of universities and factories, 40 km from the Russian border. Since 2022 it is also a city that is shelled almost every week. My parents still live there.

In Ukraine I worked eight years as a guide in the Kharkiv Historical Museum. In 2022 the museum closed and much of the collection was evacuated. I came to Geneva, where my husband already worked, and started a BA in History in autumn 2023.

My thesis: how Ukrainian museums protected their collections during the war, 2022–2025. I have interviewed staff from four museums.

Goal: master's in museum studies or cultural heritage, then work on the protection and documentation of cultural heritage in conflict zones.

Education matters to me because history is being destroyed in my city right now, and somebody has to record it.
