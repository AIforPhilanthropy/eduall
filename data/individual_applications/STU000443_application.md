## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eleven, in Sofia, I read Plato's cave allegory in a book my grandfather gave me. I did not understand it, but I could not stop thinking about it. Then, at fourteen, I became ill with anorexia. For five years I was in and out of hospitals.

What saved me was a volunteer group of former patients who visited young people in the clinic. When I recovered, I joined them, and for five years I visited teenagers in hospital, talking with them about books.

At twenty-seven I started philosophy in Basel. I want to work on the philosophy of medicine and illness.
