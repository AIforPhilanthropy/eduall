## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

I have four semesters left in my Bachelor in Biology at the University of Fribourg. I finish in July 2028.

My monthly costs are CHF 1,556. I live in a student residence room for CHF 603, which is one of the cheapest options in Fribourg. I earn CHF 484 a month working in the university library. My aunt in Nairobi, a nurse (household income around CHF 1,160), sends me CHF 346.

The gap is CHF 726 a month. I have no savings.

I will be honest about how I have managed so far: in the first two years I worked many more hours, in a restaurant kitchen in the evenings, and I covered the gap that way. This semester I reduced to the library job only, because my lab courses take whole days. Since then I have been falling behind on rent. I owe my residence one month.

With EduAll's support I could pay what I owe and finish my degree without going back to evening shifts in the last months.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My grandmother raised me in a village near Kisumu, on Lake Victoria. My parents died when I was small, and she took me in with nothing but a small shamba and a lot of stubbornness. She never went to school. She could name every plant around the lake, and what it was good for. I followed her every day. I think she was my first biology teacher.

When I was twelve, she fell ill with malaria and nearly died. The clinic was twenty kilometres away. I carried water and cooked for weeks, and missed school. My aunt came from Nairobi and took me to live with her so I could finish school.

I was the first in my family to go to university. I came to Fribourg in 2025 with a scholarship for the first semester, which ended, and since then I have worked. I study in French and English. My GPA is 3.55.

My thesis is about water hyacinth, the invasive plant that covers Lake Victoria and destroys fishing. My grandmother cursed it every day.

After my bachelor I want to do a master in ecology and work on the restoration of Lake Victoria.
