## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My first memory is the sound of my father's metal workshop, the sparks from the welding, and the smell of hot iron. When I was seven, my father let me hold a piece of steel after it cooled. He said: "Everything in the world is made of something. Find out what."

At fifteen I lost my older brother in a motorbike accident. My parents closed the workshop for a year. I worked with my uncle in construction to help the family, and I finished school at night.

I also helped organise a football league for children in our neighbourhood, to keep them away from the street, because my brother always said that sport saved him.

In 2025 I came to Fribourg to study materials science. The first year was very hard: French, mathematics, physics, everything new at the same time. But I learn every day.

I want to work in metallurgy and materials for construction, and maybe one day make my father's workshop into a small factory.
