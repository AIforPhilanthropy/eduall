## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Kolomyia is a small town at the foot of the Carpathians, where every family seems to own a guesthouse, or wishes it did. My grandmother ran one with four rooms. As a child, I helped her change sheets, cook for guests and write the bills by hand. She had no business training, and she lost money every winter.

My parents, both engineers, wanted me to follow them. I studied economics in Lviv instead, and worked in tourism development for the regional administration, helping small family businesses apply for grants and training. On weekends, I volunteered with an organisation teaching digital skills to rural women who ran guesthouses.

In 2016 I moved to Switzerland with my husband, and later completed a bachelor's degree here while working. My master's research at Lucerne looks at how small, family-run hospitality businesses can survive seasonal crises, comparing Central Switzerland and the Carpathians. I have published two papers on rural tourism resilience.

Since 2022, the war has changed everything for the region I come from. The Carpathians have become a refuge for displaced families, and the guesthouses of my grandmother's generation are now shelters. I want to use my knowledge to help these businesses rebuild when the war ends.
