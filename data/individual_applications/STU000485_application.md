## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In June 2023, on a field course above Gruyères, our lecturer stopped the group in a meadow and asked us to count the species in a one-metre square. I found forty-one. I had grown up in a city where the only green was the park by our block, and I remember kneeling there, astonished, realising that I wanted to spend my life studying exactly this: how such diversity survives, and how quickly it can be lost.

I came to Switzerland at eighteen, in 2017, to study in French, a language I had learned at a bilingual school in Timișoara. My first attempt, in medicine, lasted a year before I admitted it was my parents' dream rather than mine. Changing to biology in 2022 was the best decision I have made.

Alongside my studies, I work in a plant pathology laboratory, preparing samples and maintaining greenhouse cultures. Since 2023 I have volunteered with a regional nature conservation association, monitoring orchid populations in dry meadows, and I help coordinate a community seed library in Fribourg where residents exchange local and heirloom varieties.

I have completed twenty-one courses and will write my bachelor thesis on the effects of drought on meadow plant communities.

For me, education is the discipline of looking closely at what others walk past.
