## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was ten, in Kabul, my father ran a small carpet shop. He let me keep the accounts in a notebook. I loved adding the numbers at the end of the day. He could not read, but he trusted my columns.

My parents never went to school. In 2021, our family left Afghanistan; my father stayed behind to protect the shop, and I have not seen him since. I came to Switzerland with my mother and sisters. Our asylum application was rejected, but we were provisionally admitted, because we could not be sent back to Afghanistan.

In the refugee centre, I organised a small study group for Afghan teenagers, teaching mathematics and German. Later I was elected spokesperson of the young residents. I work in a shop in St. Gallen and help new Afghan families with administration.

German is still hard for me in exams.

I want to work in finance for small businesses, like my father's.
