## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I am a doctoral student in Environmental Science at the University of Bern, in my third year. My doctoral project is funded only partly: I receive a scholarship of CHF 6,638 a year from an Indonesian government programme, and I work as a research assistant for about 10 hours a week, earning CHF 1,123 a month. My supervisor has confirmed in writing that a full position is not possible before 2028.

My monthly costs are CHF 2,319. My rent is CHF 1,169 plus CHF 140 for utilities. My older sister, a midwife in Palu, contributes CHF 280, although the whole household lives on roughly CHF 1,280.

My room is in a building that will be demolished next spring, and I must move before March 2027. Because of my disability, I need a ground-floor or lift-accessible flat near a tram stop, which is hard to find in Bern.

A grant from EduAll would allow me to secure accessible housing and continue my research.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In January 2023, I spent my first winter in Bern. Walking to the institute on icy pavements with crutches, I fell twice in one week. A stranger helped me up the second time, carried my bag to the door and said, "Next time, take the tram, it stops right there." It was such a small kindness, but I remember it every day.

I was born in a village near Palu, in Central Sulawesi. My parents are farmers and never went to school. At fourteen, I developed transverse myelitis, an inflammation of the spinal cord, which left my legs weak. I walk with crutches.

In 2018 an earthquake and tsunami destroyed Palu. My family lost our house and my father's land. I was working as a researcher at a local university at the time, studying land use, and I helped coordinate emergency surveys of damaged areas.

My doctoral research is on land-use change and landslide risk in Sulawesi. I want to return to Indonesia and help make rebuilding safer.
