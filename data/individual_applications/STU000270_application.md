## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Eldoret sits high in the Rift Valley, surrounded by farms and running tracks. It is also the town where, in January 2008, a church sheltering families was set on fire during the post-election violence. I was twenty-one. My parents died in that violence. My younger siblings and I survived because we were at my aunt's house.

For the following years, I raised my three siblings. I was elected chairman of the youth group of the church community that took us in, and we organised support for other families who had lost parents: school fees, food, company. Those years were hard, but they taught me what a community can do.

When my youngest sister finished university in 2021, I finally took my own chance. With the help of a scholarship I came to Basel, completed my bachelor of medicine, and started the master's.

I want to become a doctor in emergency and trauma care, and return to the Rift Valley, where too many people still die before they reach a hospital.
