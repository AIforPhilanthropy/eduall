## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What happens to a girl who wants to build bridges in a village where girls do not go to secondary school? In my village near Louga, most girls of my age were married at fifteen. My parents are farmers and never went to school. My mother wanted another life for me.

At fourteen I went to live with my uncle in Dakar to continue school. I learned French properly only then; at home we speak Wolof. School was very hard for me, but I was good in mathematics.

After school I worked six years in a construction company in Dakar, first as a secretary and later as assistant site supervisor, leading a team of ten workers. I was the only woman on the site.

In 2023 I came to EPFL with a scholarship for my first year. French at university level was again a big challenge for me, and also the speed of the courses.

My bachelor project is about low-cost bridges for rural roads in Senegal.

After my degree I want to build roads and bridges that connect villages, so that girls can reach schools.
