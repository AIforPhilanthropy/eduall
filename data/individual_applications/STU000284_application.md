## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

Every Saturday and Sunday since I was sixteen, I have worked. First stacking shelves in a supermarket in Renens, then in a warehouse, and now as a technician in a small engineering firm, where I earn CHF 820 a month. I have almost never had a free weekend since. I am not complaining; I am only explaining.

I study Mechanical Engineering at EPFL, in my final master's semester. My costs are CHF 2,113 a month. My mother, who cleans offices in Lausanne and earns about CHF 1,620 part-time, gives me CHF 500, almost a third of what she earns. I have no savings. The gap is CHF 793 every month, which until now I covered by working more in the summer.

This year my laboratory courses are full-time, and I cannot work the summer hours that usually save me. I am determined to finish. I want my mother to stop cleaning offices at night, and for that I need to finish.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I came to Lausanne at twelve, from Kumasi, to join my mother. She had left when I was six. For six years we spoke on the phone every Sunday. When I arrived, she was almost a stranger, and so was the language, the cold, the school. I was put in the lowest class. A teacher told me engineering was "not realistic".

My mother never went to school. She cleaned offices at night, and I often went with her, doing my homework in empty meeting rooms under fluorescent lights. I remember reading about bridges in a textbook on a conference table at two in the morning.

I finished the maturité, then studied mechanical engineering at EPFL. My master's thesis on heat exchangers for low-cost refrigeration has led to a co-authored conference paper. My GPA is 3.62.

I want to design cooling systems for vaccines and food in regions without reliable electricity, like parts of Ghana.
