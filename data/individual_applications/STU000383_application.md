## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I want to tell you about my life here honestly, because it is complicated.

I am in the first year of the Master in Health Sciences at the University of Lucerne. My student B permit expired in July, and I applied for renewal on time. But the migration office asks me to prove that I have about CHF 2,600 every month. My only regular support is CHF 190 from my uncle in Sylhet, a teacher whose household earns around CHF 810. So my renewal is pending, and while it is pending, I am not allowed to work.

My costs are CHF 2,070 per month. My room costs CHF 975. I have type 1 diabetes, and my insulin and sensors cost more than my insurance covers.

In June my father in Sylhet lost his small shop in a flood. My family cannot help me anymore.

Every month I miss CHF 1,880. I have no savings. I am determined to finish. With a grant, I could show the migration office my means and finish my degree in July 2028.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first winter in Lucerne, in 2025, I had a hypoglycaemia on the way to the university and fell in the snow near the lake. An old woman stopped, gave me sugar from her bag, and waited with me until I could stand. She did not speak English and I did not speak German. We laughed together anyway.

I was diagnosed with diabetes at eleven, in Sylhet. My parents, who never went to school, spent all their savings on insulin. I promised myself I would work in health, so that families like mine would not have to choose between medicine and food.

Before coming to Switzerland I worked two years as a health worker for an NGO, visiting people with diabetes in villages.

My thesis is about access to insulin in South Asia.
