## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was six, my grandmother gave me a box of old buttons and asked me how many different ways I could put them in a line. I spent the whole afternoon on the floor. At dinner I told her the answer was "too many". She laughed and said: "Then find a way to count them without counting." This was my first combinatorics problem, and in some way I am still solving it.

I grew up in Cluj. My parents are doctors, and they expected that I become a doctor too. I tried one year of medicine and I was miserable. Then I changed to mathematics at Babeș-Bolyai University. It was the best decision in my life.

During my bachelor I was the coordinator of the mathematics circle for high school students in Cluj, where we prepared students for olympiads. I also volunteered with an NGO which gives free lessons to Roma children in villages near Cluj. After graduating I taught mathematics for one year in a high school.

In Basel I study combinatorics and discrete probability. With my professor from Cluj I published two papers during my bachelor, on permutation patterns. My GPA here is 3.27.

After the master I want to do a PhD and later teach at university. I also want to start a mathematics circle for children in Basel who come from families without university background.

For me education is something to give further, like my grandmother gave me the buttons.
