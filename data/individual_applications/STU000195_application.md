## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Rajshahi is a city on the Padma river, famous for mangoes and silk. It is also a city where, every year, the river takes away land. In 2017, when I was fourteen, the river took the house of my grandparents in a single night. My family took them in, and we lived nine people in three rooms for two years. My father worked double shifts to buy land for a new house.

This experience made me interested in economics: how families lose everything in one day, and how long it takes to recover.

I studied economics in Dhaka with a scholarship. During my studies I worked as a tutor and in a research project on microinsurance for farmers. I finished as the best student of my year. In Neuchâtel, my GPA is 3.94.

My thesis is about climate insurance for poor households in river regions. After the master, I want to do a PhD and work on policies that protect families like my grandparents.
