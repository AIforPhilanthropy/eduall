## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My story is simple to tell: I am twenty-nine, Palestinian, born in Gaza, raised in Fribourg since I was fourteen.

My parents never went to school. My father was a fisherman until the sea was closed to him. We came to Switzerland in 2011, when my father needed medical treatment.

I studied geosciences in Fribourg and worked for six years in a geotechnical office. For years I volunteered as a tutor for young refugees, and I help organise the Palestinian community association in Fribourg.

My doctoral research is on groundwater in coastal aquifers, which in Gaza has become salty and undrinkable. I want to understand how to protect coastal water for the future.
