## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Materials matter because they decide what survives. I grew up in Nablus and came to Fribourg at fifteen with my father, a civil engineer who had been offered work here. In 2016 he was killed in a construction accident on a site in Bulle. I was eighteen.

After his death, I left school and worked for many years in metal workshops and on construction sites to support my mother and my younger brother. I learned welding, cutting, and how steel behaves under stress. I also learned that the scaffolding that killed my father had failed because of corroded joints.

At twenty-seven I went back to school and began materials science. It is very hard to study again after so many years, but I know why I am here.

I want to work on the safety of construction materials.
