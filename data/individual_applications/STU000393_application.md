The nearest hospital had no surgeon on duty. He survived after a long transfer, but he now walks with a limp.

My parents are both doctors, and they spent months caring for him. I decided then to study medicine.

I came to Zurich in 2025 after finishing school and a year of German. I worked in a café to pay for my German course. Now I study medicine at ETH.

I want to become a trauma surgeon.
