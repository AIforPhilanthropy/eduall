## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am twenty-five, from Sialkot, and for eight years I made footballs.

Sialkot produces most of the world's hand-stitched footballs. I started stitching panels at fourteen in a small workshop, after my father, a labourer, fell ill. Neither of my parents went to school. At eighteen I became a quality inspector in a sports goods factory, responsible for testing materials: leather, synthetic panels, bladders. I became fascinated by why one material lasts and another cracks.

I also volunteered for five years with an organisation that fought child labour in the football industry, the same industry that had taken my own childhood. We visited villages and helped children return to school. I organised evening literacy classes for workers in our neighbourhood.

In 2023 I decided to finally study what I had been learning by hand for so long. I prepared for two years, learned German, passed the ETH entrance exam, and in February 2026, at twenty-four, I sat in my first lecture on materials science.

I want to work on sustainable materials for sporting goods and textiles, and to help Sialkot's industry become cleaner and fairer.
