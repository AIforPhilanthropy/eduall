## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I carry people. That is my job: for four years I have pushed beds and wheelchairs through the corridors of the University Hospital Zurich, at night and on weekends. I carry them to scans, to operating theatres, to the morgue. I have learned that the corridor is where patients are most afraid, and that a few words from the man pushing the bed can change their night.

I grew up in Fès. My father was a teacher, my mother a nurse. They always said that health is the first wealth. Before coming to Switzerland I worked for two years as a health promoter for a regional NGO, going village to village in the Middle Atlas, teaching about clean water, vaccination and nutrition.

In Zurich I help at the Moroccan cultural association, organising health information evenings in Arabic, because I saw how many older migrants do not understand the Swiss health system.

Public health is where all of this comes together: the village, the hospital corridor, the community. After my degree I want to work in health promotion for migrant communities in Switzerland.
