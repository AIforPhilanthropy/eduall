## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, in Lagos, the power went out every evening. My brother and I lay on the roof of our building and looked at the stars. He named them with made-up names, and I asked why they did not fall. Nobody could tell me.

My parents were traders who never went to school. I left school at sixteen and married young, and in 2011 I came to Switzerland with my spouse. For many years I stayed at home. At night, when the house was quiet, I studied physics from online courses and borrowed books, and I completed the maturité through evening classes.

At thirty-eight I began physics at ETH. Every lecture is a gift I waited more than twenty years for.

I would like to specialise in astrophysics, and to help young people in Nigeria discover science.
