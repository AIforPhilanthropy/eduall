After that, nothing.

I also have to explain why I have never worked. My engineering programme in Bern is demanding, and I have used all my time for my courses and for the African Students' Association. I know I could have worked some hours, and I regret not having built up any experience.

My degree ends in July 2028. I am applying so that I do not graduate with an empty account.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What would you do if your school had no electricity? In my village in the Northern Region of Ghana, we studied under kerosene lamps until I was fifteen. When the national grid finally reached us, I watched the first bulb light up in our classroom, and the whole class cheered. I decided that day to become an electrical engineer.

My parents are farmers who never went to school. When I was ten, my mother died in childbirth, because the clinic had no power for the lights and no fridge for the medicines. My aunt raised me.

I was the first girl from my village to go to university. In Bern I have been active in the African Students' Association, organising mentoring for first-year students and a yearly conference on energy in Africa.

My bachelor's thesis is on solar-powered cold storage for rural clinics.
