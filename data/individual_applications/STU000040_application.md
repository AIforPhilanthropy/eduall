## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I come from Bhola, an island in the south of Bangladesh, where the big rivers go to the sea. It is a beautiful place, green and full of water, but every year the river eats land. When I was a girl, our school moved two times because the river took the old building. Many families from my village lost their houses and moved to the slums of Dhaka.

My parents were farmers and could not read. I was the first in my family to finish school and go to college in Barisal. I became active in student politics for an opposition party, and in 2012, after I was arrested and threatened, I had to leave Bangladesh. I came to Switzerland and asked for asylum. I was recognised as a refugee.

The first years were very hard. I did not speak German, I lived in an asylum centre, then I worked in kitchens of restaurants and as a cleaner in a hospital. I learned German slowly. Later I did a bachelor in geography at the University of Bern, part-time next to work, and in 2025 I started the master.

My thesis is about river erosion and migration in the delta of Bangladesh, using satellite images from 30 years. It is my own story, but now in numbers and maps.

After the master I want to work in climate adaptation, for an organisation which helps people in river delta regions. I know what they lose, and I know that they are not only victims, they are very strong people.

For me, education gave me a new life after I lost the old one.
