## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years, I want to be a lawyer who helps Roma women in Switzerland and in Romania. Many of them do not know their rights, especially in family law, and nobody speaks with them in their language.

I grew up in a Roma neighbourhood in Craiova. At school, teachers put us in separate classes. My parents fought for me to go to the normal class. I finished high school, and I studied pedagogy, but I could never work as a teacher because of discrimination.

For many years I led a women's group in our neighbourhood, organising literacy courses and health information. In Geneva I continued: I coordinate a small association of Roma women, and we meet every month.

At thirty-nine I started to study law. My French was good enough for daily life, but not for law. I passed every year, sometimes with the minimum.

I want to be the lawyer that my mother never had.
