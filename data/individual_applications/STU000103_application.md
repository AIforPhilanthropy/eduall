## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Deir ez-Zor is a city on the Euphrates, in the east of Syria. When I was a child, it had bridges, markets and date palms. By the time I was twelve, it had become a battlefield, besieged for three years. We lived with little food and no electricity. My father drove a taxi when he could. My mother kept us alive. Neither of them had gone to school beyond primary level.

In 2017, when I was thirteen, my uncle took me to Beirut, where I finished school and worked in a shop after classes. In 2021, at seventeen, I came to Switzerland to study. I learned French in one year, and then started nursing.

During the siege, I saw neighbours die from injuries that simple care could have treated. I decided early that I wanted to learn to heal.

In Lausanne, I work in a nursing home and I also help run a support group for young Syrian men in Lausanne, many of whom arrived alone and struggle with loneliness and memories.

After graduation I want to work in surgical and trauma nursing. Maybe one day I will work in Deir ez-Zor again, when it is safe, to help rebuild its hospital.
