I should also be honest that I could work more hours than I currently do. I have chosen not to during the first year of the master's, because the ETH programme is demanding and I want to build a solid foundation. From the second year, I plan to increase my hours.

An EduAll grant would help me through the move and my first master's year.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Engineering matters because it is how ideas become things that people can hold. When I was a child in Herat, the water pump in our street broke every summer. I watched my father and our neighbours try to fix it with wire and plastic bags. I decided that I would learn to build pumps that do not break.

I came to Switzerland at eleven with my father, who worked in a Zurich bakery, and I grew up here. When I was seventeen, my mother died of cancer. My father later returned to Afghanistan to care for his parents, and I stayed in Zurich alone to finish my studies.

I completed my bachelor's in mechanical engineering at ETH. My bachelor thesis on low-cost water pumps was published as a conference paper, and a second paper followed from a semester project on heat pumps. During my bachelor I led the student team of ETH's humanitarian engineering association, which designed a solar pump for a village in Nepal.

I also help younger Afghan students in Zurich with mathematics and physics, and I translate for Afghan families at school meetings.

After my master's, I want to work on water and energy systems for regions with weak infrastructure. I still remember the pump in our street.
