## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

Let me tell you everything, even the parts that might not help me.

My costs are CHF 2,208 a month. I work in a hospital laboratory nine hours a week and earn CHF 964. But my housing is breaking apart. I live in a room that belongs to a church family who took me in when I arrived. They are moving to another canton in December, and I have nowhere to go. Every studio in Lausanne asks for three months' deposit. I have no savings.

My master's ends in July 2028. I just need to get there with a roof over my head.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years I want to run a laboratory in northern Nigeria that tests water and food for contamination, a place that tells people the truth about what they drink and eat.

My father was a pharmacist in Maiduguri, and my mother a teacher. In 2014, when I was twelve, Boko Haram attacked our town. My father was killed. My mother and I fled south, and in 2017 we came to Switzerland; we were recognised as refugees in 2019.

I finished school here in French, a language I had to learn from nothing, and studied life sciences at EPFL. I have worked since sixteen. At EPFL I was elected president of the African Students' Association, and I help run a homework club for refugee children in Renens.

My mother cries when she sees my lab coat. She says my father would be proud.
