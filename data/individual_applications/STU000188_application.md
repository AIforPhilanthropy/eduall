## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

For four years I worked night shifts in a bakery in Cluj before I could afford to study physics. I learned to live on very little, and I learned to count. So I will count honestly here.

My partner, a pharmacist in Zurich, earns around CHF 9,500 a month and pays CHF 1,430 of my costs. I earn CHF 753 as a teaching assistant for first-year physics. My costs are CHF 2,217. I have CHF 8,883 in savings. There is no monthly gap. I am not in hardship, and I do not want to pretend I am.

Why write, then? Because I have spent my whole adult life fighting for independence, and now, at thirty-one, I depend on my partner for most of my living costs. My thesis semester starts in October, full-time in the lab, and my teaching contract ends. My savings are for my doctoral applications and the months between degrees, when there will be nothing.

If the committee decides that others need this more, and I suspect they do, I will understand, and I will be glad for them.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

The bakery where I worked opened at three in the morning. Between batches of bread I read physics books from the library: Feynman, Landau, anything I could find. My co-workers laughed at me. The baker, an old man called Domnul Pop, did not. He said: "Bread needs heat and time. So do you."

My parents are both engineers, but when I was nineteen our family collapsed: my father lost his job and began drinking, my mother fell ill, and I left my first attempt at university to earn money. I stayed at the bakery for four years, and then worked another year in a warehouse to save money. During that time I helped organise the Cluj food-sharing network, collecting unsold bread from bakeries for shelters. Every night, the bread that was not sold went somewhere it was needed.

At twenty-four I finally returned to university, finished my bachelor's in physics in Cluj, and in 2025 came to ETH for the master's. My thesis is in quantum optics, and my first paper was published this year.

I want to do a doctorate and then teach. I want to be for someone what Domnul Pop was for me.
