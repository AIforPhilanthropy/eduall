## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Why do buildings fall? I asked this question in April 2013, when the Rana Plaza collapsed near Dhaka and more than a thousand garment workers died. I was twelve. My aunt worked on the fourth floor, and she was pulled out alive after two days. Somebody, somewhere, had not tested enough.

My father was a civil engineer and my mother a teacher. They always supported my studies. I studied chemistry in Dhaka and worked in a cement testing laboratory during my last year. In 2022 I married, and the same year I followed my husband to Switzerland.

In Lausanne I first worked in a bakery and learned French. I also volunteered for two years with an association that teaches cooking and nutrition to newly arrived families.

In 2025 I entered EPFL, which was a dream for me. My results are good, and my bachelor thesis is on low-carbon concrete.

After my bachelor I want to do a master in materials science and work on safe and sustainable building materials, maybe one day in Bangladesh, where the construction boom is enormous and the controls are weak.
