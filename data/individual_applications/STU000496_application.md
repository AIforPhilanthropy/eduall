## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

My costs every month are CHF 2,452. The rent is CHF 1,217 for a studio in Basel, food is CHF 375, health insurance CHF 350, fees CHF 142, and utilities CHF 140. The rest is transport, phone and small things.

My parents in Alexandria send me CHF 250 each month. Their household income is about CHF 1,120, from my father's workshop and my mother's shop. I do not have a job. Each month CHF 2,202 is missing.

I want to be honest: I have savings of CHF 13,252. My parents gave me this money when I came to Switzerland, to cover the whole master. They sold a piece of land for it. Until now I have used it for the monthly gap. What is left covers about one more year.

So why I apply? Because I want to continue with a PhD, and the savings are the only thing my parents have given me for my whole education. My father is 63 and his workshop is slower now. If I can keep part of the savings, I can use them for the time between master and PhD, when I have no income yet.

I understand that my situation is not the worst. I write honestly so you can decide.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am 26 years old and I come from Alexandria in Egypt. I study materials science because I like to know why things break.

My father has a small workshop for repairing car parts, and my mother has a shop for household goods. They did not go to university. When I was a child, I spent afternoons in the workshop and watched my father weld. He could see from the colour of the metal if it was good or bad. I wanted to know the reason.

I studied materials engineering at Alexandria University, and I was the first in my family with a university degree. In 2025 I was admitted to the master in Basel. I have passed 6 courses, and my average is very high. Many of my professors told me I should do a PhD, and I have started to apply.

My thesis is about corrosion of steel in sea water. Alexandria is on the sea, and I saw all my life how metal is eaten by the salt air.

The first year in Switzerland was lonely. I do not have family here, and German is new for me, but my courses are in English, so this is fine.

For me education is the answer to the questions I asked in my father's workshop.
