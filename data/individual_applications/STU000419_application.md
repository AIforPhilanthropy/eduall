## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Biology matters because life is fragile and connected. My grandmother kept bees in a village near Plovdiv. When I was a child, she let me hold a honeycomb and told me: "If the bees die, we die." In the last years, her bees died every winter.

My parents were teachers. I studied biology in Plovdiv but married young and followed my husband, first to Germany, then to Zurich. For twenty years I raised two children and did not work. I was active in the parents' association of their schools and in the Bulgarian community.

Both are adults now: they finished their apprenticeships, work, and live in their own flats. When the younger one moved out, I started biology again, at forty-two. German scientific language is still difficult for me.

My thesis is on the decline of wild bees in alpine meadows.
