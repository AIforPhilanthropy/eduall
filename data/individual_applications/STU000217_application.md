## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was nine, my grandmother fell ill with a fever that would not break. The local clinic gave her antibiotics, and nothing changed. Only when my father drove her to a hospital in Ho Chi Minh City did doctors discover the bacterium was resistant. She survived. I kept asking why the first medicine had not worked.

My parents never went to university. They started their business from a single shop and built it through years of work. They taught me patience and precision.

I studied biotechnology in Ho Chi Minh City and worked three years as a laboratory technician in a hospital microbiology department, where I contributed to a published study on resistant bacteria.

At UZH I study how bacteria become resistant to antibiotics at the molecular level. I want to contribute to the development of new antibiotics, and to return to Vietnam to strengthen laboratory diagnostics.
