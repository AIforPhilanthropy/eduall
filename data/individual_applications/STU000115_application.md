## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first week at the university in Lucerne, in September 2025, I sat in the big lecture hall and the professor said: "Health is not only medicine, it is how society is organised." I almost cried. This sentence explained my whole life to me.

I was born in Makassar, in Indonesia. My father was a fisherman who died at sea when I was six. My mother worked as a domestic worker, and nobody in my family studied. In 2012, when I was eighteen, my mother married a Swiss man she met through work, and we came to Lucerne.

It was very hard for me. I did not speak German. I felt lost, and soon I became ill, a long illness that took me many years to recover from. I spent a lot of time in clinics. There, I saw how much the way people are treated depends on money, language and where they come from.

When I became healthy again, I learned German, did the passerelle, and at thirty-one I started university.

I want to work in health promotion for migrants, especially women who don't speak the language and who are alone, like my mother and I were.
