## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was ten, I worked in the summer at my uncle's pharmacy in Alexandria, sorting medicine boxes. I asked him how a little pill can stop a fever. He said: "Biology." Since then, I wanted to understand life at the small scale.

My parents were teachers. I studied pharmacy technology in Alexandria and worked eleven years in pharmacies. In 2016 I came to Switzerland with my spouse. It was a very hard start: no language, no friends, my diploma not recognised. I volunteered at a Red Cross centre, helping Arabic-speaking families with health questions.

At forty I started biology in Zurich. It is hard, but I love it.

After my bachelor I want to work in a laboratory for infectious diseases.
