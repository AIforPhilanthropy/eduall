## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Kaolack is a city of salt and peanuts, in the heart of Senegal. The salt flats shine white in the sun. My mother worked there, carrying salt on her head for twenty years. My father was a teacher, and he taught me to read before I went to school.

At twelve, my father died. My mother kept us alive with the salt. I left school for two years to help her, then went back and finished. After teacher training I worked for two years as a primary school teacher in Kaolack, and I coordinated the reading programme of the lower grades, with five teachers.

In 2024 I came to Bern to study education, to learn how to teach children who do not speak the school language, like so many in Senegal, where school is in French and home is in Wolof.

My thesis compares how Swiss and Senegalese schools support multilingual children.
