## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

I am a doctoral student in Biology at the University of Fribourg, in my third year, studying how alpine plants respond to warming. My doctoral position has no salary: my only income is a teaching assistant contract of about 8 hours a week, which pays CHF 932 a month, and my research time is unfunded. My fieldwork takes place every summer at high-altitude sites in the Valais Alps and requires travel, accommodation in mountain huts and equipment, costs that my department covers only in part. An EduAll grant would allow me to complete two more field seasons without asking my parents for additional support.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My grandmother kept a garden on the slopes above Brașov, where she grew plants she had collected from the mountains over fifty years. When I was small, she knew every one by name and told me which flowered first in spring. In the last years before she died, she noticed that they were flowering earlier and earlier. "The mountain is in a hurry," she said.

That sentence became my research question. I studied biology in Cluj and worked two years as a research technician on a Carpathian biodiversity project, where I coordinated a field team of five students. I have co-authored two papers on alpine plant phenology.

My doctoral research uses long-term observations and experiments in the Swiss Alps to predict how mountain plant communities will shift under climate change.

I hope to continue in research and, one day, lead a monitoring programme for mountain ecosystems in the Carpathians.
