## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Why did my cousin die from dengue in 2019, in a big city, with a hospital two kilometres away? This question changed my life. I was sixteen. The hospital was full, there was no bed, and the family waited too long. It was not a medical failure, it was a failure of the system.

I studied public health in Lahore and during my studies I took part in dengue prevention campaigns organised by my faculty. After the floods of 2022, I worked for six months with an NGO in Sindh, organising health camps in villages under water. I saw children with diarrhoea, skin diseases, malnutrition. I saw also how much a clean water point can change.

In Zurich I help in the Pakistani student community, and I organised this year a health information evening for new students about the Swiss health insurance.

My thesis is about early warning systems for dengue outbreaks using climate data. After my master, I want to work for public health institutions in South Asia, where climate change will make these diseases worse every year.
