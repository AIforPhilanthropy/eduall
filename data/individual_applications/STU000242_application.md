## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I came to Fribourg at sixteen, to live with my uncle, after my mother died of kidney failure in Dhaka. She needed dialysis, and we could not afford it regularly. I was fifteen when she died. I have wanted to understand how the kidney works, and fails, ever since.

My father is an engineer, and he sent me to Switzerland because he believed I would have a better chance here. The first years were very hard: a new language, a new country, grief. I learned French, finished the collège, and studied biochemistry at the University of Fribourg while working nights.

For many years I have volunteered with a kidney patients' association in Fribourg, helping organise transport to dialysis for older patients who cannot drive.

My master's research focuses on proteins involved in kidney disease. I want to continue to a PhD and work in biomedical research on chronic kidney disease, which affects millions of people in South Asia who cannot afford treatment.
