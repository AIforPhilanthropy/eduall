## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

I study Biology (Microbiology) at the University of Lausanne, part-time until now. From September 2027 I will study full-time, so that I graduate in July 2029 instead of July 2030.

My monthly costs are CHF 2,001, with a room share of CHF 913. I work about 12 hours a week as a laboratory assistant in a food-testing company and earn CHF 1,333. My wife, who is a nurse at a hospital in Morges and earns around CHF 5,700, contributes CHF 930 to my costs.

When I study full-time, I will keep only my Saturday shifts at the company, about CHF 1,240 a month. On paper my budget still closes. But the full-time years include long laboratory courses and my bachelor thesis in a research laboratory, and at forty-five, one year less of study is one year more of work as a microbiologist. A grant from EduAll would give me the margin to do this well and to complete my degree with a strong thesis.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was a child in Agadir, my father took me fishing on Sundays. One summer, the fish in the bay died by the thousands. The water was red. The fishermen said it was a curse. My father, a pharmacist, said it was "microbes" and that nobody would test them. I remember thinking that someone should.

I studied biology in Agadir and worked for many years as a laboratory technician in fish-processing plants and later in a public health laboratory. In 2019 I came to Switzerland with my wife. My Moroccan diploma was not recognised, and at forty-two I decided to start again with a Swiss bachelor.

For many years I have also volunteered with an association that offers free first-aid courses to newly arrived migrants in Lausanne.

My bachelor thesis is on harmful algal blooms in Lake Geneva. After my degree I want to work in environmental microbiology or food safety.
