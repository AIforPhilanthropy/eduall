## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years I want to run a teacher-training centre in central Vietnam, for teachers who work in poor rural schools. Many of them have almost no training and teach forty children with one book.

My parents never went to university. They have sold noodles in the same small shop in Da Nang since before I was born. They wanted me to have a different life, and they put everything into my schooling.

After secondary school I worked for three years, teaching English in a language centre and guiding tourists in Hoi An. In my last year at the language centre I coordinated the children's programme, with eight teachers. I also organised free English classes on Sundays for children from fishing families in our neighbourhood.

In 2025 I came to Geneva to study Education. It is taught in French, and I learned French for two years before. My GPA is 3.0. In Geneva I am a member of the Vietnamese student association, and I help new students with the university administration.

My thesis compares how Swiss and Vietnamese primary schools support children who are struggling. I hope that what I learn here can help teachers at home.

For me, education is the best gift my parents gave me. I want to pass it on.
