## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first week in Fribourg, the lift to the chemistry laboratory was broken. Four students I did not know carried me and my wheelchair up two floors. Since then, they are my friends.

I have spinal muscular atrophy since childhood. In India, many schools did not accept me. My parents, both teachers, fought for me to go to school. They carried me up stairs for years.

During my bachelor in Pune I was the president of the students' disability association, and we made the university install ramps and lifts.

French is new for me, and I still find it difficult, but my laboratory work is in English.

My thesis is on polymers for medical devices. I want to work in research on better assistive technologies.
