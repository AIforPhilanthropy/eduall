## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

My situation is: my costs are CHF 1,943 per month, and my parents send me CHF 350. The gap is only CHF 1,593. I want to say honestly that this is small.

My parents are seafood traders in Hai Phong. They never went to university, and they work at the port from four in the morning. Their income is around CHF 1,310 per month, and they send me more than a quarter of it. My younger brother also starts university next year, and they will have to pay for him too. I know that they cannot continue to send me this much.

I do not work now. I tried to find a job in my first year, but my German was not enough. I also have no savings.

My master ends in July 2028. I apply so that my parents can reduce what they send in my last semester, and use it for my brother.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother told me once that the day I was born, a typhoon hit Hai Phong, and the water came into the hospital. She held me above the water on a table. She always says: "You were born fighting."

When I was twelve, my father's boat sank in a storm. He survived, but the boat and the cargo were lost, and my parents had a big debt for many years. We moved to a smaller house, and my mother worked night and day at the market. I studied very hard, because I knew that was the only way out.

I studied business in Hanoi with a scholarship and came to Bern for the master. My GPA is 2.93; the first semester in a new language and system was difficult, but my results improve every semester.

My thesis is about small family businesses in the seafood trade and how they manage risk. I want to work in trade finance and help small traders like my parents get access to insurance and credit.
