## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I want to write honestly, with every number.

For two years in Kumasi I worked in a malaria research lab as a technician, and I saved money for my master. I have CHF 5,300 of it left. I also receive a scholarship of CHF 5,731 per year from a Swiss foundation for African scientists, and I work at ETH as a teaching assistant, earning CHF 929 per month. My father, a cocoa farmer, gives me CHF 180, a large share of the roughly CHF 760 the household has.

My costs are CHF 2,093 per month. So the real gap, after my scholarship, is small, and my savings cover it now.

But there is a new problem. In July my mother fell very ill with kidney failure. She needs dialysis, which is not free in Ghana. My father has sold part of his land, and I am sending my savings home. They will not last for my thesis semester.

My master ends in July 2028. I apply so that I can send my savings to my mother and still finish my studies.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In the malaria lab in Kumasi, I dissected mosquitoes every day, hundreds of them. The work was repetitive, but every mosquito told us something about how the parasite survives. I wanted to understand more than we could see with our old microscopes.

My parents are cocoa farmers who never went to school. I was the first in my family to finish secondary school, and the first to go to university.

In Zurich I help organise the African students' community at ETH, especially events for new students.

My thesis is about how malaria parasites resist drugs. My GPA is 3.86, and I co-authored one article. After my master, I want to do a PhD and go back to Ghana to lead research on malaria.
