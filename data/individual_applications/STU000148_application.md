I am applying so that I can concentrate on passing those exams and finishing my degree in January 2030, rather than adding working hours at the most critical moment.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am twenty-six, born in Cusco, raised between the Andes and Zurich, and I have loved rocks since before I could read.

My grandfather took me on walks above Cusco and showed me the stones of the Inca walls, cut so precisely that no knife fits between them. He told me each stone had a story millions of years old. My father, an engineer, died when I was eight. My mother came to Zurich in 2010, when I was ten, to marry my stepfather, and I followed her.

School in Switzerland was hard at first. I learned German, fought my way through secondary school, and worked in shops from the age of sixteen. I also volunteered for four years with a mountain rescue youth group, which taught me to read terrain and to stay calm in danger.

My geology bachelor has been demanding, and my results are uneven. I have strong fieldwork and mapping skills, and weaker results in theoretical courses. My thesis maps landslide risk in a valley in Graubünden.

I want to specialise in natural hazards in mountain regions, in Switzerland and in the Andes, where landslides destroy villages every rainy season.
