## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I was born in the Balata camp, near Nablus, and I have lived in camps, rented rooms and borrowed apartments most of my life. When I was fourteen, during a raid on the camp, our house was demolished. My family moved in with relatives, fourteen people in three rooms. I learned then that a home is not walls. It is the people who hold you.

As a teenager I volunteered with women's organisations in the camps, running literacy circles for younger girls, and I began sociology in Nablus. In 2019 I came to Switzerland, and after three years of German courses, at twenty-three, I began my bachelor's at the University of Zurich.

German is still very hard for me, especially in the academic readings and exams. But in the interviews for my thesis, I have found my strength: women open their hearts to me because they know I have lived what they have lived.

Every Wednesday I volunteer with a refugee women's group in Zurich, where I help organise language exchange and support circles.

After my degree, I want to work in social integration, helping refugee women build networks in their new countries. A home is the people who hold you. I want to help people find each other.
