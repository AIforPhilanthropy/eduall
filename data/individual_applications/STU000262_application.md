## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was a child in Tamale, my father, who was a mathematics teacher, drew the planets in the sand behind our house with a stick. He told me that the same laws which make a mango fall make the moon go around the Earth. I thought it was magic. He said it was physics.

My father died when I was fourteen. My mother raised four children with her sewing machine.

I came to Bern at twenty-three, with a scholarship for the first year from a church. German was very difficult. I understood almost nothing in the first semester. I worked as a cleaner in offices in the evening to live, and after two years the company made me a team supervisor for eight cleaners, because I was reliable.



My thesis is about laser spectroscopy. After my bachelor, I want to do a master in physics, and then teach physics in Ghana, like my father.
