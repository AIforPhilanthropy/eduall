## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, my grandfather took me to see the old town of Plovdiv. He stopped in front of a house from the 19th century, painted blue, with a wooden balcony that hung over the street. He said: "Somebody built this so that it would be beautiful for two hundred years." I did not forget that.

I did not go straight to architecture. After school I trained as a construction technician and worked for six years, first on building sites in Sofia and later in Switzerland, as a site foreman for a construction company in the Vaud region. I led teams of up to eight workers. I learned how buildings are really made, and also how often they are built badly and fast.

In 2025, at twenty-six, I started architecture at EPFL. Most students came straight from school, but my experience helps me in the studio. My grades are average so far, the theory courses are hard for me, but my studio projects are going well.

I also help organise a monthly repair workshop for the neighbourhood in Renens, and I give free advice on small renovation projects to families who cannot pay an architect.

I want to design affordable housing that people will still like to live in after fifty years. For me education means finally being able to draw what I have spent six years building.
