My budget this year works: my parents, both doctors in Timișoara with a household income of around CHF 4,370, send me CHF 940 a month, I earn CHF 770 as a research assistant, and my costs in Bern are CHF 1,502.

In May my younger sister, who is fourteen, was diagnosed with leukaemia. She is being treated in Vienna, because the specialised therapy she needs is not available in Romania. My parents insist on continuing to support me. I want to reduce that burden as much as I can during my final year, so that the money can go where it is needed most.

A grant from EduAll would let me finish my bachelor's degree in July 2028 without taking from my sister's treatment.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I started studying psychology at seventeen, the youngest in my year, after finishing school early in Timișoara. My parents are doctors, and I grew up with medical conversations at dinner, but I was always more interested in the patients than in the illnesses: why one person recovers and another gives up.

In Bern I work as a research assistant in a lab studying resilience in adolescents with chronic illness. I code interviews, run questionnaires and help with data analysis. Now, the research has become personal: I see my sister's fear on video calls and recognise what we study.

My thesis looks at how siblings of seriously ill children cope, a group often forgotten by health systems.

After my bachelor I want to do a master's in clinical psychology and specialise in paediatric psycho-oncology.
