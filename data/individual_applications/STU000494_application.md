## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. For four years I volunteered every Saturday in a women's shelter in Lahore, where I did the bookkeeping and helped women to open their first bank account. I learned there that money is not only numbers, it is freedom. So I want to be open about my own money.

I am in the first year of the Master in Business Administration at University of Bern, finishing in July 2028. My costs are CHF 2,292 each month. Rent is CHF 1,166, food CHF 349, health insurance CHF 320, fees CHF 158, and the rest utilities, transport, phone and books.

I work as a part-time bookkeeper for a small company and I earn CHF 521. My sister in Birmingham sends CHF 591 from her household income of about CHF 2,500. So every month CHF 1,180 is missing.

I have CHF 12,570 in savings. I must explain them. This money is from the sale of my jewellery after my divorce and my share of my late father's house. It is all I own. I have used it for the gap since I started the master, and I will need what is left for the time after graduation, until I find a job and can pay a deposit for a flat of my own.

A grant from EduAll would let me finish without using everything I have left.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I was born in Lahore. My father was a bank officer and my mother a school teacher. I studied commerce and worked for two years as an accountant in a textile company. I was the only woman in the finance department.

In 2018 I married and moved to Switzerland with my husband. The marriage was not good. For three years I was not allowed to work or to learn German, and I had no money of my own. In 2021 I left, with the help of a women's organisation in Bern. I stayed some months in a shelter, and then I started again: German courses, a job as a cleaner, then as a bookkeeper.

In 2026, at thirty-two, I started the master. My German is still not perfect, but my numbers are. I have passed 3 courses. My grades are good, GPA 3.22, and I am proud, because I study with a very tired head some days.

Since 2022 I volunteer with the same women's organisation that helped me. I give workshops about budgets and bank accounts for women who are in the situation I was in.

After my degree I want to work in the finance department of a non-profit organisation.

For me education means that I will never again depend on someone else for my own life.
