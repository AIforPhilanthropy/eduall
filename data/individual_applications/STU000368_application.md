## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Computer science matters to me because it can make information reach people who have none. When my family arrived in Zurich in 2014, my parents struggled for months with forms, letters and official websites in German. I was fourteen, and I became the family's translator and IT support. I learned that technology can be a wall or a door.

At school I founded a coding club for newcomer children, and since 2020 I have volunteered teaching digital skills to older migrants at a community centre. At the university, I was elected president of the computer science students' association, organising hackathons for social projects.

My thesis builds a multilingual chatbot that helps refugees understand official letters.

After my degree I want to work on accessible digital public services.
