## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Microbiology matters because it is where medicine meets the invisible. When I was twelve, shortly after my family moved from Iași to Geneva, my grandmother in Romania died of a hospital infection after a routine operation. The bacteria were resistant to every antibiotic the hospital had. I did not understand how something so small could be so powerful.

For six years I have volunteered with the Red Cross youth section in Geneva, first in first-aid training and later in a programme accompanying elderly migrants to medical appointments. I also help organise the Romanian community's cultural association, where I coordinate Romanian lessons for children.

During my bachelor's in biology, I did a research project in a laboratory on bacterial biofilms, which led me to my current research interests.

After my master's, I plan to pursue a PhD on antimicrobial resistance, and to contribute to infection control in Eastern European hospitals.
