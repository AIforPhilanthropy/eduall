## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first winter in Fribourg, in 2022, I saw snow for the first time. I stood in the old town at night and caught snowflakes on my glove, and looked at them with a magnifying glass. Each one was a perfect crystal. I thought: this is physics.

My father teaches mathematics and my mother is a nurse. They always encouraged me. I finished school early and came to Fribourg at seventeen.

French and German were both new languages for me. I still read physics texts slowly in French.

During my studies I have tutored younger students, and for two years I was captain of the university's African students' football team, which I also organised.

My thesis is about the physics of crystal growth. After my bachelor, I want to do a master in materials physics.
