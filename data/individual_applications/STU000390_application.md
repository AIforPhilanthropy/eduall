I want to explain my situation honestly. I started the Bachelor in Educational Sciences at University of Neuchâtel in February 2026, part-time.

My costs are CHF 2,016 per month. My room costs CHF 739. I work about 22 hours a week as a teaching assistant in an after-school programme and in a school canteen, and earn CHF 2,502. My brother, a nurse in Ramallah, still sends CHF 130, although the whole household lives on roughly CHF 880. I told him to stop, and I feel very bad about this.

So my costs are covered. But French is my third language and I need a lot of time to read and write, and after my work I am very tired. I finish in January 2032. The grant would help me not to depend on my brother, and to work a little less when the placements begin.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In Ramallah I was an English teacher for eight years in a girls' school. My parents are both teachers too; in our house, education was like bread.

I came to Switzerland in 2024, when my husband found work in Neuchâtel. My Palestinian teaching diploma is not recognised here, so I must study again. It is not easy to be a student at thirty-two, with students who are eighteen. But my first semester went very well; my GPA is 3.74.

French is my big challenge. I understand, but writing academic texts takes me three times longer.

I want to teach children who arrive without the language, because I know now how it feels.
