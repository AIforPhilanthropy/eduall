## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years, I want to lead a laboratory for tuberculosis diagnosis in Senegal. In my country, too many people die from TB because the diagnosis comes too late.

My parents are both teachers in Saint-Louis. When I was twenty, my younger brother died of tuberculosis. He was diagnosed after four months of cough. The laboratory in our town had no equipment for the modern tests. I studied biology, and I worked seven years as a laboratory technician at the regional hospital.

I also volunteered in a community programme that visited families of TB patients to explain treatment and prevention. And I helped organise the women's association of my neighbourhood.

In 2025 I came to Geneva for my master's. My thesis is about rapid diagnostic tests for tuberculosis. I co-authored one article with my supervisor.

After my master's I want to go back and build the laboratory my brother needed.
