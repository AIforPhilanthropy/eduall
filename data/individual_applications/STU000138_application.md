## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What do you do after the ground itself has betrayed you? In September 2018, an earthquake and liquefaction in Palu swallowed whole neighbourhoods. My house, my street and my older brother were among them. I survived because I was at the market with my mother. I was eighteen.

For two years I lived in a temporary shelter camp. I worked as an assistant coordinator for an NGO distributing aid, and I became the person other women came to at night, when they could not sleep, when their children screamed in dreams. I had no training. I just listened. I also helped organise a women's group in the camp, which is still active today.

My father was a teacher and my mother a nurse; they always said I should study. The earthquake came one month after I finished school.

In 2023 I came to Switzerland with a scholarship for a German preparatory programme, and in 2025 I started psychology. I want to become a trauma psychologist. I know what disaster does to the mind, and I know how little help survivors receive.
