## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I came to Fribourg from Chittagong at fourteen, to join my mother, who had worked here as a nurse for eight years while I lived with my grandparents. I did not speak French. I spent my first year in a class for newcomers and cried every day. By the end of school I was one of the best in chemistry.

My father is a teacher in Chittagong. I studied biology in Fribourg, and since 2021 I have worked in a hospital laboratory. I also help organise the Bangladeshi community's youth group, which brings together teenagers who arrived like I did.

My master's thesis is on bacterial infections in hospital patients.

I want to do a PhD in clinical microbiology.
