## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

What does a mountain city do when its water runs out? In Baguio, where I grew up, this is not an abstract question. Every dry season, taps in our neighbourhood run dry for days. Families queue for water trucks. At the same time, the springs that once fed the city have been lost to construction and poor planning.

I was twelve when I first asked my father where the water in the ground comes from. He did not know; neither of my parents went to college. So I went to find out. I studied geology at the University of the Philippines Baguio, where I was the first in my family to graduate.

For four years I worked as a research assistant at a geotechnical company, mapping aquifers and landslide risks in the Cordillera. On weekends I volunteered with a local organisation teaching schoolchildren about water conservation, visiting mountain schools where children walked an hour to reach a water source.

I chose Neuchâtel because of its strength in hydrogeology, and especially karst systems, which are similar in some ways to the limestone formations near Baguio. My studies have gone well: I have a GPA of 3.23 and have completed 3 courses.

After my master's, I want to work on groundwater management for mountain cities in South-East Asia. No child in Baguio should have to queue for water that lies under their feet.
