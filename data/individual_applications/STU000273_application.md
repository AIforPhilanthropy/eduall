## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In my first lecture in Bern, in February 2026, the professor gave me a small microphone to wear. It sends her voice directly to my hearing aids. For the first time in years, I understood every word in a big room. I wanted to cry.

I lost most of my hearing at fourteen, after an explosion near our house in Gaza. In school I could not hear the teacher any more. I taught myself to read lips, and I learned sign language with an association for deaf children, where I helped the younger ones.

In 2016 my family came to Switzerland. I was sixteen, in a new country, with a new language that I had to read from lips. I finished school here and since 2020 I work as an assistant in schools. I also coordinate the youth section of a group for Arabic-speaking deaf people in Bern.

Now, at twenty-five, I study education, to become a teacher here, for children with disabilities. I know what it is to be in a classroom and not understand.
