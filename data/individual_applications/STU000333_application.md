## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

I would like to present my situation fully and honestly.

My monthly expenses are CHF 2,420, the largest item being rent: CHF 1,412 for a studio in Zurich. It is more than I should pay; when I arrived, I could not find a shared flat that would take a woman alone from Pakistan without references, and I am still looking. I earn CHF 836 a month as a laboratory assistant. My younger brother, a factory worker in Faisalabad, gives me CHF 110, a large share of the roughly CHF 450 the household has.

I have CHF 7,427 in savings. They come from a university prize and from my work before coming to Switzerland. I have used them to cover the gap of CHF 1,474 a month, and they will run out around the time I submit my thesis in July 2028. After that, I will have nothing to bridge the months until I find work.

I am applying so that I can finish my master's without spending my last reserve.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother was married at fourteen, and could not read. When I was ten, she sat beside me every evening while I did my homework, following the lines with her finger, trying to learn with me. She never managed. But she made sure I did.

My father was a farm labourer near Faisalabad. When my father died, my mother worked in the fields to keep me in school.

I studied biochemistry at the University of Agriculture on a scholarship and graduated first in my year. I worked for two years as a research assistant, and on weekends I taught science to girls in a village school. In Zurich I help at a Pakistani women's association, which supports newly arrived women.

German is still hard for me in daily life.

My thesis is about enzymes in wheat under heat stress. I want to help farmers like my father grow food in a hotter world.
