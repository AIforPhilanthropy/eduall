## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was ten, my grandmother in Tétouan showed me a key. It was the key to her grandparents' house in Granada, which they had left in 1609, she said, when the Moriscos were expelled from Spain. I did not know if it was true. I have wanted to know ever since.

My parents were both teachers. I studied history in Tétouan and worked for thirteen years as a history teacher in a secondary school, and later as a guide at the city museum. In 2019 I came to Lausanne with my husband.

My master's research reconstructs the lives of Morisco families who settled in northern Morocco after the expulsions, using archives in Spain, France and Morocco. My GPA is 3.54.

After my master's I hope to begin a doctorate and to continue telling the stories of people history tends to forget.
