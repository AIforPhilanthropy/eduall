## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My brother Ahmad was the one who taught me chemistry. He was a pharmacist in Mosul, and when I was a child he let me mix solutions in his shop. He always said I would become a real chemist one day.

We grew up in a family of teachers. In 2014, when ISIS took Mosul, I was in my last year of chemistry studies. I left without finishing. For four years I volunteered with an organisation for displaced families in Erbil, running a water-testing programme in the camps. In 2018 I came to Switzerland with my wife.

I worked in a laboratory supply company and learned French. In 2025 I restarted my chemistry degree from the beginning.

Ahmad did not live to see it. I will finish it for both of us.
