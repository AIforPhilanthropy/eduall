From September 2027 the chemistry curriculum has laboratory courses almost every afternoon, and I will have to reduce my hours to about CHF 1,680, leaving CHF 291 a month uncovered. An EduAll grant would allow me to keep my attention on my studies, where my results so far have shown what I can achieve.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Peshawar is a city of bazaars and dust, of spice markets and brick kilns on the outskirts where entire families work from dawn. My father ran a small stall selling cooking oil in the Qissa Khwani bazaar, and my mother never learned to read. When I was a child, I used to watch the oil turn cloudy in the winter cold and ask my father why. He did not know. I have been asking questions like that ever since.

Neither of my parents went beyond primary school. When I was fifteen, it became clear that I could not continue my education safely at home, and my family sent me to live with my uncle in Basel. I have been here under provisional admission since then: my asylum application was rejected, but as a minor I could not be sent back without anyone to receive me safely. I arrived knowing a little English and no German at all.

Within three years I had learned enough German to pass the university entrance examination, and I began my chemistry studies in the autumn of 2025, aged eighteen. In my first year I completed all eight courses, with results among the best in my cohort. Scientific German is still a daily effort, but I have found that the language of chemistry, formulas and structures, is the same everywhere.

This spring my father was badly injured in a traffic accident and can no longer work at the stall. From a distance, I have helped my family arrange his care, while continuing my studies without interruption.

On Saturdays, I volunteer as a tutor for younger newcomers learning German and mathematics at a youth centre.

I hope to pursue research in green chemistry, perhaps on cleaner processes for small industries like the brick kilns of my childhood. Education, for me, is where every question I asked at my father's stall finally gets an answer.
