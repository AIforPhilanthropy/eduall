I understand that my need is not urgent now. But I want to tell you honestly that the situation can change very fast.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

For six years I worked as a civil engineer in Cairo, in the water sector. I designed pumping stations and pipelines for new neighbourhoods. My parents never went to university, my father was a carpenter and my mother a housewife, and they were very proud of me. But in my work I was always the only woman in the room, and often my ideas were given to a male colleague.

I was also active in an association of women engineers in Cairo, organising workshops for female engineering students, because I wanted the young women to feel less alone than I did.

In 2025, when my husband got a job in Zurich, I decided to do the Master in Civil Engineering at ETH, with focus on water resources. It was very difficult to come back to study after six years of work, and my first semester was hard, my GPA is 3.11. But I learn things I always wanted to learn, like modelling of floods and water networks.

After my master I want to work in water management for cities in dry countries, like Egypt, where every drop is important.

For me, education is a way to finally have my ideas count.
