## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Quezon City is where I grew up: a place of traffic, power cuts, and a thousand small businesses in narrow streets. My parents ran a small printing shop; my father had studied accounting and my mother was a teacher before the shop.

I studied computer science and worked for two years as a systems analyst in a city government IT department. In 2021, I discovered that a procurement system I maintained was being used to channel public funds to fictitious companies. I reported it. Within weeks, I was threatened, my car was vandalised, and a colleague who supported me was attacked. I left the Philippines in 2022 and was granted asylum in Switzerland.

In Basel, I help organise digital literacy workshops for Filipino domestic workers, many of whom struggle with online forms and banking.

My thesis examines methods to detect anomalies in public procurement data. I want to work on transparency and fraud detection in public administration.
