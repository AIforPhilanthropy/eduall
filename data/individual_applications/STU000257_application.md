I am in the second year of the Bachelor's in History at the University of Lausanne, and I will complete my degree in July 2028.

My monthly expenses are CHF 1,903, including CHF 932 in rent for a room I sublet from another student. My grandmother in Kisumu (household income around CHF 730) sends me CHF 209 when she can. I also receive a scholarship of CHF 6,422 a year from a Kenyan community foundation.

My studies in French take all my energy at the moment, and I have focused on them rather than on paid work this year. An EduAll grant would allow me to secure a stable room and continue my degree.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My grandmother raised me in Kisumu after my mother died. She sold fish at the lake shore, and in the evenings she told stories: about her own grandmother, about the colonial chiefs, about the years of independence, about the first time she saw a car. She could not read, but she was the best historian I have ever met.

Nobody in my family went to university. I finished school with a scholarship from a church, and then worked four years as a hotel receptionist in Kisumu, saving for my studies. During those years I volunteered as a guide at the Kisumu Museum and founded a youth history club, which I led for three years, where young people recorded the memories of their grandparents.

In 2025 I came to Lausanne to study history. French is my fourth language, and my first year was very difficult, but I am determined.

I want to become a historian of East Africa, and to make sure that people like my grandmother are part of history, not only witnesses to it.
