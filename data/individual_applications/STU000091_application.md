## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother sells pepper soup at a roadside stand in Port Harcourt. My father died when I was ten, in an oil pipeline fire near our village. After that, we moved to the city with nothing. I helped my mother at the stand before and after school. Nobody in our family had gone to university.

I loved mathematics because it was the one subject where nobody could say I was wrong if I was right. My teacher entered me in the national mathematics olympiad, and I won a bronze medal. With that, a church scholarship paid my secondary school.

After school I worked three years, as a shop assistant and later as a bookkeeper for a hotel, to save money. In 2024 I came to Neuchâtel to study mathematics in French, a language I learned in one intensive year.

My thesis is about statistics for predicting floods. The Niger Delta, where I come from, floods every year.

After my bachelor I want to do a master in statistics and work in risk analysis for disasters.

For me education is the way my father's death does not decide my whole life.
