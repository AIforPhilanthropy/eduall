## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

I am doctoral student in Mathematics at EPFL, starting my third year. For the first two years my position was financed by a fellowship of the Bangladesh government for young researchers, which was fixed for two years only. It ends in August 2026. My supervisor applied for a new project that can pay me, but the decision comes only in spring 2028, and until then the laboratory has no money for my salary. I attach the letter of my supervisor.

Now I have CHF 352 per month from teaching assistant work, and CHF 150 which my older brother sends from Dhaka. He is a bank clerk and his family has around CHF 830 per month. My costs are CHF 2,210, mostly rent of CHF 1,160 for a studio in Renens. I have asthma, severe type, and I cannot live in old humid flats with mould, which are the cheaper ones.

So I miss CHF 1,708 per month. This period is only until the new project starts. EduAll grant will allow me to continue my doctorate without interruption.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother cannot read, but she can calculate the price of rice for a whole month in her head, faster than any calculator. When I was small, I sat next to her at the market in Sunamganj and tried to be faster. I never was. I think I became mathematician because of this game.

My father was a boatman on the haor wetlands. Nobody in my family went to school after primary. I got a scholarship for secondary school in Sylhet and then studied mathematics at Dhaka University. I worked two years as lecturer in a private university before coming to EPFL.

This summer, my family's village in Sunamganj was flooded, the worst flood since 2022. Our house was under water for two weeks. My parents now live with my brother in Dhaka. My brother supports them and me at the same time.

My research is in probability theory, random walks on graphs. It is very abstract, but it has applications in networks and epidemics.

My French is still weak, and in daily life in Lausanne this makes things difficult: administration, doctor, landlord. In the laboratory we speak English.

After my doctorate, I want to go back to Bangladesh and build a research group in probability. There are very few people doing research mathematics in my country.
