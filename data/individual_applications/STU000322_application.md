## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My father was a geography teacher in Accra. On Sunday afternoons he took me to the Korle Lagoon and showed me the black water, the plastic, the smoke from the burning electronics dump at Agbogbloshie. "This used to be where fishermen lived," he said. "Someone has to clean it." He died before anyone did.

I started geography in Legon at eighteen, but I stopped when my father became ill. For eight years I was his carer, his cook, his driver, and I finished my degree part-time. I read everything I could find about pollution and water.

In 2026 I began the Master's in Environmental Science in Bern. My thesis is about heavy metals in urban lagoons in West Africa, and one article with my supervisor has already been published.

I want to work on the restoration of polluted waters in Ghana. It is my father's lagoon.
