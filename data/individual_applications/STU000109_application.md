## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My sister is the reason I am here. When our father died, she was nineteen and I was twelve. She left her own studies to work as a teacher's aide, and she made sure I finished school. She still sends me money every month, from her small salary, with her three children at home.

Nobody in our family went to university before us; our father was a guard at a factory and our mother never went to school. My sister later completed her teaching diploma in the evenings. After school I worked three years as a laboratory assistant in a hospital in Rawalpindi.

In the hospital laboratory, I saw how often infections did not respond to antibiotics. I wanted to understand why. This is why I study microbiology.

In Bern I help at the Pakistani community association. I organise the Urdu Saturday school for children and help new families with registration and insurance.

After my bachelor I want to do a master in infection biology and work in a hospital laboratory, maybe here, maybe in Pakistan. I want to make my sister proud of what she gave up for me.
