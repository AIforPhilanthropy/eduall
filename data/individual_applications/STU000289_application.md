## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Business administration matters to me because I have seen what bad management does to good people. For five years I worked in a tea company in Mombasa, first in the office and then as a supply chain assistant. I saw how small tea farmers were paid late, and how many lost their farms because of bad contracts. My father is a teacher and my mother a nurse, and they always told me to use my education for people.

At my company I wrote a report on payment delays to farmers, which was later published with my manager in a small business journal.

I came to St. Gallen to learn how to build fair supply chains. I want to go back to Kenya and work for a cooperative or a company that pays farmers fairly and on time.
