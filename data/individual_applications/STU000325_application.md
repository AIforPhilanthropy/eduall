## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Nakuru is a city next to a lake of flamingos, but also of slums where cholera comes every year. I grew up in one of them. My parents are day labourers; nobody in my family went to school after primary.

When I was fourteen, my mother became sick with typhoid. We had no money for the hospital. A neighbour paid for it. I promised myself I would study so that my family would never depend on luck again.

I studied microbiology in Nairobi with a scholarship and worked two years as a laboratory technician in a hospital. I saw many infections which did not respond to antibiotics.

My master's research will be about antibiotic resistance. After, I want to go back to Kenya and work in a public health laboratory.
