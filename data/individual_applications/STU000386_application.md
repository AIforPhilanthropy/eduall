What does a woman do when her marriage ends and she is alone in a foreign country, with no language and no degree that counts? I asked myself this question in 2018, in an empty flat in Winterthur.

My parents are farmers in Uttar Pradesh who never went to school. I had studied chemistry in Lucknow before I married, and I came to Switzerland in 2009 with my husband. For years I worked in a laboratory supply company, packing orders. When the marriage ended, I decided to finally use my degree.

I learned German properly, completed bridging courses, and in 2026 started the master. My GPA is 3.61. German scientific language is still harder for me than for my classmates.

My thesis is on catalysts for cleaner pharmaceutical production. I want to work in the pharmaceutical industry in Basel.
