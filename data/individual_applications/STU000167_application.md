I study Medicine in the St. Gallen track of the joint medical programme of the University of St. Gallen and the University of Zurich, and I finish my bachelor in January 2028.

My costs are CHF 1,874 per month. My room in Zurich costs CHF 773, and the tuition is CHF 203 per month. My parents, who own a small printing shop in Surabaya with income of about CHF 1,250, send me CHF 190 every month. They work very hard for this.

The grant from EduAll would help me finish my bachelor and continue to the master without asking my parents for more.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Medicine is important for me because in my family, doctors were always far away. My grandmother died of a stroke in our village near Surabaya, because the nearest hospital was two hours away and nobody recognised the signs. My parents did not study, they are printers, and they always told me: "Become the person who knows."

I came to Switzerland at twenty-two, after one year of German in Indonesia. The first years were very hard. Medical German is like a third language. I still study with dictionaries every evening, and I have passed all my exams.

I worked for three years as a care assistant in a home for elderly people in Zurich. I learned so much there about patience and dignity. I also volunteer with the Red Cross youth group, giving first aid courses to newly arrived migrants.

After my studies I want to become a general practitioner, maybe in a rural region in Switzerland, where doctors are missing, or one day in Indonesia.
