## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Tamale, in northern Ghana, is a city of dust in the dry season and floods in the rainy season. Every year the harmattan brings sand from the Sahara, and every year the rains come later and harder. My father, a regional agricultural officer, used to say that the north would be the first to feel the climate change, and the last to be helped.

My parents died in a road accident in 2016, when I was twenty-one. I left my studies to raise my three younger siblings. For years I also coached a youth football team in our neighbourhood, which kept many boys off the street, and helped organise tree-planting days with local schools.

When my youngest sister finished school, I finally had the chance to study again.

I want to work on climate adaptation for farming communities in northern Ghana, as my father did.
