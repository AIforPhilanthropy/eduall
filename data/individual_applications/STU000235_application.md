## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in a village in the Mürz valley, in Styria, after the last factory that still hired people without a trade certificate closed when I was six. My mother worked in a bakery, my father as a mechanic when there was work, which was not always. Neither of them studied beyond school.

I fixed radios at twelve and, at seventeen, helped the technician who serviced the equipment at our regional hospital, which is how I first saw what a broken ventilator does to a ward. After school I worked a year as an assistant technician there, then won an engineering scholarship and came to Bern.

Swiss German was my wall at first; standard German helped less than I expected, since lectures and daily life both run faster than I was used to. I am at B2 now.

In Bern I organise a repair café once a month, fixing small appliances people would otherwise throw away.

My goal is to design medical devices simple enough to be repaired outside a capital city, for hospitals like the one that gave me my first job.
