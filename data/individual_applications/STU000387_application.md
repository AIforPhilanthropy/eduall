## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Data science matters because the numbers that decide our lives are often invisible. When I was nine, my father lost his job in a Lausanne factory because a computer programme decided which workers were "less efficient". Nobody could explain the decision to him.

My parents came to Switzerland when I was two. They never went to school. My father worked in factories and my mother cleaned. In 2023, when my father became ill, they went back to our village in Punjab. I stayed, alone, to finish my studies.

I finished school early and started at EPFL at seventeen. It has been difficult. But I work in the library, tutor younger students, and on weekends I teach coding to children at a community centre in Renens, and help organise a Punjabi cultural association.

I want to work on fair and transparent algorithms.
