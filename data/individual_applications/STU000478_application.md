Parents: father stonecutter in a quarry, mother at home. No one studied. Father knew every stone by touch, not by name.

2025–2026: bad year. Landlord sold the building, we moved twice. Then my mother in Nablus had a stroke. I flew back twice. My grades fell.

Still here. 17 courses done. Seven semesters left.

Goal: engineering geology, slope stability. Quarries kill workers; my father's friend died in one.

Education: finally the names for the stones.
