## Section 4 — Financial Need Statement

*Instructions: Describe your financial situation and explain why you need EduAll support to pursue your studies in Switzerland. Address why paying for your education is difficult, which costs are most challenging for you, what would happen to your studies without support, and whether you have exhausted other funding options (300–400 words).*

My mother calls me every evening at nine. Sometimes the call drops because of an air-raid alert in Dnipro, and I wait, holding the phone, until she calls again. This is how my days in St. Gallen end.

I am in the second year of my Master's in Business Administration at HSG. My thesis is due in July 2028. My costs are CHF 2,502 a month: my studio costs CHF 1,010 plus CHF 137 utilities, tuition at HSG is CHF 522, and my health insurance, CHF 220.

My parents, both surgeons at the regional hospital in Dnipro, earn about CHF 3,140 together and have sent me CHF 720 every month since I arrived. The hospital where they work was hit twice this year. My father now works double shifts with wounded soldiers and civilians. Their salaries are paid in hryvnia, which lost a lot of value, and they have said they may need to support my grandparents, who lost their home near the front.

I would like to finish my studies without being one more weight on parents who are carrying so much.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

My mother is the bravest person I know. In March 2022, when everyone who could was leaving Dnipro, she stayed at the hospital. She operated for thirty hours without sleep during the first weeks. She told me to go, to study, to "build something for after".

I was twenty-two, in the final year of my bachelor's in economics. I finished it online, from shelters and trains, and after two years in a logistics company in Kyiv, organising humanitarian supply chains, I came to Switzerland in 2025.

German is still difficult for me. My master is in English, but half of the reading lists, all the administration and all the social life are in German.

My thesis is about how small Ukrainian companies kept operating during the war. I want to work in reconstruction finance, to help rebuild the businesses that kept my country alive.
