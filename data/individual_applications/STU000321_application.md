My parents did not go to university. They run a small printing shop. I finished secondary school early, and at eighteen I came to Zurich. German was very difficult; in the first year I understood maybe half of the lectures. I have improved every semester.

In Zurich I volunteer at a health information desk for African women, and on Saturdays I work in a pharmacy.
