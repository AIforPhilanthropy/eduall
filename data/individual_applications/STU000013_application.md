## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am 44 years old, I come from Kharkiv, and I am a nurse. I have been one for twenty-two years.

I trained at a medical college in Kharkiv. My parents worked in a tractor factory; nobody in my family had studied before. For the first ten years I worked on a cardiology ward. Then I became head nurse of the ward, responsible for 26 nurses. I introduced a structured discharge plan for heart failure patients, and our readmissions went down. I was very proud of this, but I did not have the scientific training to measure it properly or to publish it.

We came to Switzerland in 2021, when my spouse got a job offer in Basel. My Ukrainian nursing diploma was only partly recognised, so I decided to go back to university and do it properly. I did bridging courses, then the master's programme. I have completed 4 courses, with an average of 88.2.

My goal is to work as an advanced practice nurse in cardiology and to develop nurse-led programmes for chronic patients. In Ukraine, after the war, there will be enormous need for this kind of care, because many doctors have left. I want to be able to teach it.

Education for me is not a new start. It is the continuation of what I did for twenty years, with better tools.
