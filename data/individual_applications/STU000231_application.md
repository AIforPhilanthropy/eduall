## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Physics is important because it is the language of nature. In my island, Bohol, we have earthquakes and typhoons, and nobody explains to the people why they happen. In 2013, when I was eighteen, a strong earthquake destroyed our church and our house. The same year a typhoon came. For one year we lived in a tent.

My parents are farmers and never went to school. My sisters left to work abroad to support the family, and they paid for my studies. I studied physics in Cebu and worked seven years as a physics teacher in a public high school.

In 2026 I came to Zurich for my master. My thesis is about seismic waves. I want to go back to the Philippines and work in earthquake monitoring, so that people know more and can prepare.
