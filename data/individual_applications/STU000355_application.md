## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

On my first morning in Fribourg in 2022, I heard the church bells ring and instinctively looked for the nearest shelter. A woman at the bus stop saw my face, and without a word, she offered me her umbrella. I was nineteen, alone, and that small kindness is still with me.

I grew up in Lviv. My mother is a doctor and my stepfather an engineer; my father died when I was twelve. When the war began, my school class scattered across Europe. I came to Fribourg to finish my studies, and since then I have worked with children in a daycare centre and at a Ukrainian Saturday school, where children who had lost everything came to learn their own language.

My research is on how schools maintain education in wartime: underground classrooms, online teaching, and what children lose and gain.

I want to work in education reconstruction in Ukraine after the war.
