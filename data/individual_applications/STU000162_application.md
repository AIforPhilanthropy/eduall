## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Why does a river that fed my grandmother's village for a hundred years dry up in ten? I asked this question for the first time in 2009, when the White Volta near our village in the north of Ghana was almost dry in March. Our fields died. My parents, who are farmers and never went to school, sold our goats to buy food.

I was the first in my family to go to university. I studied agriculture in Tamale, and worked seven years for the Ministry of Food and Agriculture, first as extension officer and later as district coordinator, leading a team of twelve officers. We helped farmers to adapt: new seeds, small irrigation, soil conservation.

At twenty-nine I came to Fribourg to study environmental science, to understand better the science of water and climate. It is not easy to study again after seven years of work, and French is difficult, but I have a GPA of 3.44.

My thesis is about small dams and water storage in northern Ghana. After my master, I want to go back and work on water management for farmers.
