## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I grew up in Völklingen, in Saarland, in the shadow of the old ironworks, which is a museum now and was my grandfather's workplace before it was. My parents both lost their factory jobs in the same year, when I was nine, and neither found steady work again; my father does odd building jobs, my mother some cleaning.

During my bachelor's I worked two years part-time as a quality control assistant in a small chemical plant, one of the few still operating nearby. Lead, solvents, a lab with one working spectrometer that we all fought over.

In Zurich I help organise a support network for students from working-class and first-generation backgrounds at ETH, because I remember how alien the place felt in my first weeks.

My research is on detecting pesticide and heavy-metal residues in food and water with cheap sensors, the kind of tool that towns like mine, without money for proper labs, actually need.
