My financial need is small, and I want to say so first. My costs are CHF 2,071 a month. I earn CHF 941 as a teaching assistant for first-year physics, and my parents in Cusco, who run a small travel agency with a household income of around CHF 2,080, send me CHF 550. The monthly gap is CHF 580.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Cusco is a city of stone, built by people who understood the sky. As a child I visited Machu Picchu's Intihuatana, the stone that tracks the sun, and asked my father how the Incas knew where the sun would rise. He did not know; he only finished primary school. "Go and find out," he said.

I studied physics in Lima, the first in my family at university, and worked two years at an astronomical observatory in the Andes as a technical assistant. I also volunteered at the observatory's school outreach programme, bringing telescopes to rural schools, and in Basel I help organise the Latin American students' association.

My master's thesis is on the detection of exoplanets. I hope to continue to a PhD and later help build astronomy education in Peru, where the skies are among the clearest in the world.
