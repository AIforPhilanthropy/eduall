## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In November 2024 I found my name, almost. In the cantonal archives of Neuchâtel, in a register of 1890, there was a Bengali sailor, "Abdul R.", who worked on a ship to Marseille and came up to Neuchâtel to work in a watch factory. Nobody knows what happened to him. I sat in the reading room and I felt that history is not about kings, it is about people like him, and like me.

I am from Chittagong, the big port city of Bangladesh. My father is a dock worker and my mother sells vegetables. Nobody in my family went to university. My grandmother told me stories about the partition and the war of 1971, and I always wanted to know if they were true.

I came to Neuchâtel in 2023 with a scholarship, and started the bachelor in 2025 after one year of French. I passed 19 courses. French is now my language for history.

Since 2024 I have volunteered with a reading club for migrant children at the city library, and I help at the Bangladeshi community association with translation and events. At the museum I work as a guide, and I like best the school visits.

My thesis is about South Asian workers in Swiss watch industry before 1914. I want to continue with a master in migration history.

For me education means to find the people history forgot, and to give them their names back.
