## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Mosul's old city was nine hundred years old. There were houses with courtyards and carved wooden balconies, the al-Nuri mosque with its leaning minaret, narrow streets where neighbours knew each other for generations. I grew up there. In 2017, during the battle to retake the city, almost all of it was destroyed.

My parents were a baker and a housewife. Nobody in my family went to university before me. I studied architecture at the University of Mosul and worked ten years in an engineering office, designing schools and houses. When ISIS took the city in 2014, I fled with my family. After years in camps in Kurdistan, I came to Switzerland in 2019 and received provisional admission.

In Zurich, I started from nothing. I learned German, had my degree partly recognised, and entered the ETH master's programme in 2026. My GPA is 3.11, and I published one article on post-war reconstruction.

My thesis is about rebuilding Mosul's old city: how to rebuild houses for returning families, respecting the old structure but with modern safety and climate needs.

After my master's, I want to work on post-conflict reconstruction, in Iraq and other countries. Rebuilding a city is also rebuilding the memory of the people who live there.
