## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

When I was eight, in 2006, a flood submerged our neighbourhood in Jakarta, as it does most years. After the water went down, many children became sick with diarrhoea. My mother, a doctor, worked for days without sleeping. I went with her to the clinic and gave water to the children.

After my master in public health, I worked three years for the WHO country office in Jakarta, on dengue and flood-related diseases. Two articles came out of this work. I was also active in a community group that organised clean-water campaigns in poor neighbourhoods.

My doctorate is on how floods spread diseases in cities, using climate and health data.

I want to go back to Indonesia and help cities prepare for disease after floods.
