## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

I am thirty-two, a public health researcher from Ziguinchor, and the first person in my family to attend university. My parents are rice farmers in Casamance who never went to school.

For six years, I worked as a health programme officer for the regional health district, organising vaccination campaigns in remote villages. On weekends I volunteered with a local association that trained young women as community health relays, and I helped it grow from ten to over a hundred volunteers.

My doctoral research examines why vaccination coverage remains low in rural southern Senegal despite free vaccines: distance, trust, gender, and the legacy of conflict.

Writing and publishing in academic English is my biggest challenge. French is my language of study, but most of my field publishes in English, and I spend long evenings with my drafts.

After my doctorate, I want to return to Senegal and strengthen the research capacity of regional health services.
