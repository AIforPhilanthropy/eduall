I began the Master's in Computer Science at ETH Zurich in February 2026. My monthly expenses are CHF 2,200, of which CHF 1,097 is rent for a temporary room and CHF 243 is tuition. My younger brother, a clerk in Quetta (household income around CHF 320), sends me CHF 50, which is about a sixth of what he has.

A grant from EduAll would allow me to continue my studies while I secure stable housing and part-time work.

In 2023, after a series of attacks on members of the Hazara community in Quetta, including my cousin, my family was forced to leave our home. I lived in fear for two years.

At ETH I want to specialise in secure and resilient health information systems. My goal is to build technology that keeps working in fragile places, and to return to Pakistan to lead that work.
