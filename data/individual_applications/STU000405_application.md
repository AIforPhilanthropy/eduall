## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

In ten years, I want to lead a team building open-source tools for disaster response in South Asia.

In August 2018, when I was sixteen, the Kerala floods reached our street in Kochi. We spent three days on the roof of our school with two hundred people. I used my father's phone to post our location on a volunteer rescue map. A boat came. It was the first time I understood what software could do.

After the floods, I joined a volunteer group of students building flood-alert apps, and later worked two years as a software developer for a disaster-management start-up. I also helped organise coding workshops for children in fishing communities.

My parents are teachers, and they taught me that knowledge should serve people.
