A grant from EduAll would serve as proof of means for my permit, and would allow me to finish my law degree.

## Section 5 — Personal Statement

*Instructions: Tell us who you are and where you come from, describe your educational goals and aspirations, share any personal challenges or circumstances you wish to (optional), and explain what education means to you (max. 500 words).*

Tondo, Manila: one of the most crowded places on earth. Families of eight in one room, flooded streets every rainy season, and a police operation every few nights. When I was sixteen, my neighbour's son was shot during an anti-drug raid. He was nineteen. There was no investigation.

That night I decided to study law.

From the age of sixteen I volunteered for four years with a legal aid group in Tondo, helping families file complaints, find detained relatives and understand their rights. In my last year I coordinated the group's youth volunteers, twenty students, and organised trainings on rights during arrests.

In 2025 I came to Basel to study law. Studying in German is my biggest challenge. I read every text two or three times. But I have not failed a single course.

In Basel I lead a small group of Filipino students and workers who meet to discuss legal questions about work contracts and permits.

After my degree I want to specialise in human rights and migration law, and one day return to Manila to work for families like my neighbours.
